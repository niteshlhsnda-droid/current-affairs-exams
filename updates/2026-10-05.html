🎯 Top stories of the day

Diplomacy & Trade 🌍

  1. Swiss President's state visit begins today (5–7 Oct) — Swiss Confederation President Guy Parmelin arrives today on a three-day state visit at PM Modi's invitation — his first state visit to India and his second trip this year (he attended the AI Impact Summit in New Delhi in February 2026). He is accompanied by a high-level delegation of senior officials and business leaders. Bilateral talks with PM Modi today (5 Oct) will review cooperation in trade and investment, science, technology and innovation, mobility, culture and people-to-people contact, plus regional and global issues; Parmelin also meets President Droupadi Murmu (who hosts a banquet) and EAM S. Jaishankar. On 7 October he participates in the 2nd India–EFTA Prosperity Summit. The visit marks one year of the India–EFTA Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement (TEPA) — EFTA comprises Switzerland, Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein, and TEPA targets USD 100 billion of investment into India over 15 years. 🎓⭐
  2. India–Canada CEPA: 5th round opens in Ottawa today — chief negotiators begin the fifth round of the Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement talks in Ottawa, 5–9 October, aiming to conclude the pact by the end of this calendar year (the 4th round concluded in Mumbai on 18 September). The push matters because PM Modi is expected to visit Canada around December, and Canada's Trade Minister Maninder Sidhu will visit New Delhi later this month with a 300-strong business delegation; Sidhu and Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal last met at the G20 Trade Ministers' meeting in Milwaukee. The two sides share a target of USD 70 billion in bilateral trade by 2030 (from over USD 30 billion today). CEPA is a free-trade-style pact: steep cuts or elimination of duties on maximum traded goods, easier movement of skilled professionals and more investment. 🎓🏦⭐

Banking & Economy 🏦🎓

  1. RBI MPC meets from today; hike buzz ahead of the 7 Oct decision — the six-member Monetary Policy Committee meets 5–7 October, with Governor Sanjay Malhotra announcing the decision on 7 October. A businessline poll of 12 bank chief economists found 11 expecting a 25 bps hike to 5.50% — only Bank of Baroda's Madan Sabnavis expects a hold — and a majority see a cumulative 50–75 bps tightening by end-FY27 (six economists see the repo at 6%). A PTI poll of 16 economists/bankers also found a majority expecting a hike with a hawkish tone (L&T's Sachchidanand Shukla dissents, expecting status quo). SBI Research forecasts hikes in October and December; BofA now expects a cumulative 100 bps of hikes through H1 2027. Drivers: CPI at 4.82% in August (third straight month above the 4% target), crude above $100/barrel, a ~6% weaker rupee, and a strengthening El Niño/weak October rainfall threatening Rabi output — with strong growth giving policy space for a pre-emptive move. 🎓🏦⭐
  2. GST Council set to debate a big enforcement reset on 7 Oct — the GST Council is expected to consider removing tax officers' direct arrest power under Section 69 of the CGST Act; any arrest would instead need judicial authorisation. Other proposals in the package: raising the prosecution threshold from ₹1 crore to ₹5 crore; narrowing the scope of criminal proceedings so routine disputes over classification, valuation or ITC don't turn criminal; protecting genuine buyers' input tax credit; and easing GST registration for small e-commerce sellers. Intentional fraud would still be prosecuted (through courts, under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita), and tax recovery, interest and penalties continue. Nothing is decided yet — this is a proposal to watch — and legislative changes would need to come, potentially in the Winter Session of Parliament. 🏦🎓⭐

Governance & Policy 🎓

  1. Five Centres of Excellence for classical languages approved — the Ministry of Education's Department of Higher Education (Language Division, OM dated 1 October 2026) approved five Centres of Excellence for studies in Classical Assamese, Classical Bengali, Classical Marathi, Classical Pali and Classical Prakrit. The host institutions: Classical Assamese — Tezpur University (Assam); Classical Bengali — Visva-Bharati University (West Bengal); Classical Marathi — Deccan College Post-Graduate and Research Institute (Deemed University), Pune (Maharashtra); Classical Pali — Central University of Odisha; Classical Prakrit — Central University of Gujarat. The CoEs will anchor advanced research, documentation, preservation and digitisation of linguistic and literary resources, and wider dissemination of the heritage of these classical languages. 🎓⭐
  2. Rahul Gandhi's 'Satyagraha' platform goes live — Leader of the Opposition Rahul Gandhi launched the 'Satyagraha' digital platform on 3 October (a day after Mahatma Gandhi's 157th birth anniversary) to bring issue-based protests across India onto a single interactive map. Citizens can register their movement and grievance in a short online form; within nine hours, over 10,000 people and groups had signed up — students, farmers, Adivasis, Dalits, OBCs, women and Gen Z — with early entries spanning education and examinations, land rights, farmers' issues, corruption, environment and health. Congress pledges to read every submission and stand with each movement in Parliament, on the streets and through letters. At the 'Chhatron Ki Goonj' event in Pune, the Maharashtra Competitive Examination Coordination Committee registered its anti-paper-leak movement as 'satyagraha', demanding the MPSC chairman's resignation, an honest and qualified officer in his place, and immediate filling of 70,000+ vacant government posts. 🎓
  3. Congress state chiefs meet today over EC agitation — the Congress holds a meeting of all AICC in-charges and PCC chiefs in New Delhi today (5 Oct) to finalise its strategy for a planned agitation against the Election Commission of India over the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls; the party has sought action against CEC Gyanesh Kumar, while the EC has rejected the allegations. The INDIA bloc has announced a nationwide 'Save Democracy March' at district level from 2 to 8 October. 🎓

International & World 🌍

  1. Kautilya Conclave's final day; US carriers in focus — the 5th Kautilya Economic Conclave (theme "Resilience in an Age of Flux") concludes today in New Delhi after opening on 3 October; RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra warned that systemic risks are increasingly exogenous, cross-border and interconnected, with the next crisis potentially starting from a geopolitical event, cyberattack or tech failure. On the world front, the US has deployed its third aircraft carrier to the theatre as the conflict with Iran enters its eighth month — relevant context for India's oil-import and inflation watch, with the G7 already agreeing to release 100 million barrels from emergency reserves. 🎓🏦

🌆 Evening updates

Sports 🏅

  1. Asian Games 2026 conclude: India finishes 4th with 85 medals — the 20th Asian Games (Aichi-Nagoya 2026) closed on 4 October; India ended 4th overall with 85 medals (21 gold, 27 silver, 37 bronze) — its second-highest haul ever after Hangzhou 2023's record (106, later revised to 107). China topped the table with 169 gold and 341 medals. India's headline act was a golden sweep in team sports — both men's and women's sides won gold in cricket, hockey and kabaddi (men's cricket beat Pakistan by 19 runs in the final; men's hockey beat Malaysia 5-1, women's hockey beat China 1-0; both kabaddi teams beat Iran), and all six golds also sealed LA 2028 qualification quotas. Individual milestones: Kumkum Mohod became India's first individual recurve archery Asian Games champion (and booked an LA 2028 quota); Pranavi Urs won India's first individual golf gold; Lovlina Borgohain became only the second Indian woman boxer after Mary Kom to win Asian Games gold; Neeru Dhanda (double-gold shooter) was India's flag-bearer at the closing ceremony. 🎓🏭⭐
  2. Vaibhav Sooryavanshi named to TIME100 NEXT 2026 — the young cricketer, a breakout star at the Asian Games (youngest Indian Asian Games gold medallist, via men's cricket), is the only Indian cricketer on this year's TIME100 NEXT list of emerging global leaders. 🎓

Banking & Economy 🏦

  1. HDFC Bank names Anup Bagchi MD & CEO — first external appointee — HDFC Bank appointed Anup Bagchi as Managing Director & CEO for three years, after RBI approval, making him the bank's first external candidate to lead it. He takes charge on 27 October 2026, succeeding Sashidhar Jagdishan (whose term ends 26 October 2026). Bagchi earlier served as MD & CEO of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance and held senior roles at ICICI Bank and ICICI Securities. 🏦⭐
  2. Bank of Baroda becomes 2nd PSU lender to sponsor a pension fund under PFRDA — through its subsidiary BoB Pension Fund Management Company, Bank of Baroda received the Certificate of Appointment as Sponsor of a Pension Fund from the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) — presented to MD & CEO Debadatta Chand during NPS Divas 2026. SBI (April 2008) was the first PSB in this segment. 🏦⭐
  3. Finance Ministry projects 7.3% GDP growth for Q2 FY27 — the Ministry of Finance's Monthly Economic Review (September 2026) projected real GDP growth of 7.3% for Q2 FY27 (July–September 2026) on its nowcasting model, after 7.8% in Q1 FY27 — citing resilient consumption and investment, strong services and steady auto sales; exports touched nearly USD 400 billion in the first five months of FY27. 🏦🎓⭐

Policy & Economy 🎓

  1. Power Ministry notifies CAFE-III norms: ~17% fuel-efficiency gain by 2032 — the Ministry of Power notified the Corporate Average Fuel Economy (CAFE)-III norms for passenger vehicles (M1 category), applicable 1 April 2027 – 31 March 2032. Fuel consumption benchmarks tighten from 3.996 litres/100 km (2027–28) to 3.3273 litres/100 km (2031–32) — a nearly 16.7% improvement — with super credits for cleaner tech (BEVs/range-extended EVs 3×, plug-in hybrids 2.5×, strong hybrids 1.6×) and Carbon Neutrality Factors for alternative fuels (E20+ ethanol −8%, flex-fuel ethanol −22.3%, CNG/CBG −5%); recognised fuel-conservation technologies expand from 4 to 12. 🎓⭐
  2. RoDTEP and RoSCTL export schemes extended to 31 December 2026 — the Department of Commerce (Ministry of Commerce and Industry) and the Ministry of Textiles extended the RoDTEP (Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products) and RoSCTL (Rebate of State and Central Taxes and Levies) schemes to 31 December 2026, retaining existing rates — RoDTEP from 30 September, RoSCTL for apparel/garments/made-ups from 1 October — amid geopolitical disruptions in West Asia. 🎓🏦

Defence & Tech 🛡️

  1. Rajnath Singh unveils digital reforms at DAD's 279th Annual Day — Defence Minister Rajnath Singh launched three initiatives at the Defence Accounts Department's 279th Annual Day (DRDO Bhawan, New Delhi): SAKSHAM (DAD's bill-audit and payment system) integrated with the Indian Army Financial Information System (IAFIS); the Treasury Single Account (TSA) operationalised via the PRISM platform integrated with RBI's eKUBER; and a revamped National Compilation System (NCS) for unified defence-expenditure accounting. 🎓🏭
  2. FWDA partners Portugal's Keydef to build AI-based UAVs — Indian defence deep-tech startup Flying Wedge Defence & Aerospace (FWDA) tied up with Portugal's Keydef to manufacture the Kaal Bhairava autonomous UAV and the YAMA swarm-interceptor drone in Portugal (initial €6 million investment), pursuing NATO certification for access to alliance markets. 🎓
  3. INS Sahyadri and INS Kulish complete AIME 2026 — the two Indian naval ships concluded participation in the ASEAN–India Maritime Exercise (AIME) 2026 in the Philippines (theme "Anchored in Trust, United at Sea"), taking part in HADR, Visit-Board-Search-Seizure and underway-replenishment drills — underscoring the Act East Policy and MAHASAGAR vision. 🎓

Science, Education & Environment 🔬

  1. SpaceX Transporter-18 carries 130 payloads, including Google's AI-chip prototype — SpaceX launched 130 payloads aboard a Falcon 9 from Vandenberg, California, on its Transporter-18 rideshare mission — including Google's Project Suncatcher prototype testing Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) for AI workloads in orbit, a step toward an orbital data centre. 🎓
  2. THE World University Rankings 2027: IISc Bengaluru India's best at 254 — Times Higher Education released the THE World University Rankings 2027: the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru (rank 254) is India's highest-ranked institution; University of Oxford retained the top spot for the 11th consecutive year, followed by MIT; India had 143 universities represented, second only to the US (168). 🎓
  3. India reaffirms whale-conservation pledge at IWC-70 in Hobart — India reiterated its commitment to cetacean conservation at the 70th meeting of the International Whaling Commission (IWC-70) in Hobart, Australia (28 September – 2 October), which coincided with the 80th anniversary of the International Convention for the Regulation of Whaling and adopted the Hobart Declaration by consensus; Dr Urbain Brito of Benin was elected Chair. India joined the IWC in 1981. 🎓

Governance & Digital 🎓

  1. Department of Pharmaceuticals launches New Discovery Track under PRIP — the Department of Pharmaceuticals announced a New Discovery Track under the Promotion of Research and Innovation in Pharma and MedTech (PRIP) Scheme — financial assistance of up to ₹50 crore per project (with 25% mandatory co-funding) for startups and MSMEs doing early-stage drug discovery, moving projects from TRL 1–3 to TRL 6. PRIP was launched on 17 August 2023 with a ₹5,000 crore outlay. 🎓🏭
  2. CAG launches 'CAG-BHASHINI Anuvaad' for AI-based translation — the Digital India BHASHINI Division with the Office of the Comptroller and Auditor General of India launched 'CAG-BHASHINI Anuvaad', an AI-based translation tool for official documents and audit reports, during the closing ceremony of the 74th Hindi Diwas / Hindi Pakhwada 2026. 🎓
  3. Amended BharatNet Programme for Maharashtra: ₹10,520 crore — Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN), under the Department of Telecommunications, signed an agreement with the Maharashtra government, MahaNet Digital Infrastructure Ltd, BSNL and MahaIT to implement the Amended BharatNet Programme in Maharashtra under the state-led model, with ₹10,520 crore of central support — covering 28,237 Gram Panchayats. 🎓🏭
