## 🎯 Top stories of the day

### Banking & Finance 🏦
1. **RBI's bond sales & FX intervention halve liquidity surplus** — surplus fell to ₹4.92 trillion on 21 Sept (down 55% from record ₹11.16 trillion); RBI sold ₹750 bn bonds in a week, ₹250 bn more planned; diaspora deposit scheme had raised $133 bn; ANZ expects three 25bp hikes from Oct 2026, repo to 6.00% by end-FY27. 🏦🎓⭐
2. **RBI extends simplified KYC certification for FPIs at co-operative banks** — effective 18 Sept 2026; FPIs at Urban/Rural Co-operative Banks put on par with NRIs/PIOs; no physical in-person verification required under PMLA norms. 🏦
3. **RBI bulk-deposit rate disclosure rules from 1 Oct 2026** — banks must publish rates on their websites; 10-minute grace after the 10 am daily deadline. 🏦
4. **SEBI pushes banks into commodity derivatives; RBI/IRDAI hold back** — SEBI Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey bats for bank/pension-fund entry to deepen liquidity and hedging; MCX shares fell 3.4% on the commentary. 🏦

### Defence 🛡️
5. **Tarang Shakti 2026: F-35s to fly with IAF for the first time** — 2nd edition at Jodhpur, 26 Sept–12 Oct 2026; 7 friendly foreign air forces with assets (US F-35s, French Rafales, UAE F-16s; Germany, Australia, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka), 32 nations as observers; announced by Vice Chief of Air Staff Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit on 22 Sept. ⭐

### International 🌍
6. **First-ever V4+India Foreign Ministers' Meeting** — held 21 Sept in New York on the sidelines of the 81st UNGA; Jaishankar met ministers of Czechia, Hungary, Poland & Slovakia; annual ministerial meet + annual business forum agreed; V4 backed India's UNSC permanent-seat bid. 🎓⭐
7. **Ireland fines Google €403 million over location-data privacy** — DPC's GDPR action announced 21 Sept over Web & App Activity, Location History and Location Accuracy (2018–20); Google must comply within 6 months; 4th-largest fine by the Irish regulator. 🏦🎓

### Awards & Culture 🏆

### Sports 🏆
9. **India's first gold at Asian Games 2026** — women's cricket beat Sri Lanka by 147 runs in the T20 final (216/3 vs 69) at Korogi Sports Park, Nisshin, on 22 Sept; Mandhana 79 off 45 — became the first batter in women's cricket to 11,000 international runs; India's tally: 1 gold, 4 silver, 2 bronze. ⭐
10. **Suchika Tariyal wins bronze in MMA** — India's first-ever Asian Games MMA medal, women's traditional 60kg (21 Sept); lost to Singapore's Teo Hui Hoon in the semifinal. ⭐
11. **Men's 10m air rifle team silver** — Rudrankksh Patil, Parth Mane & Himanshu Dhillon (Asian Games, 21 Sept).
