📰 Current Affairs — 24 September 2026

🎯 Top stories of the day

Defence 🛡️

  1. MoD signs ₹810.79 crore SAT-SAAW deal with BDL — contract signed 23 Sept 2026 with Bharat Dynamics Ltd for 160 Satellite Smart Anti-Airfield Weapons (SAT-SAAW) for the IAF under Buy Indian (IDDM), in the presence of Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh; DRDO-designed precision glide bomb with 60% indigenous content; will arm Jaguar, Hawk and Su-30MKI aircraft; deliveries from 2027-28 to 2028-29. 🎓🏭⭐
  2. DRDO's 30kW laser anti-drone weapon moves toward production — DRDO is taking its indigenous 30kW-class truck-mounted laser weapon toward production and will seek an industry partner; it engages drone swarms and UAVs up to 5 km; part of Project Sudarshan Chakra, with 50kW and 100kW classes in the works. 🎓🏭
  3. Exercise Yudh Abhyas 2026 (22nd edition) — India–US troops held joint artillery drills at Mahajan Field Firing Range, Rajasthan, on 23 Sept with M777 ultra-light howitzers, Dhanush guns, Pinaka MBRLs, L70 air-defence guns and M-LIDS, focused on interoperability of fire planning and coordination; the exercise is being held across Rajasthan and Uttarakhand. 🎓
  4. Tata's defence plant inaugurated in Berrechid, Morocco — Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and Morocco's Defence Minister Abdelatif Loudyi inaugurated TASL's 20,000 sq-m facility on 23 Sept, which will build 150 WhAP 8x8 armoured vehicles for the Royal Moroccan Army over three years — the biggest contract for Indian-made armoured vehicles. 🎓⭐

Banking & Finance 🏦

  1. SEBI Board meets 24 Sept with major reforms on the agenda — overhaul of PMS regulations including a new mutual-fund-only PMS (minimum investment ~₹25 lakh vs ₹50 lakh; minimum net worth ~₹2 crore vs ₹5 crore), a common advertisement code for regulated entities, settlement regulation changes, eased FPI norms for non-agri commodity derivatives, corporate debt reforms (Credit Risk-o-Meter for debt securities), depository receipts for REITs/InvITs, and vault-manager rules. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. SEBI's net-settlement proposal: comments close 24 Sept — SEBI's 3 Sept consultation paper proposes net settlement of funds (with gross securities settlement) for mutual-fund cash-market trades, only at individual scheme level; public comments invited till 24 Sept 2026. 🏦
  3. Motilal Oswal gets SEBI nod for custodial services — Motilal Oswal Custodial Services received SEBI approval on 24 Sept to offer custodial services (safekeeping, trade settlement, corporate actions, regulatory reporting) to institutional clients including AIFs, portfolio managers, mutual funds, insurers and pension funds; operations begin Q4 2026. 🏦
  4. 3-day nationwide bank strike from 28 Sept 2026 — United Forum of Bank Unions (UFBU, seven unions covering ~90% of the banking workforce) called a strike for 28–30 Sept; SBI advised customers to finish branch work by 25 Sept, while ATMs, YONO, internet/mobile banking and UPI stay functional; demands include a 5-day banking week, pension updation, a uniform DA formula and an NPS-to-OPS switch option; a continuous strike from 26 Oct was also announced. 🏦🎓🏭⭐
  5. PIB Fact Check busts fake UPI/ATM rumours — on 23 Sept the Finance Ministry clarified: UPI P2P stays free, merchant payments up to ₹2,000 stay free, and a 0.4% MDR applies only on specified person-to-merchant transactions above ₹2,000, capped at ₹300 for transactions of ₹75,000 and above; ~96% of merchant transactions remain unaffected. 🏦🎓
  6. EPFO wage ceiling raised to ₹25,000 — Cabinet approval of 16 Sept, effective 17 Sept 2026 (Vishwakarma Jayanti): mandatory EPF coverage ceiling raised from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month, the first revision in ~12 years (since Sept 2014); ~51 lakh additional employees expected under coverage; annual government outgo ~₹11,339 crore. 🏦🎓🏭
  7. RBI rate-hike buzz returns — repo has stayed at 5.25% since August (fourth straight pause); MPC next meets 5–7 Oct; S&P expects one 25-bps hike to 5.5% in FY27, Fitch expects 5.5% in October and then 5.75% in early 2027, and SBI Research also recommends two hikes. 🏦🎓

International 🌍

  1. India co-sponsors inaugural P4M Summit at the UN — on 21 Sept in New York, EAM S. Jaishankar co-chaired the first-ever 'Partners for Multilateralism, International Law, Peace & Prosperity (P4M)' Summit with leaders of Australia, Barbados, Brazil, Canada and Kenya; he stressed transparent decision-making and action on the '4F' crisis of fuel, food, fertiliser and finance. 🎓

Awards & Culture 🏆

  1. WION Iconic Tourism Summit & Awards 2026 — held 23 Sept at ITC Maurya, New Delhi, presented by VFS Global in association with Red Hat Communications; sessions on the future of global mobility, biometric travel and AI in tourism, concluding with the Iconic Awards 2026 for travel and tourism contributors. 🎓

Sports 🏆

  1. Roshibina Devi wins wushu silver — on 24 Sept, Naorem Roshibina Devi lost the women's 60kg sanda final 0-2 to China's Zhang Xiaoyu and took silver — she is the first Indian to win medals at three successive Asian Games (bronze 2018, silver at Hangzhou 2022, silver 2026); India's tally now stands at 14 (1 gold, 6 silver, 7 bronze). 🎓⭐
  2. Satnam Singh & Salman Khan row to bronze — on 24 Sept the Indian pair won bronze in men's double sculls (6:13.25), finishing behind China and Uzbekistan. 🎓
  3. Five medals on Day 4 (23 Sept) — Mirabai Chanu won silver in women's 49kg weightlifting (total 198 kg: 87 snatch + 111 clean & jerk), ending India's 28-year wait since Karnam Malleswari's 1998 Bangkok silver; Jay Meena won bronze in men's singles soft tennis — India's first-ever Asian Games medal in soft tennis; bronzes also came in men's and women's skeet team events and the men's badminton team (lost 1-3 to China in the semifinal). 🎓⭐
  4. Elena Rybakina wins US Open women's singles — on 12 Sept, Elena Rybakina (Kazakhstan) beat Aryna Sabalenka 6-4, 5-7, 6-2 in the final, denying her a three-peat; Rybakina's third Grand Slam title (Australian Open 2026, Wimbledon 2022). 🎓

Important Days 📅

  1. World Maritime Day — 24 September — UN/IMO observance held on the last Thursday of September; 2026 theme "From Policy to Practice: Powering Maritime Excellence" — the first two-year theme (2026–27), endorsed by the IMO Council at its 134th session (7–11 July); focuses on implementing maritime regulations through technical assistance and training. 🎓⭐

Schemes & Governance 🏛️

  1. Ayushman Bharat turns 8 — on 23 Sept PM Modi said 60+ crore beneficiaries now have ₹5 lakh annual health cover, making it the world's largest healthcare scheme; 48.54 crore Ayushman cards created, 13+ crore availed treatment, ₹2+ lakh crore of care through 38,000+ empanelled hospitals; Ayushman Arogya Mandirs recorded 500+ crore visits. 🎓⭐
  2. 20th Rozgar Mela — on 19 Sept PM Modi distributed 51,000+ appointment letters at 45 locations (~12.75 lakh total through Rozgar Melas); he highlighted the new Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (~₹1 lakh crore outlay, ~2 crore first-time youth, ₹15,000 incentive to first-time job seekers plus incentives to employers). 🎓🏭
