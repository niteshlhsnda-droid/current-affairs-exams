📰 Current Affairs — 29 September 2026

*Daily exam-ready digest for Bank • UPSC • PSU aspirants, compiled from

🎯 Top stories of the day

Economy & Banking 🏦🎓

  1. India's first blue bond pulled on launch day — Sagarmala Finance Corporation withdrew the country's maiden blue bond on 28 Sept 2026 after receiving coupon bids higher than the expected below-8% level, Managing Director L.V.S. Sudhakar Babu confirmed. The up-to-₹600 crore 10-year issue (₹100 crore base + ₹500 crore greenshoe, rated AA+ by ICRA/CARE, arranged by SBI Capital Markets) had been scheduled for 28 Sept — covered in yesterday's edition. Rising borrowing costs bit elsewhere too: SIDBI cancelled a ₹60-billion note sale (bids 7.6–8.1%) and Power Grid accepted only ₹20 billion of a planned ₹50 billion raise. Borrowing costs are rising on expectations the RBI will tighten later this year to contain oil-price-driven inflation. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. Supreme Court refuses to stay 0.4% MDR on UPI above ₹2,000 — the Supreme Court declined to stay the Centre's framework under which a 0.4% Merchant Discount Rate will apply to person-to-merchant UPI payments above ₹2,000 from 15 October 2026. UPI has historically been MDR-free; the move reopens the zero-MDR debate (MDR was withdrawn in 2020) — a hot Bank-prelims current-affairs item on digital payments. 🏦🎓⭐
  3. UFBU strike deferred: no 28–30 Sept bank strike — the nationwide three-day strike called by the United Forum of Bank Unions was deferred after late-night talks on 27 Sept between the UFBU and the Indian Banks' Association (IBA) (update to yesterday's edition). The IBA agreed to a joint high-level committee to examine the long-pending demand for a five-day banking week and other issues including the performance-linked incentive scheme; banks are operating normally. 🏦🎓
  4. Fitch raises India's FY27 growth forecast to 6.9% — Fitch Ratings lifted its FY2026-27 GDP projection to 6.9% from 6.4% (stronger-than-expected 7.8% June-quarter growth; S&P and Moody's are at 7%). It still expects momentum to moderate and the RBI to hike the policy rate 25 bps in October to 5.5%, with a further rise to 5.75% in early 2027 — broadly matching HSBC's two-hike call carried yesterday. Fitch expects investment to rise over 10% and non-food credit growth touched 19% YoY in July. 🏦🎓⭐

Infrastructure & Industry 🏭

  1. NHSRCL launches first 100-metre steel bridge for the bullet train — the National High Speed Rail Corporation launched the first 100-metre-long steel bridge for the Mumbai–Ahmedabad High Speed Rail corridor in Maharashtra (reported 28–29 Sept). The bridge weighs around 1,405 tonnes — a flagship milestone for the MAHSR project, whose Japanese Shinkansen-based trains are targeted at 320 km/h. 🏭🎓⭐

Governance & Education 🏛️

  1. Nilekani task force on exam reforms consults K. Radhakrishnan — the Nandan Nilekani-led task force on exam reforms consulted former ISRO chairman K. Radhakrishnan, who headed the committee constituted by the Centre after the 2024 NEET-UG paper leak (reported 29 Sept). The consultation signals the task force is building on the Radhakrishnan committee's recommendations — exam-integrity architecture is a recurring GS-2/Polity-reform theme. 🎓⭐

Foreign Policy & Diplomacy 🌍

  1. Foreign Secretary Misri meets US Congressional delegation — Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri met a bipartisan US Congressional delegation led by Representative Brian Mast (reported 29 Sept), discussing the Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act, India-US ties, energy security, counter-terrorism and freedom of navigation. Watch for GS-2 spillover: India's oil-buying latitude under sanctions scrutiny. 🎓

Defence & Science 🛡️🔬

  1. Yudh Abhyas 2026: India-US precision-strike exercise at Mahajan — on 26 Sept, the Indian and US Armies conducted a precision-strike exercise using the Indian-made Sky-Striker UAV at the Mahajan Field Firing Range, Rajasthan, as part of the 22nd edition of Yudh Abhyas (reported 27–29 Sept). This year's exercise focused on joint combat capabilities in mountainous and semi-mountainous terrain, showcasing indigenous drones and unmanned systems. 🎓⭐
  2. Project 15B: the Indian Navy's newest stealth destroyer, INS Surat — today's roundups recap INS Surat, the fourth and final Visakhapatnam-class destroyer under Project 15B (commissioned January 2025 by PM Modi; designed by the Navy's Warship Design Bureau, built by Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders). Exam-ready facts: 75% indigenous content, India's first AI-enabled warship, 7,400-tonne displacement, BrahMos + Barak-8 armament; built after INS Visakhapatnam, Mormugao and Imphal. 🛡️🎓⭐
  3. India–Vietnam bilateral exercise (VINBAX) in focus — the 29 Sept roundup flags the India–Vietnam bilateral military exercise on counter-terrorism and jungle warfare under UN mandates (FAQ answer confirms the partner nation as Vietnam). VINBAX is the only annual field UN-peacekeeping exercise India conducts with Vietnam — a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership anchor under the Act East policy. 🛡️🎓

Agriculture 🌾

  1. ICAR × NDDB pact for indigenous cattle genomics — the Indian Council of Agricultural Research signed a strategic pact with the National Dairy Development Board to scale up genomic selection in indigenous cattle breeds, accelerating genetic advancement and milk productivity (reported in the 29 Sept roundup). Note the pairing: ICAR (research) with NDDB (the Anand-headquartered dairy cooperative body under the Ministry of Cooperation). 🎓

Sports 🏆

  1. Asian Games 2026: Sable finishes 4th; Miura breaks his Games record — Avinash Sable missed the steeplechase medal, finishing 4th in 8:21.67 (season best) in the men's 3000m steeplechase final on 28 Sept; Japan's Ryuji Miura pulled clear to win and broke the Asian Games record Sable had set at the previous edition. 🎓
  2. Boxing: Kapil Pokhariya assures India a medal — Kapil Pokhariya beat Korea's Gichae Kim 5-0 in the men's 90kg quarter-final on 28 Sept, reaching the semi-final and guaranteeing an Asian Games boxing medal. 🎓
  3. Day 11 today (29 Sept): semi-finals on cards — after the 28 Sept rain-out, India meet the Sri Lanka–Nepal quarter-final winner in the men's cricket semi-final today; check today's evening games for the final tally. India's tally stood at 39 (4G/17S/18B), 12th, after Day 10 — see the 28 Sept evening edition for the full Day 10 athletics haul. 🎓

The morning edition's "semi-final today" line is corrected — five straight men's games have now been rain-affected. 🎓

Defence & Security 🛡️🎓

  1. KAZIND-2026: 60-member Indian contingent departs for Kazakhstan — India joined Kazakhstan for the 9th edition of Exercise KAZIND (Insights IAS, dailygkupdate); the contingent is at Oskemen, Kazakhstan, and the drill focuses on joint counter-terrorism operations in a sub-conventional environment under a UN mandate (semi-urban and mountainous terrain). The morning edition carried Yudh Abhyas (US) and VINBAX (Vietnam); KAZIND completes today's joint-exercise set. 🎓⭐
  2. Theatre-commands operationalisation conference — single-source report — freejobalert's 29 Sept roundup reports that CDS General Anil Chauhan chaired the inaugural Joint Services Commanders' Conference on 'Theatre Commands Operationalisation 2026-27' at Integrated Defence Staff HQ, New Delhi, on 28–29 Sept, finalising a staggered rollout of India's three integrated theatre commands — Northern (Lucknow), Western (Jaipur), Maritime (Thiruvananthapuram) — between January and December 2027. Not yet corroborated by a second source — carried with this caveat; context: the CDS had earlier submitted the final theatre-commands report to the Defence Ministry (reported at the Kalam & Kavach 3.0 dialogue). 🎓

Appointments & Infrastructure 🏛️

  1. IRDAI gets two whole-time members — Dinesh Pant (former Managing Director of LIC, voluntary retirement effective 24 Sept 2026) assumed charge as Whole-Time Member (Actuary); Girija Subramanian (former CMD of New India Assurance, retired July 2026) assumed charge as Whole-Time Member (Distribution) of the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India. Static GK: IRDAI established 1999, HQ Hyderabad, chair Ajay Seth. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. Akhil Gupta appointed Chairperson of Coforge — effective 29 September 2026 for a five-year period, as Non-Executive Independent Director and Chairperson; Gupta is the former Vice Chairman of Bharti Enterprises with four decades of experience. 🏦🎓
  3. ₹122 crore approved for Barbil–Bolanikhadan rail doubling — Indian Railways approved doubling a 6-km stretch between Barbil and Bolanikhadan (South Eastern Railway) for iron-ore movement under the Energy, Mineral and Cement Corridor; the single line carries ~9 MTPA (~6 loaded iron-ore rakes/day) today, with an additional 8.9 MTPA expected after doubling. 🏭🎓⭐
  4. GATE 2027 registration deadline extended — IIT Madras (the organising institute) extended registration without late fee until 5 October 2026; GATE is organised jointly by the IITs and IISc. 🎓

Update to a morning story 🏦

  1. UPI-MDR: more framework details surface — a bench led by Chief Justice of India Surya Kant (with Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohana) declined the stay and sought responses from the Centre, RBI and NPCI; the Finance Ministry says ~96% of merchant transactions will remain unaffected, P2P payments stay free, the 0.4% MDR is capped at ₹300 for transactions of ₹75,000 and above, and essential thin-margin sectors (railways, telecom, insurance, fuel, agricultural inputs) get a flat ₹5 MDR on qualifying transactions above ₹2,000 — from 15 October 2026. 🏦🎓⭐
