📰 Current Affairs — 1 October 2026

*Daily exam-ready digest for Bank • UPSC • PSU aspirants, compiled from

GKToday, Insights IAS, Drishti IAS, Testbook & AffairsCloud.*

Tags: 🏦 Bank • 🎓 UPSC • 🏭 PSU • ⭐ high-yield

🎯 Top stories of the day

Banking & Markets 🏦🎓

  1. Windfall tax cut on diesel and ATF exports from 1 Oct — the Finance Ministry cut the Special Additional Excise Duty on diesel exports to ₹16/litre (from ₹20) and on ATF to ₹10.5/litre (from ₹15) for the fortnight beginning 1 October 2026; petrol export duty stays at ₹0.50/litre. The export levies were introduced on 27 March to protect domestic availability amid West Asia tensions and are revised fortnightly; no change in domestic excise duties. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. RoDTEP and RoSCTL export schemes extended till 31 Dec 2026 — the government extended the Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products (RoDTEP) at existing rates and value caps for DTA, advance authorisation, SEZ and EOU exports, and extended RoSCTL for apparel, garments and made-ups at existing rates. Key support for textile and manufacturing exporters. 🏦🎓⭐
  3. India's external debt $778.2 billion in June quarter (RBI) — external debt rose to USD 778.2 billion at end-June 2026; loans 34.3% (largest component), currency & deposits 22.2%, trade credit & advances 19.1%, debt securities 16.5%. Net international liabilities rose to $220.3 billion. Separately, the Centre's fiscal deficit reached 41.9% of the full-year target by August (~₹7.10 lakh crore); major subsidy spend rose to ₹1.87 lakh crore from ₹1.50 lakh crore a year earlier. 🏦🎓⭐
  4. Apple Pay launches in India with Axis Bank — Apple rolled out its flagship payments service in India after years of global rollout, with Axis Bank as the first partner bank. Watch for UPI-interoperability and tokenisation norms — a ready-made fintech exam question. 🏦🎓

Governance & Policy 🎓

  1. CCEA hikes MSP for all mandated Rabi crops (Marketing Season 2027-28) — the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approved an increase in Minimum Support Prices for all mandated Rabi crops for RMS 2027-28. The Cabinet also approved three decisions with a combined outlay of ₹2.79 lakh crore, including a ₹1.86 lakh crore electricity grid upgrade scheme with PM DHARA in focus, amid a CEA study on grid stability triggered by the data-centre surge. 🎓⭐
  2. PM Modi at UPSC centenary celebrations — the Prime Minister participated in the centenary celebrations of the Union Public Service Commission at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi on 1 October and addressed the gathering. The UPSC, India's central recruiting agency under Article 315, was established in 1926. 🎓⭐
  3. FASTag Annual Pass crosses 1 crore — NHAI's ₹3,075 Annual Pass (one-year validity or 200 toll-plaza crossings at ~1,150 plazas) has crossed one crore passes issued since its August 2025 launch. 🎓
  4. UIDAI launches five initiatives on Aadhaar Day 2026 — at Aadhaar Samvaad, Kolkata, UIDAI launched Face Authentication for ePDS, SWIK Portal 2.0 and a Sub-AUA/Sub-KUA framework among five new initiatives. 🎓
  5. NHAI orders quality inspections of 17 highway projects — special inspections ordered for 17 under-construction National Highway projects where the winning bid was more than 15% lower than the estimated project cost. 🎓
  6. ₹1,200+ crore under PM-RKVY to 10 states; Khet Bachao Abhiyan — the Centre sanctioned over ₹1,200 crore under PM-RKVY to 10 states and announced Khet Bachao Abhiyan (22 Oct–30 Nov) focused on soil health and balanced fertiliser use. 🎓
  7. ₹1,600 crore for 41 Pharma & MedTech innovation projects — the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers approved ₹1,600 crore for 41 projects under the first round of PRIP (Promotion of Research and Innovation in Pharma MedTech Sector). 🎓
  8. Tewolde Gebremariam gets security clearance to head Air India — the incoming CEO and Managing Director received government security clearance and is expected to formally take charge soon. 🎓
  9. ECI removes SIR declaration from Form 6 in finalised states — the Election Commission dropped the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) declaration it had added to the statutory voter-enrolment Form 6, in states where SIR is over — two Election Commissioners had called the added declaration 'illegal'. 🎓
  10. Delhi records lowest September average AQI in five years — the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) reported Delhi's lowest September monthly-average AQI for 2022–2026. 🎓

Defence & Security 🛡️🎓

  1. Operation Korag: LeT terrorist 'Moosa' killed in Budgam — a Pakistan-based Lashkar-e-Taiba militant, Mohammad Asif alias Hashim Moosa, was neutralised in an overnight encounter with security forces in Budgam, Jammu & Kashmir. 🛡️🎓⭐
  2. Coast Guard seizes ₹3,000 crore drugs from Iranian boat — the Indian Coast Guard intercepted an Iranian dhow in the Arabian Sea carrying 526 kg of drugs worth ₹3,000 crore; 5 Pakistani nationals detained. 🎓⭐
  3. Kaveri engine clears key step in Russia, set for Ghatak UCAV — India's indigenous Kaveri fighter-jet engine cleared a key testing step in Russia and is ready for integration with the stealth UCAV Ghatak — a major boost for the indigenous aero-engine programme. 🛡️🎓⭐
  4. MEA warns Indians against joining Russian armed forces — the Ministry of External Affairs issued an advisory cautioning Indian nationals against enlisting in the Russian military. 🎓

Foreign Policy & World 🌍

  1. Modi–Trump call reviews bilateral ties — the Prime Minister spoke with US President Donald Trump to review India–US bilateral ties (per US Ambassador Sergio Gor). Meanwhile, US forces withdrew all troops from Iraq, ending the 12-year mission against the Islamic State; the last troops left a northern Iraq airbase under the Baghdad–Washington deal. 🎓⭐
  2. Iran warns UAE of 'highly dangerous consequences' — Iran's Foreign Ministry warned the UAE after Israeli PM Netanyahu's visit; a senior Saudi official denied reports of any meeting with Israel. 🎓

Environment & Monsoon 🌿

  1. India received 759 mm rainfall this monsoon (IMD) — the India Meteorological Department reported 759 mm of rainfall for the 2026 monsoon season. 🎓

Science, Education & Rankings 🔬🎓

  1. IISc Bengaluru tops Indian institutions in THE World University Rankings 2027 — the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru emerged as the highest-ranked Indian institution in the Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings 2027. 🎓⭐
  2. Bharat Life Sciences Award Innovation Summit 2026 — the Innovation Summit of the Bharat Life Sciences Award (BLSA) 2026, organised by FICCI and powered by Eli Lilly (India), was held on 1 October at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi — 50+ policymakers, 100+ industry CEOs and 1,200+ delegates, aligned with Viksit Bharat 2047, with MoS Science & Technology Jitendra Singh attending. 🎓
  3. IRIS 2026: 3rd Faculty Research Awards — at the Careers360 IRIS 2026 summit on 1 October, 54 researchers across 27 subject domains were recognised with the Faculty Research Awards, presented by Dr. D. Purandeswari, MP and Chairperson of the Joint Parliamentary Committee on the Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan Bill. 🎓

Sports 🏆

  1. Asian Games 2026 Day 12: India 8th with 60 medals (10G/21S/29B) — a golden day fetched four golds: a compound archery clean sweep — women's team (Jyothi Surekha Vennam, Chikitha Taniparthi, Prithika Pradeep) beat China 238-234, equalling the world record; men's team gold; mixed team gold (Chikitha & Sahil Jadhav edged Korea 158-157 in a shoot-off); and Neeru Dhanda & Kynan Chenai won mixed trap shooting gold. Bronzes: boxers Priya Ghanghas (women's 60kg) and Kapil Pokhariya (men's 90kg), wrestler Sunil Kumar (Greco-Roman 87kg). India climbed to 8th; China lead with 275 medals (150 gold). Day 13 today (1 Oct): men's cricket semifinal vs Sri Lanka, women's hockey semifinal, more archery and shooting medal chances. 🏆🎓⭐
  2. India chase 406 vs West Indies — highest successful ODI chase — India chased 406 in the 2nd ODI at Guwahati (30 Sept), winning by 8 wickets to seal the three-match series 2-0 — their highest successful chase in ODI cricket. Shubman Gill hit his second ODI double century. 🏆🎓⭐

🌙 Evening updates

National Security 🛡️🎓

  1. AFSPA extended 6 months in Manipur, Nagaland and Arunachal Pradesh — the Ministry of Home Affairs extended the Armed Forces (Special Powers) Act, 1958 in notified "disturbed areas" of the three states from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027 after reviewing the law-and-order situation. In Manipur, the whole state stays disturbed except areas under 13 police stations; in Nagaland, 9 districts plus 21 police-station areas; in Arunachal Pradesh, Tirap, Changlang and Longding districts plus three police-station areas in Namsai district. 🎓🛡️⭐
  2. BKI operative Sonu Barnala deported from UAE to India — Harwinder Kumar alias Sonu Barnala, wanted in the murder of VHP leader Vikas Prabhakar in Nangal, Punjab, by Babbar Khalsa International operatives at the behest of Pakistan-based chief Wadhawa Singh, was handed over by UAE authorities after a Red Corner Notice (17 April 2026). The MHA cited its PRAHAAR initiative for expediting terrorist deportations. 🛡️🎓

Banking & Markets 🏦🎓

  1. RBI approves Anup Kumar Saha as Kotak Mahindra Bank MD & CEO — under Section 35B of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, the RBI approved Anup Kumar Saha's appointment for a three-year term from 1 January 2027, succeeding Ashok Vaswani (subject to shareholder approval). Saha is currently the bank's whole-time director; he was earlier MD & CEO of Bajaj Finance (April 2025–January 2026) and spent 14 years at ICICI Bank. 🏦🎓⭐

Environment & Agriculture 🌿🎓

  1. India's first PRAMAAN forest certificates handed over — Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav handed over the first PRAMAAN certificates (Programme for Recognition and Accreditation of Sustainable Management Practices for Agroforestry and Natural Forestry Resources) under the Indian Forest and Wood Certification Scheme (IFWCS, launched December 2023) — to the Andhra Pradesh Forest Development Corporation (33,000 ha) and to Govind Mohapatra, a bamboo farmer under the Odisha Bamboo Development Agency. The scheme is operated by the Indian Institute of Forest Management (IIFM), Bhopal. 🎓⭐
  2. Paraquat Dichloride banned nationwide — the Centre banned the registration, manufacture, import, transport, distribution, sale and use of the herbicide Paraquat Dichloride under the Insecticides Act, 1968, cancelling all existing registration certificates (draft notification: 10 July 2026). The Registration Committee cited severe health risks — no known antidote — and India joins ~70 countries restricting it. 🎓

Governance 🎓

  1. PRAGATI-50: 50 model Swachhata cities launched — MoHUA Minister Manohar Lal launched PRAGATI-50 (29 Sept 2026) to develop 50 model cities across 24 states as cleanliness role models under Swachh Bharat Mission–Urban 2.0, with KPI-based action plans, tech-enabled monitoring, and bi-monthly ministerial reviews. 🎓
  2. Narendra Bhooshan takes charge as Environment Secretary — Narendra Bhooshan, a 1992-batch IAS officer of the Uttar Pradesh cadre, assumed charge as Secretary, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change. 🎓

Defence 🛡️🎓

  1. Second Naval Commanders' Conference 2026 concludes — the second edition of the Naval Commanders' Conference 2026 was held 29 September–1 October at Nausena Bhawan, New Delhi, the Navy's apex forum for senior leadership. 🛡️🎓

Sports 🏆

  1. Asian Games Day 13 (1 Oct): India passes 63 medals — Prince Noble & Manu Francis won silver in the men's skiff, India's first sailing medal of the Games; the women's squash team took bronze (lost 2-1 to Hong Kong China in the semifinal); Ganesh Mani Ratnam Thirumuru added individual compound archery bronze. Updated tally: 10 gold, 22 silver, 31+ bronze — 63+ medals, India 8th. 🏆🎓⭐
