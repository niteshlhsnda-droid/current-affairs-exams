📰 Current Affairs — 11 October 2026

🎯 Top stories of the day

Polity & Governance 🏛️

  1. PM Modi in Chennai today — projects worth over ₹13,000 crore (11 Oct) — the PM inaugurates/dedicates and lays foundation stones across metro rail, highways, railways, ports, petroleum and education. Headliners: the Poonamallee–Vadapalani stretch of Chennai Metro Phase II (~₹4,900 crore, 3.7-km double-decker structure, signalling built for driverless operations); National Highway projects worth ~₹3,700 crore (the 36-km Vadugapatti–Therkuvenganallur section of NH-744, the 30-km Madurai Ring Road section of NH-744A, plus the foundation stone for the Thiruvarur Bypass on NH-83); and railway works of ~₹750 crore — the Karaikal–Peralam line, three rail flyovers around Coimbatore, and five redeveloped Amrit stations (Palani, Vellore Cantonment, Ambasamudram, Paramakkudi, Namakkal). The day also includes a Chennai Metro ride from Porur Bypass to Poonamallee Bypass (~11:30 AM), darshan at the Jalakandeswarar Temple (~12:15 PM), a walkthrough of the Sudha Gopalakrishnan Brain Centre at IIT Madras, and the golden jubilee of Jaigopal Garodia Hindu Vidyalaya (~3 PM). 🏦🎓⭐
  1. Lok Sabha passes the twin One Nation One Election bills (9 Oct) — the Constitution (129th Amendment) Bill, 2026 and the Union Territories Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026 cleared the Lok Sabha by the required two-thirds majority after a 12-hour debate. The plan: synchronise Lok Sabha and Assembly polls from 2029, with simultaneous local-body elections within 100 days. Background register: the High-Level Committee was chaired by former President Ram Nath Kovind (report 14 March 2024); amendments touch Articles 83, 85, 172, 174 and 356. The bills still need Rajya Sabha passage and ratification by at least 15 state legislatures. 🎓⭐
  1. President visits the Hidimba Devi Temple, Manali — the President offered prayers at the historic Hidimba Devi Temple in Himachal Pradesh. Exam register: the temple was constructed in 1553 during the reign of Kullu ruler Raja Bahadur Singh. 🎓

Economy & Trade 📊

  1. NSO's first Q2 FY2026-27 GDP advance estimate: 7.4% (10 Oct) — the National Statistics Office (MoSPI) put Q2 FY27 (July–September 2026) growth at 7.4% YoY at constant 2011-12 prices, up from 7.1% in Q1. Sector split: manufacturing GVA 10.3%, construction 9.1%, services 8.4%, while agriculture moderated to 3.4% on uneven monsoon in Central India. 🏦🎓⭐
  1. Global Innovation Index 2026: India at 39th (WIPO, 10 Oct) — India rose to 39th of 132 economies (from 40th in 2025 and 81st in 2015), retaining its slot as the top innovative lower-middle-income economy for the 5th straight year and the top economy in Central and Southern Asia. Switzerland topped for the 16th consecutive year. 🎓🏦⭐
  1. SEBI notifies Settlement Regulations, 2026 — the market regulator has revised how settlement calculations and procedures work for eligible securities-market violations: settlement is pegged to a base amount referencing the minimum prescribed penalty, disgorgement of wrongful gains is treated separately (no double counting), and a fast-track mechanism applies to cases with settlement amounts of ₹10 lakh or less — factoring seriousness of violation, stage of proceedings, aggravating/mitigating circumstances and remedial measures. 🏦
  1. PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana completes one year today (11 Oct) — approved by the Cabinet on 16 July 2025 and launched by the PM on 11 October 2025, the six-year scheme targets 100 Aspirational Agricultural Districts by converging 36 Central schemes across 11 Ministries/Departments plus State schemes and private participation. A government factsheet notes: all 100 districts have prepared District Agriculture Plans, monthly output-indicator rankings run since November 2025, and the average district score rose from 18.85 (April 2026) to 46.89 (August 2026). 🏦🎓
  1. AITIGA 15th Joint Committee: next meeting January 2027 in India — following the 6–9 October Jakarta round (covered yesterday), the committee agreed the next meeting will be held in India in January 2027 in hybrid mode. Exam register: AITIGA was signed in Bangkok in 2009 (Framework Agreement on Comprehensive Economic Cooperation traces to 2003) and entered into force on 1 January 2010; three sub-committees ran in parallel — SC-LII (legal & institutional issues), SC-NTMA (national treatment on market access) and SC-ROO (rules of origin). 🏦🎓
  1. GST Council reforms: simplified compliance from April 2027 — Insights IAS flags the GST Council's compliance-simplification reforms, targeted for rollout from April 2027 — keep the timeline for prelims. 🏦🎓

Defence 🛡️

  1. Indian Navy to induct indigenous ASW vessel 'Mangrole' — the Navy's next indigenous anti-submarine warfare vessel Mangrole is set for fleet induction, adding to the Make-in-India naval build-up (Insights IAS highlights its indigenous ASW capabilities). Also in the naval register: Project Seabird at Karwar continues as India's expanding western-seaboard naval base. 🎓🏭

Society & Environment 🌱

  1. NFHS-6: child marriage at 20.1% — but 2,000+ cases intercepted in Telangana alone — Insights IAS's 10-October analysis traces the persistence of child marriage despite progress: NFHS-6 (2023–24) reports 20.1% of women aged 20–24 married before 18, down from 23.3% (NFHS-5) and 47.4% (NFHS-3). Yet field reporting shows over 2,000 child marriages intercepted in Telangana between 2025 and mid-2026 alone, with poverty, school dropouts, gender inequality and social coercion as the drivers. 🎓
  1. Nanda Devi Biosphere Reserve: growing landslide-prone zones — monitoring has flagged growing landslide-prone zones inside the Nanda Devi Biosphere Reserve — a climate-vulnerability data point for Uttarakhand and a likely mains geography link. 🎓
  1. India to host its first World Landslide Forum (23 Nov, Faridabad) — India will host the inaugural World Landslide Forum from 23 November in Faridabad, Haryana. 🎓

Sports 🏅

  1. 3rd T20 tonight (11 Oct, 7 PM, Indore) — the five-match India–West Indies T20I series resumes at the Holkar Stadium, Indore with the series level 1–1 after India's Lucknow win (6 Oct) and West Indies' record 252/4 chase in Ranchi (9 Oct; Shai Hope 102* off 45). Remaining: 4th T20 on 14 Oct (Hyderabad), 5th on 17 Oct (Bengaluru). 🎓🏦
  1. IOA elections on 5 December — Indian Olympic Association chief P.T. Usha announced the IOA elections for 5 December, calling the process a milestone of good governance. 🎓

International 🌍

  1. Oil tanker attacked at Sharjah Anchorage — all Indian crew safe — Union Minister Sarbananda Sonowal said all Indian crew members are safe after an oil tanker was attacked at the Sharjah Anchorage. 🌍🎓
  1. Magnitude 7.6 earthquake hits Panama (USGS) — a 7.6-magnitude quake struck Panama — the biggest global seismic event of the day. 🌍

Important Days 📅

  • 11 October — International Day of the Girl Child: the UN's annual day for girls' rights and empowerment.
  • Continuing: National Postal Week (9–15 October) in India; 13 October is the International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction.
