📰 Current Affairs — 25 September 2026

*Daily exam-ready digest for Bank • UPSC • PSU aspirants, compiled from

GKToday, Insights IAS, Drishti IAS, Testbook & AffairsCloud.*

Tags: 🏦 Bank • 🎓 UPSC • 🏭 PSU • ⭐ high-yield

🎯 Top stories of the day

Defence 🛡️

  1. INS Trishul arrives at Toulon for 24th Exercise VARUNA — the Indian Navy's guided-missile stealth frigate arrived in Toulon, France, on 22 Sept 2026 for the 24th edition of the bilateral maritime exercise with the French Navy (inception: 2001); the drill features harbour and sea-phase engagements — cross-deck visits, operational interactions and sporting activities — aimed at interoperability; the deployment follows a 14–17 Sept port call at Alexandria, Egypt, with a PASSEX alongside ENS Bernees. 🎓⭐
  2. Yudh Abhyas 2026: US Ambassador Sergio Gor witnesses the drill — the US envoy reached Rajasthan to witness the 22nd edition of the India–US joint exercise, under way at Mahajan Field Firing Range (Rajasthan) and across Uttarakhand; Indian and US troops have already conducted joint artillery drills with M777 howitzers, Dhanush guns, Pinaka MBRLs and M-LIDS. 🎓
  3. CDS stresses integration of technology, data and industry — Chief of Defence Staff General N.S. Raja Subramani emphasised that integration of technology, data and industry is central to strengthening India's defence ecosystem. 🎓

Banking & Finance 🏦

  1. SEBI Board approves sweeping reforms at 215th meeting (24 Sept) — the board in Mumbai cleared: the new SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2026 replacing the 2020 framework (PMS may invest in IPOs, primary-market debt and overseas securities; a new mutual-fund-only PMS with ~₹25 lakh minimum investment vs ₹50 lakh); wider FPI access to non-agri commodity derivatives (physically-settled positions must be exited at least 3 days before expiry, before the tender period); depository receipts for listed REITs/InvITs in overseas jurisdictions; a common advertisement code (celebrities allowed for brand/entity-level promotion, not specific products); new Settlement Regulations 2026 with 9% p.a. disgorgement interest; a ₹75 crore net-worth threshold for vault managers; widened accredited-investor norms; and relaxed corporate-debt rules. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. Bank strike 28–30 Sept: today is the last full working day — the United Forum of Bank Unions (seven unions covering ~90% of the banking workforce) has called a 3-day nationwide strike from 28 Sept; SBI advised customers to finish branch work by 25 Sept (today); ATMs, YONO, internet/mobile banking and UPI stay functional; key demands: 5-day banking week, pension updation, uniform DA formula, NPS-to-OPS switch option; a continuous strike from 26 Oct is also on notice. 🏦🎓🏭⭐

International 🌍

  1. Jaishankar chairs 2nd FIPIC Foreign Ministers' Meeting in New York — on 24 Sept, on the sidelines of UNGA 81, EAM S. Jaishankar chaired the second Foreign Ministers' Meeting of the Forum for India–Pacific Islands Cooperation (FIPIC, platform launched 2014 in Fiji); leaders reviewed the 12-Point Action Plan announced by PM Modi in 2023 and discussed renewable energy, climate action, disaster resilience, education, healthcare and capacity building; Jaishankar endorsed the Pacific Islands Forum's 2050 Strategy for the Blue Pacific Continent and proposed tailored capacity-building programmes for Pacific professionals. 🎓⭐

Schemes & Governance 🏛️

  1. FSSAI proposes ban on analogue paneer sold as 'paneer' — draft Food Safety and Standards (Prohibition and Restrictions on Sales) Amendment Regulations, 2026 (notified 22 Sept, Gazette-published 23 Sept) proposes adding "paneer made of constituents not derived from milk" to restricted products; items already licensed under 'Analogue in Dairy Context' must drop the word 'Paneer' from nomenclature, labelling and marketing; objections/suggestions invited within 60 days of Gazette publication; at least eight states (including Maharashtra, Karnataka, Gujarat) have already acted against analogue paneer at state level. 🎓⭐
  2. Draft National Electricity Policy 2026 sent to Cabinet — the Power Secretary said the Power Ministry has forwarded the draft National Electricity Policy 2026 to the Cabinet for approval. 🏦🎓🏭
  3. Railways: 2nd Saraighat rail-cum-road bridge over the Brahmaputra — the Ministry of Railways will build the Second Saraighat Rail-cum-Road Bridge in Assam along with doubling of the Agthori–Kamakhya track, both under the Northeast Frontier Railway (NFR) zone; separately, Railways approved a ₹166.5 crore four-lane ROB at Alkapuri in Vadodara and a new daily overnight train between Kanpur and Anand Vihar. 🎓🏭

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. India meets 99.2% of domestic mobile phone demand — Electronics Secretary S. Krishnan said domestic manufacturing now meets 99.2% of India's mobile phone demand, marking a major milestone for electronics self-reliance. 🎓🏭⭐

Education 📚

  1. NEET-PG 2026 results declared — the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) declared results on 24 Sept for the exam held 30 Aug 2026 (2,63,544 candidates) plus a Jaipur re-exam on 5 Sept for 2,416 candidates hit by a power failure; category cut-offs: General/EWS 262 (50th percentile), General-PwBD 244 (45th), SC/ST/OBC 226 (40th); ranks based on actual marks with tie-breaking criteria; individual scorecards and answer keys to be released on natboard.edu.in in October; the Board found none of the questions technically incorrect. 🎓⭐

Sports 🏆

  1. Asian Games 2026 Day 6: five medals, India at 17 — on 24 Sept India won 2 silver and 3 bronze: mixed 4x400m relay silver (Poovamma Raju, Vishal Thennarasu, Vithya Ramraj, Manu TS); Seema Kumari bronze in women's 10,000m — India's first athletics medal of the Games; Parinaaz Dhaliwal and Anantjeet Singh Naruka bronze in skeet mixed team; Roshibina Devi silver in women's 60kg wushu; Satnam Singh and Salman Khan bronze in men's double sculls (6:13.25); India's tally rose to 17 (1 gold, 6 silver, 10 bronze) but the country slipped to 15th; China crossed the 100-medal mark on the sixth day. 🎓⭐
  2. Hockey: India thrash South Korea 8-2 — India's biggest-ever win over South Korea in Asian Games history; in kabaddi, the women's team beat Japan 50-25 to reach the semifinal and the men's team beat Chinese Taipei to top the group — both assured of medals. 🎓

Important Days 📅

  1. World Pharmacists Day — 25 September — the International Pharmaceutical Federation's (FIP) annual observance; 2026 theme: "Empowering pharmacists for healthier futures", highlighting pharmacists' role in prevention, access to care and health-system resilience. 🎓⭐
  2. Antyodaya Diwas — 25 September — observed on the birth anniversary of Pandit Deendayal Upadhyaya to honour his Antyodaya vision — the upliftment of the last person in the queue. 🎓

🌙 Evening updates (published 19:41 IST)

International & Economy 🌍🏦

  1. India–New Zealand FTA to enter into force on 20 Oct 2026 — Piyush Goyal announced the pact, signed on 27 April 2026 at Bharat Mandapam by Goyal and NZ Trade Minister Todd McClay, will take effect after ratification in both countries; 100% of India's exports to NZ get duty-free access (textiles & apparel, leather & footwear, gems & jewellery, engineering goods, processed foods); India also gets duty-free inputs like wooden logs, coking coal and metal scrap; aim: double bilateral trade to ₹35,000 crore within five years. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. Fitch raises India's FY27 GDP growth forecast to 6.9% — up 50 bps from 6.4%, citing stronger-than-expected growth and resilient investment; India grew 7.8% YoY in Q1 FY27 despite the energy-price shock from the US–Iran conflict; Fitch expects headline inflation to rise to 5.5% by Dec 2026, easing to 4.2% by end-2027. 🏦🎓

Defence & Security 🛡️

  1. MoD signs ₹810.79 crore contract with BDL for 160 SAT-SAAW systems — signed 23 Sept in New Delhi (Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh) under the Buy (Indian-IDDM) category; the Satellite Smart Anti-Airfield Weapon is a DRDO-developed air-to-ground precision glide bomb (60% indigenous content) that can be launched from Jaguar, Hawk and Su-30 MKI aircraft; delivery in 2027-28 and 2028-29 will strengthen the IAF's precision-strike capability. 🎓⭐
  2. Nomadic Elephant 2026: 18th India–Mongolia joint exercise begins — under way at Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand, with 45 personnel from each side; focus on counter-insurgency operations under a UN mandate, plus drone operations, cybersecurity and cordon-and-search drills. 🎓
  3. 3rd ANTF Conference; Amit Shah sets drug-free India target for 2029 — the two-day National Conference of Anti-Narcotics Task Force heads of states/UTs (New Delhi, 22–23 Sept; 36 states/UTs) was organised by the NCB on the theme "United Resolve, Shared Responsibility"; it will implement the Vision Document on Narcotics Control (2026–2029) targeting a drug-free India by 31 December 2029, aligned with Viksit Bharat 2047; Shah also released four anti-narcotics TVCs, launched the revamped NCB website and laid foundation stones for NCB zonal complexes at Lucknow and Dehradun. 🎓

Health & Multilateral 🏥

  1. OneSCD: India co-hosts global sickle-cell partnership at UNGA81 — launched 23 Sept in New York alongside Nigeria, WHO, UNICEF, Africa CDC, St. Jude Children's Research Hospital and the World Coalition on Sickle Cell Disease; the partnership aims to close global gaps in diagnosis, treatment and survival for sickle cell disease; India showcased its National Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission (2023, elimination target 2047) — 72.9 million+ screened, 2 million+ carriers identified, 2.5 lakh diagnosed and 49.3 million Sickle Cell Cards issued. 🎓⭐
  2. AB PM-JAY completes 8 years with 60+ crore beneficiaries — launched by PM Modi on 23 Sept 2018 at Ranchi, the Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (MoH&FW) is the world's largest government-funded public health insurance scheme: ₹5 lakh annual cover per family for secondary and tertiary care; centrally sponsored with 60:40 Centre–state funding (90:10 for NE states, HP, Uttarakhand, J&K). 🎓

Banking & Finance 🏦

  1. ICICI Prudential Life Insurance renamed ICICI Life Insurance — effective 22 Sept 2026 after RoC approval; UK-based Prudential plc is being reclassified from promoter to passive investor in the company as it plans to acquire a controlling stake in Bharti Life Insurance. 🏦
  2. BIS certification made mandatory for smartphone screen protectors — MeitY brought them under the Compulsory Registration Order, 2021, from 1 April 2027, requiring conformity to IS 19348:2025 (thickness, dimensions, light transmission, visual defects, strength) before manufacture, import or sale; the 66th product category under the framework, alongside smartphones, laptops, chargers, LED TVs and set-top boxes. 🏦🎓

Energy & Sports ⚡🏆

  1. Premier Energies commissions India's largest solar cell facility — a 7 GW N-type TOPCon (G12R) plant at Naidupeta, Andhra Pradesh (101 acres, ₹3,293 crore capex), lifting the company's total solar cell capacity from 3.6 GW to 10.6 GW — India's largest; trial runs are on before commercial production, with ~88,000 cells/hour at ~25.8% average efficiency and Zero Liquid Discharge recycling. 🏦🏭⭐
  2. Pune to host UCI Road World Championships 2032 — announced at the UCI's 195th Congress in Montréal: cycling's road world championships come to India for the first time; Pune's bid (jointly led by the Maharashtra government and the Cycling Federation of India) beat Groningen/Drenthe, Netherlands, in the final round. 🎓
  3. Jay Meena: India's first-ever Asian Games soft-tennis medal — the 26-year-old from Dewas (MP) took bronze in men's singles on 23 Sept, losing 2–4 to Japan's Takuya Kurosaka in the semifinal; he had beaten Pakistan's Arain Hussain 4–0 in the group stage and the Philippines' Sherwin Nuguit 4–3 in a 7-game quarterfinal thriller. 🎓

State & Governance 🏛️

  1. Delhi Cabinet approves DURGA scheme: 1,300 pink e-autos — the Driving Upliftment and Rozgar for Women/Transgender Green E-Auto scheme gives dignified self-employment to women and transgender persons (1,170 autos for women, 130 for transgender persons; up to 90 and 10 respectively per revenue district); eligibility: women aged 20–40, Delhi residents with voter ID, LMV licence and PSV badge, family income up to ₹5 lakh; 3-year vehicle lock-in. 🎓
