🎯 Top stories of the day

Asian Games 2026 — Final day (4 Oct) 🏆

  1. India finish 4th with 85 medals (21 gold, 27 silver, 37 bronze) — India ended the 20th Asian Games (Aichi-Nagoya) 4th in the medal table, its second-highest tally ever. India were tied with Uzbekistan on 21 golds each, but edged ahead on silvers (27 vs 25) after Uzbekistan failed to add gold on the final day — their 21st gold came in Saturday's men's hockey final. Final table: China 169 gold, Japan 83 (host-nation record), South Korea 39, India 21, Uzbekistan 21, Iran 19. India had no events on Sunday — the final day was a send-off. 🏆🎓⭐
  2. Closing ceremony today: Neeru Dhanda carries the flag — the closing ceremony is scheduled for 2:30–4:00 PM IST today at the Nagoya City Mizuho Park Athletic Stadium (Paloma Mizuho Stadium), telecast on Sony Sports Network / Sony LIV (DD Sports also listed). India's flag-bearer is shooter Neeru Dhanda, 27 — women's individual trap gold (India's first-ever Asian Games individual trap gold), trap mixed-team gold with Kynan Chenai, and women's trap team silver with Manisha Keer and Aashima Ahlawat. She had flown home but was recalled to Nagoya by deputy chef de mission Anjali Bhagwat. 🏆🎓⭐
  3. Golden final weekend — hockey double & boxing trio — men's hockey beat Malaysia 5-1 for India's 5th men's hockey gold and a first-ever back-to-back title defence; captain Harmanpreet Singh finished tournament top scorer with 12 goals and is the first Indian captain to lead two straight Asian Games golds — plus a direct LA 2028 Olympic berth. The women's hockey gold (1-0 over China) ended a 44-year wait — the first time India won men's and women's hockey gold at the same Asian Games. In boxing, Lovlina Borgohain (only the second Indian woman after Mary Kom to win Asian Games boxing gold) and Parveen Hooda headlined three boxing golds. 🏆🎓⭐
  4. More final-weekend medals — marathoner Sawan Barwal took silver in a national record 2:11:37; women's 50m rifle 3-positions team silver (Ashi Chouksey, Tilottama Sen, Vidarsa Vinod); table tennis mixed doubles bronze (Manush Shah, Diya Chitale); squash mixed doubles bronze (Joshna Chinappa, Velavan Senthilkumar); sailing bronze for Nethra Kumanan (women's dinghy); and men's quadrant sepak takraw bronze. India's equestrian campaign ended medal-less (4th in team dressage). 🏆🎓
  5. Games controversies in focus — Chinese judoka Tang Jing was disqualified for biting Japan's Maeda Rin on the arm (women's 70kg quarterfinal); Japanese spectators waving the Rising Sun flag during the judo mixed-team final sparked a row after organisers posted, then removed and apologised for, a prohibition notice; organisational problems included no athletes' village (hotels, container "villas" and a cruise ship), transport delays, and the wrong anthem at a South Korean hockey match. 🏆

Banking & Markets 🏦🎓

  1. RBI MPC meets Oct 5–7: rate-hike buzz — the six-member Monetary Policy Committee meets 5–7 October, with Governor Sanjay Malhotra announcing the decision on 7 October. The repo rate stands at 5.25%; a Reuters poll (Sept 18–28) of 61 economists found 35 expecting a 25 bps hike to 5.50% — the first increase since February 2023 — with 8 of 10 in a Business Standard poll agreeing. Drivers: CPI rose to 4.82% in August (from 4.45% in July), above RBI's 4% target for a third straight month; crude is above $100/barrel amid Middle East tensions; the rupee has weakened ~6% this year. SBI Research (Oct 2) expects CPI at ~5.65% in September and above 6.5% in Oct–Nov, forecasting hikes in October and December; BofA sees a cumulative 100 bps of hikes through H1 2027. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. RBI names Sudhakar Malli Executive Director — announced 2 October, effective 1 October 2026: Malli, earlier Chief General Manager-in-Charge of the Department of Supervision, now heads the Department of Supervision (Supervisory Assessment), overseeing banks, NBFCs and co-operative banks. A career central banker (~3 decades, 25+ years in supervision, ~5 years overseas supervisory experience) with a BTech (Mechanical) and CAIIB. The same announcement also noted recent ED appointments Suman Ray (September) and Monisha Chakraborty (August). 🏦🎓
  3. UPI: 145 billion transactions in H1 FY27; 0.4% MDR from Oct 15 — NPCI data (reported Oct 2): UPI volume hit ~145 billion in April–September 2026, up 27% YoY (vs 114 bn in H1 FY26); value rose 20% to ₹177 lakh crore. From 15 October 2026, NPCI introduces a 0.4% Merchant Discount Rate on specified person-to-merchant UPI transactions above ₹2,000 — capped at ₹300 for transactions of ₹75,000 and above — ending six years of zero-MDR. P2P transfers and merchant payments up to ₹2,000 stay free; ~96% of P2M transactions are expected to be unaffected. The Supreme Court issued notices to the Centre, RBI and NPCI in a legal challenge but declined an interim stay. 🏦🎓⭐
  4. SEBI's 'Project Jagrook' circular + NSE corporate bond futures — a SEBI circular dated 1 October 2026 requires all stock brokers to display SEBI-prescribed investor-awareness messages on websites 5–31 October (alongside risk disclosures), mandatory on website landing pages from 1 November; on trading apps, voluntary Oct 5–31 and then mandatory on alternate days with risk disclosures from 1 November — timed with World Investor Week 2026. Separately, SEBI gave NSE a no-objection certificate to launch corporate bond index futures (launch subject to RBI approval) to deepen the corporate bond market; outstanding corporate bonds stood at ₹61.05 lakh crore as of August 2026. 🏦🎓
  5. Bank/insurer leadership churn — HDFC Bank: Anup Bagchi takes charge as MD & CEO on 27 October 2026 for a 3-year term (till 26 Oct 2029), succeeding Sashidhar Jagdishan; RBI approved under Section 35B of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (approval Oct 1); appointed Additional Director from 2 October; Bagchi (ICICI Group since 1992) is currently MD & CEO of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance. Kotak Mahindra Bank: RBI approved Anup Kumar Saha as MD & CEO from 1 January 2027 (3 years), succeeding Ashok Vaswani. South Indian Bank: Mahesh Muralidhar Pai took charge as MD & CEO from 1 October 2026 (3 years), succeeding P R Seshadri. New India Assurance (NIACL): DFS appointed Lavanya R Mundayur as Chairman-cum-MD till superannuation on 31 May 2029. 🏦🎓⭐
  6. Small savings rates unchanged; RoDTEP extended — the Finance Ministry's DEA notification (30 Sept) keeps small-savings rates unchanged for 1 Oct–31 Dec 2026: PPF 7.1%, NSC 7.7%, Sukanya Samriddhi 8.2%, SCSS 8.2%, KVP 7.5% (maturing in 115 months), 1-yr TD 6.9%, 2-yr 7%, 3-yr 7.1%, 5-yr 7.5%, 5-yr RD 6.7%, monthly income 7.4%, savings deposit 4%. Also: RoDTEP (Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products) extended three months to 31 December 2026 (Notification No. 41/2026-27) with rates (0.3%–3.9%) unchanged — covering DTA units, Advance Authorisation holders, SEZs and EOUs; and timelines under Component II of RELIEF (Resilience & Logistics Intervention for Export Facilitation) were extended with 95% risk coverage for insurance on upcoming shipments amid West Asia disruptions. CCI approved BNP Paribas Cardif's 26% stake buy in IndiaFirst Life Insurance from Warburg Pincus (post-deal: Bank of Baroda 65%, BNP Paribas Cardif 26%, Union Bank of India 9%). 🏦🎓⭐

Governance & Policy 🎓

  1. MoHUA launches PRAGATI-50 — Union Minister Manohar Lal Khattar launched PRAGATI-50 in New Delhi: 50 cities across 24 states will be developed as role-model sanitation cities under Swachh Bharat Mission-Urban 2.0, with a PRAGATI digital review mechanism on the Swachhatam Portal; a Swachh Bharat Test Bed & Innovation Centre will come up at Durgapur (West Bengal) with CSIR-CMERI. 🎓🏭⭐
  2. National Coastal Mission 2.0 guidelines — Union Minister Bhupender Yadav launched the NCM 2.0 guidelines: a Central Sector Scheme with a total outlay of ₹767 crore, running till 2030-31 across 9 coastal states (Gujarat, Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, West Bengal) and 4 UTs, in five thematic categories — blue-economy growth, coastal vulnerability, climate adaptation, capacity building and project management. NCM 1.0 (2021-22 to 2025-26) had an outlay of just ₹87 crore. 🎓⭐
  3. MHA standardises 28 Ladakh place names — an MHA/PIB release (2 Oct) standardised the official names of 28 peaks, mountains, passes, glaciers, valleys and lakes/rivers in Ladakh on Survey of India maps (in consultation with the UT administration): peaks Atisha Giri, Kanishka Peak, Martand Giri, Zorawar Peak, Rinchen Zangpo, Sakyasri Giri, Marpa Lotsava Ri, Milarepa Kangri, Shiva Ri, Maheshvara Ri; mountains Karun Pir, Drak Karpo, Jolmori; passes Chapchingal, Shachmirk; glaciers Parpik, Skamri; and Shkorga Valley. 🎓
  4. Big energy pushes: GOBARDHAN & Samudra Manthan — the ₹23,731-crore GOBARDHAN scheme (Cabinet approval 6 Aug 2026) targets scaling compressed biogas to ~5 million cubic metres/day — nearly 10x current output — over a 10-year roadmap (FY 2026-27 to 2035-36). And the Samudra Manthan Mission (National Offshore Exploration Scheme, MoPNG): ₹84,084 crore till FY 2030-31 for deep/ultra-deepwater hydrocarbon exploration — ₹43,200 crore for 60 wells, ₹28,534 crore for seismic work, ₹10,000 crore for shared subsea infrastructure — targeting the Mahanadi, Andaman-Nicobar, Krishna-Godavari, Cauvery and Kutch-Mumbai basins. 🎓🏭⭐
  5. Appointments & security — Dhirendra K. Ojha (1990-batch IIS) was re-employed as PIB Principal Director General on contract (1 Oct 2026–30 Sept 2027) on ACC approval post-superannuation; ACC also upgraded a DG-level post to Pr. DG level for Pragya Paliwal Gaur. Lt Gen Akhilesh Kumar assumed charge as GOC of 1 Corps (Mathura) on 1 October, succeeding Lt Gen V Hariharan. India secured the deportation of Navpreet Singh from Türkiye (3 Oct) in drug-trafficking-linked cases. INS Trishul reached Valencia, Spain, on overseas deployment — an India-France maritime-diplomacy touchpoint. 🎓🏭

International & World 🌍

  1. Morocco gets its first woman PM — King Mohammed VI appointed Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri as Morocco's first woman Prime Minister (September 2026, after the Sept 23 parliamentary elections). She leads the Authenticity and Modernity Party (PAM, 97 of 395 seats — needing a coalition for the 198-seat majority) and succeeds Aziz Akhannouch — becoming only the second woman PM in the Arab world after Tunisia's Najla Bouden (2021). 🎓🏦
  2. G7 to release 100 million barrels from emergency reserves — the G7 agreed on 2 October to release 100 million barrels of oil and fuel products from emergency reserves amid the West Asia crisis — context for the RBI's inflation watch and India's fuel-levy management. 🎓🏦
  3. 5th Kautilya Economic Conclave begins — the conclave opened 3 October in New Delhi on the theme "Resilience in an Age of Flux". RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra warned that "today's resilience may not necessarily imply tomorrow's immunity," noting it took nearly a decade to clean up the early-2000s lending/NPA legacy, and flagged new systemic risks as increasingly exogenous, cross-border and interconnected — the next crisis could begin with a geopolitical event, cyberattack or tech failure. Also announced: Kolkata will host the 5th India Energy Week (28–31 Jan 2027, New Town Convention Centre) — theme "Unlocking Innovation. Securing Energy. Shaping Tomorrow."; ~75,000 professionals and 700+ exhibitors expected; the first edition in eastern India (debut Bengaluru 2023; 4th edition Goa 2026). 🎓🏦

Science, Tech & Environment 🎓

  1. New caecilian from Kolhapur; Great Indian Bustards released — *Gegeneophis chandgadi*, a new caecilian (subterranean amphibian) species, was described from Kolhapur, with the Northern Western Ghats flagged as a caecilian biodiversity hotspot. Ten captive-bred Great Indian Bustards are being released at Ramdevra, Jaisalmer (3–4 Oct). And a Wayanad vulture survey (26–27 Sept) counted 65 vultures, including 54 white-rumped vultures. 🎓⭐
  2. First direct radio-signal detection from an exoplanet — astronomers announced the first direct detection of radio signals from exoplanet Beta Pictoris b (63 light-years away, a gas giant) — reported 23 September; a milestone in exoplanet magnetosphere studies. 🎓
  3. Health & agri-trade notes — CSIR-CCMB developed an India-specific disease severity score for sickle cell anaemia; APEDA flagged off a frozen-food consignment from Sonipat (Haryana) to Canada (1 Oct). 🎓🏭
