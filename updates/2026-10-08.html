🎯 Top stories of the day

Nobel Prizes 🌍🔬

  1. Nobel Prize in Literature 2026 announced TODAY at 4:30 PM IST — the Swedish Academy names this year's Literature laureate on 8 October at 13:00 CEST (4:30 PM IST), the fourth Nobel of 2026. Already announced this week: Medicine (5 Oct) — Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel for light-gated ion channels/optogenetics; Physics (6 Oct) — Francis Halzen for the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and high-energy astrophysical neutrinos; Chemistry (7 Oct) — Henri B. Kagan (France) and Kenso Soai (Japan) for non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis. Bookmakers' favourite for Literature is Chinese writer Can Xue. Still to come: Peace (9 Oct, Norwegian Nobel Committee, 2:30 PM IST) and Economic Sciences (12 Oct). Prize money: 12 million Swedish kronor (~US$1.2 million). 🎓⭐

Economy & Trade 📊

  1. RBI raises repo rate to 5.5% — first hike in nearly 4 years — the Reserve Bank of India on 7 October raised the policy repo rate by 25 bps to 5.50%, the first increase since February 2023, citing inflation that is "no longer benign". The 6-member MPC (met 5–7 Oct in Mumbai) voted unanimously for the hike and shifted the stance from "neutral" to "calibrated tightening" (4–2 majority), ruling out rate cuts in the near term. SDF rate adjusted to 5.25%, MSF rate and Bank Rate to 5.75%. Drivers: August CPI inflation at 4.82% (third month above the 4% target), West Asia escalation pushing crude to ~US$114–116/barrel, and a 13% southwest-monsoon deficit + El Niño lifting food prices. FY27 inflation forecast raised to 5.2%; GDP forecast raised to 7.1% (Q1 grew 7.8%). Governor: Sanjay Malhotra. 🎓🏦⭐

Defence & Security 🛡️

  1. SAREX-2026: mass SAR demo off Paradip coast TODAY (8 Oct) — the 12th National Maritime Search & Rescue Exercise (SAREX-2026) was inaugurated on 7 October by Raksha Rajya Mantri Sanjay Seth; the main search-and-rescue demonstration runs today (8 Oct) off the Paradip coast, organised by the Indian Coast Guard — one of the region's largest, most complex SAR drills. 32 observers from 30 countries are attending. Assets: 15 ICG ships, 2 Dornier aircraft, 3 Advanced Light Helicopters, 1 Chetak, IAF C-130J & Mi-17, 2 Indian Navy ships, plus civil/state assets. The drill simulates a mass rescue operation — a passenger aircraft ditching at sea with 228 persons on board, colliding with container vessel MV Zumba. Alongside, the 24th NMSAR Board Meeting meets at Bhubaneswar under DG ICG Paramesh Sivamani, aligned with the SAGAR / MAHASAGAR vision. 🎓🏭⭐

Exams & Education 📚

  1. 72nd BPSC Prelims postponed AGAIN — will not be held on 25 Oct — the Bihar Public Service Commission has postponed the 72nd Combined (Preliminary) Competitive Examination, previously scheduled for 25 October 2026, for the second time (it was first moved from 26 July). No fresh date has been announced; the October date was only tentative in the revised exam calendar, and the commission is expected to notify the revised schedule on bpsc.bihar.gov.in separately. The drive fills roughly 1,186 posts (SDO, DSP and allied Bihar government services); selection is through prelims (objective screening) → mains → interview. 🎓⭐

International 🌍

  1. Jaishankar in Ghana; India–Ghana exchange 2 MoUs — External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar arrived in Accra on 6 October on the first leg of a three-nation Africa tour (Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Ethiopia), meeting Ghana's Foreign Minister Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa and President John Dramani Mahama. The two sides exchanged 2 MoUs: Zoological Survey of India ↔ University of Ghana (academics, scientific research, capacity building) and ICAR ↔ Ghana's CSIR-Crops Research Institute (agricultural research & education). Talks covered the Comprehensive Partnership in trade, pharma, defence, technology and people-to-people ties, plus multilateral cooperation and India's engagement with the African Union and ECOWAS (15-member Economic Community of West African States; Ghana a founding member). Ties were elevated to Comprehensive Partnership during PM Modi's 2025 state visit. 🎓⭐

🌆 Evening updates

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. IISc's Mahesh Kakde wins Ramanujan Prize 2026 — Mahesh Kakde, professor of mathematics at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, has been awarded the Ramanujan Prize 2026 for Young Mathematicians from Developing Countries, jointly given by the International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP) and the International Mathematical Union (IMU). The $10,000 prize recognises researchers under 45 working in developing countries; Kakde was honoured for algebraic number theory — fundamental results in noncommutative Iwasawa theory and the Gross-Stark & Brumer-Stark conjectures, with implications for Hilbert's 12th problem (one of David Hilbert's 23 problems of 1900). Earlier honours: Infosys Prize in Mathematical Sciences (2022). 🎓⭐

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. PM Modi inaugurates India Mobile Congress today (8 Oct) — the 10th India Mobile Congress (7–10 Oct, Yashobhoomi, New Delhi; theme "Scale Without Boundaries", by DoT + COAI) sees PM Narendra Modi inaugurate the event today on its second day. Built on four pillars — Srishti, Samvardhan, Sashakt, Suraksha — the congress hosts 1.5 lakh+ attendees, 300+ exhibitors from 100+ countries and 1,500+ tech use cases, spanning 6G, AI, satcom, semiconductors, quantum and cybersecurity. 🎓🏭⭐

Economy & Trade 📊

  1. World Bank: India among top-10 emerging-market AI performers — a World Bank report, "India and Artificial Intelligence: Seizing the Development Opportunity", ranks India among the top 10 emerging-market performers in AI readiness, citing technical talent, digital public infrastructure and a strong IT ecosystem. Private AI investment in India rose from US$1.2 billion (2024) to US$4.1 billion (2025); about 23% of Indian firms report using AI. 🎓🏦⭐

Governance & Polity 🎓

  1. INDIA bloc MPs to stage another protest against CEC today (8 Oct) — a day after Rahul and Priyanka Gandhi's detention during the march toward the Election Commission over the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls, opposition MPs plan another protest today, continuing their demand for the resignation of CEC Gyanesh Kumar. Meanwhile the Supreme Court has agreed to examine a plea to recall its 23 September split verdict on the law governing CEC/EC appointments; the matter was mentioned before CJI Surya Kant. 🎓

National 🇮🇳

  1. Five-storey building collapse in Delhi's Seemapuri (7 Oct) — a five-storey residential building collapsed in New Seemapuri, Delhi on 7 October, killing one person and injuring eight others; rescue teams from the Delhi Fire Service, NDRF, police and civic authorities searched the debris for the trapped, including a pregnant woman and children. 🏦

Important Days 📅

  • 8 October — Indian Air Force Day: commemorates the establishment of the Indian Air Force on 8 October 1932 — this is the 94th Air Force Day.
  • 2–8 October — National Wildlife Week concludes today: the 70th National Wildlife Week (2–8 October) promotes wildlife conservation and biodiversity awareness.
