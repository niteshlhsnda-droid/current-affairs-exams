🎯 Top stories of the day

Nobel Prizes 🌍🔬

  1. Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2026 announced TODAY at 3:15 PM IST — the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences names this year's Chemistry laureate(s) on 7 October at 3:15 PM IST. It is the third Nobel of 2026: the Medicine prize (5 Oct) went to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel for light-gated ion channels/optogenetics, and the Physics prize (6 Oct) went to Francis Halzen for the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and high-energy astrophysical neutrinos. Still to come this week: Literature (8 Oct, Swedish Academy, 4:30 PM IST), Peace (9 Oct, Norwegian Nobel Committee, 2:30 PM IST) and Economic Sciences (12 Oct). Prize money: 12 million Swedish kronor (~US$1.2 million). 🎓⭐

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. 10th India Mobile Congress begins today (7–10 Oct), Yashobhoomi, New Delhi — Asia's largest digital-technology forum opens its 10th edition today at Yashobhoomi (India International Convention & Expo Centre), New Delhi, with the theme "Scale Without Boundaries" (unveiled by Communications Minister Jyotiraditya Scindia). Organised by the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) and the Cellular Operators Association of India (COAI), the event is expected to draw 1.5 lakh+ attendees, 300+ exhibitors from 100+ countries and 1,500+ technology use cases. The programme is built on four pillars — Srishti (building the future: 6G, AI-native telecom, quantum, semiconductors), Samvardhan (fuelling growth: 5G monetisation, satcom), Sashakt (expanding opportunities) and Suraksha (strengthening trust: cybersecurity). PM Narendra Modi inaugurates the event on 8 October, with keynotes expected from Akash Ambani (Jio), Sunil Bharti Mittal (Bharti) and Kumar Mangalam Birla; day 1 features Starlink's Lauren Dreyer, Japan's vice-minister Hideo Fuseda, and executives from Qualcomm, GSMA, Ericsson, Jio, Vi, Airtel and Intel. 🎓🏭⭐

International 🌍

  1. India condemns projectile strike on ship MT On Peace in the Strait of Hormuz — a Panama-flagged commercial vessel, MT On Peace, was struck by a projectile while transiting the Strait of Hormuz off the coast of Oman on 6 October; of the 19-strong crew (17 Indians), 12 were injured — 11 of them Indian nationals. The Ministry of External Affairs condemned the attack, calling the continuing attacks on shipping in the region a "cause of deep concern", and said the injured were evacuated with Omani assistance and are being treated in Khasab, Oman. MEA also flagged a separate attack in the Black Sea on MV Royad Mammadov (fire on board, 2 dead, 11 rescued; 3 Indian crew members safe), and called for an immediate cessation of attacks on commercial shipping, seafarers and civilian infrastructure. 🎓⭐

Defence & Security 🛡️

  1. MoD signs ₹661.50-crore BrahMos contract for Navy — on 6 October, the Ministry of Defence signed a ₹661.50-crore contract with BrahMos Aerospace Private Limited (BAPL) for BrahMos Fire Control Systems and launchers for Indian Navy ships, in the presence of Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh. Placed under the Buy (Indian-IDDM) category (Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured), the contract mandates a minimum 68% indigenous content with participation from Indian industries including MSMEs, and is expected to generate employment over three years. BrahMos is the Navy's primary surface-to-surface missile system for maritime strike operations (supersonic, land and sea targets, pinpoint accuracy); the buy follows the Defence Acquisition Council's August 2025 approval, linked to Shivalik-class ships and Next Generation Missile Vessels. 🎓🏭⭐

🌆 Evening updates

Economy & Trade 📊

  1. First upper-Assam cargo to a foreign port since Independence: Bogibeel → Dhaka (5 Oct) — a barge carrying 540 metric tonnes of methanol left Bogibeel terminal (Dibrugarh) for Dhaka, Bangladesh on 5 October, flagged off by Ports, Shipping & Waterways Minister Sarbananda Sonowal (Assam CM Himanta Biswa Sarma joined virtually) — the first cargo shipment from upper Assam to a foreign port since 1947, reviving a river route silent for ~70 years. The methanol, produced by Assam Petro-Chemicals Ltd (APCL) at Namrup, is bound for Pangaon Port; the vessel (barge DLB Patkai, tug Kushal Konwar) travels 768 km on the Brahmaputra (National Waterway-2) to the border, then along the Indo-Bangladesh Protocol Route (IBPR) — 1,300 km total to Narayanganj. The voyage tests the Brahmaputra as a commercial freight corridor for the Northeast; regular methanol shipments are planned, enabled by new customs and immigration complexes at Bogibeel and Dhubri. 🎓⭐

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. ₹130 crore RDI support for BigEndian's Project VeerAI — the Technology Development Board (TDB), under the Department of Science & Technology (DST), signed an agreement on 5 October with Bengaluru-based BigEndian Semiconductors for ₹130 crore under the Research Development and Innovation (RDI) Fund, to be routed as Optional Convertible Debt (OCD). The funding backs Project VeerAI (total project cost ₹260 crore) — an indigenous camera-focused AI Vision System-on-Chip (SoC) on a sovereign technology platform, initially for secure CCTV/surveillance applications (India and the Global South), with a roadmap into defence, automotive, industrial and medical sectors. The project aims to move the technology from TRL-5 (technology validated in a relevant environment) to TRL-9 (proven and ready for commercial deployment) — a flagship "application-specific AI chip" step to cut dependence on foreign semiconductor supply chains. 🎓🏦⭐

Governance & Polity 🎓

  1. INDIA bloc leaders detained after march toward Election Commission — on 6 October, Congress leaders Rahul Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi Vadra, along with other INDIA bloc leaders, were detained by Delhi Police after a hours-long sit-in at Akashvani Bhawan during their protest march toward the Election Commission headquarters, demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls. Rahul Gandhi alleged a "vote chori" conspiracy against the Modi government, the Home Minister, the RSS chief and the CEC. 🎓

International 🌍

  1. 2nd India–EFTA Prosperity Summit today (7 Oct) — with Swiss President Guy Parmelin concluding his 5–7 October state visit to India, the two sides hold the 2nd India–EFTA Prosperity Summit today — marking one year of the India–EFTA Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement (EFTA = Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein; USD 100 billion investment target into India and 1 million jobs over 15 years), following the migration and mobility partnership pact signed on 5 October. 🎓⭐

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. Nobel Chemistry 2026 winners announced (7 Oct, 3:15 PM IST) — the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences awarded the prize to Henri B. Kagan (France, 95) and Kenso Soai (Japan, 76) for "the discovery of non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis" — solving a century-old puzzle of chirality: many molecules exist in two mirror-image forms, yet life uses only one (homochirality), and drug manufacture depends on steering reactions to the right hand. Kagan (Université Paris-Sud, Orsay) showed in 1986 how to tilt a reaction toward one mirror image; Soai (Tokyo University of Science) reported in 1995 the famous "Soai reaction" — an autocatalytic loop that amplifies one handedness from a tiny initial difference. Prize money: 12 million Swedish kronor (~US$1.2 million). Still to come this week: Literature (8 Oct, 4:30 PM IST), Peace (9 Oct, 2:30 PM IST), Economics (12 Oct). 🎓⭐

Governance & Polity 🎓

  1. Union Cabinet's 10-point resolution on PM Modi's 25 years as head of government — the Union Cabinet on 6 October adopted a 10-point resolution congratulating PM Narendra Modi on completing 25 years of continuous leadership as head of government — from taking oath as Gujarat Chief Minister on 7 October 2001 to becoming Prime Minister in May 2014; the milestone is formally marked today, 7 October. I&B Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said the PM received a standing ovation from Cabinet colleagues. The resolution traces Gujarat initiatives carried to the national stage (e.g. the SWAGAT grievance portal, which won the UN Public Service Award in 2010, inspiring PRAGATI; Jyotigram separate farm-power feeders), and cites ~60 crore Jan Dhan accounts, ₹4 lakh+ crore PM-KISAN transfers, public capex up from ₹2 lakh crore to over ₹12 lakh crore, plus the 2016 surgical strikes, 2019 Balakot strikes and 2025 Operation Sindoor on national security. 🎓⭐

Defence & Security 🛡️

  1. Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit named 29th IAF Chief — the Centre on 6 October appointed Vice Chief Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit as the 29th Chief of the Air Staff (in the rank of Air Chief Marshal) from the afternoon of 31 October 2026, succeeding Air Chief Marshal Amar Preet Singh who superannuates that day; his tenure runs to 31 December 2028. Commissioned into the fighter stream in December 1986, Dixit has 3,500+ flying hours on the Mirage-2000, MiG variants, Jaguar, Hawk, Tejas and more; he has served as Chief of Integrated Defence Staff and AOC-in-C, Central Air Command. Honours include the Param Vishisht Seva Medal (2026), AVSM, VM, VSM. 🎓🏭

Sports 🏏

  1. Smriti Mandhana named India women's cricket captain — the BCCI on 6 October appointed Smriti Mandhana as captain of the India women's team (full-time white-ball captain) after Harmanpreet Kaur stepped down across formats earlier the same day. Mandhana (in the team since 2013, Harmanpreet's deputy) led Royal Challengers Bengaluru to WPL titles in 2024 and 2026, was the second-highest run-scorer at the 2025 ODI World Cup (434 runs), and is the first Indian woman to score international hundreds in all three formats. 🎓

Economy & Trade 📊

  1. HAL appoints DRDO's Anindya Biswas as Government Nominee Director — Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd (HAL) on 7 October appointed Anindya Biswas (DS & DG (Aero), DRDO) as Part-time Official Director (Government Nominee Director) with effect from 7 October 2026 (until further orders), replacing Dr K Rajalakshmi Menon whose nomination was withdrawn; the appointment was approved by the HAL Board on the Ministry of Defence's direction. 🏭

Important Days 📅

  • 7 October — World Cotton Day: initiated in 2019 at the WTO on the proposal of the Cotton-4 countries (Benin, Burkina Faso, Chad, Mali); proclaimed by the UN General Assembly in August 2021 (Resolution A/RES/75/318) to be observed every 7 October. Highlights cotton's role in economic development, international trade and poverty alleviation — cotton is grown in 75+ countries and is a key livelihood crop for millions of smallholders.
