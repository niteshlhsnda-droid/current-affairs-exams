🎯 Top stories of the day

Banking & Finance 🏦

  1. NPCI revises UPI MDR framework — 0.4% charge on merchant (P2M) UPI transactions above ₹2,000, capped at ₹300 per transaction. 🏦⭐
  2. Cabinet raises EPFO wage ceiling ₹15,000 → ₹25,000 — EPS pension contribution cap up to ₹2,080/month; effective 17 Sept 2026. 🏦🎓
  3. Moody's raises India's GDP forecast to 7% for FY27 (from 6%); Baa3 rating, stable outlook retained; inflation seen at 4.8%. 🏦🎓⭐
  4. NSE enables India's first tokenised corporate bond issuances worth ₹1,000 crore — capital-market innovation. 🏦
  5. novio & Indian Overseas Bank launch co-branded RuPay credit card at Global Fintech Fest 2026; FD-backed, UPI-linked. 🏦
  6. Jayanti Prasad gets additional charge as IBBI Chairperson (assumed 13 Sept 2026). 🏦
  7. NHAI launches OneTag FASTag bank portability on the Rajmargyatra app — switch issuer banks without changing the RFID tag; 6-month cooling period. 🏦
  8. GIFT City jumps to 37th in the Global Financial Centres Index (GFCI 40). 🏦

Economy & PSU 🏭

  1. Indian Oil approves ₹2,449 cr Kochi–Thoothukudi gas pipeline — 424.65 km natural gas pipeline. 🏭
  2. Mazagon Dock plans ₹27,000 cr shipbuilding cluster at Dighi, Raigad (Maharashtra); 2 million GT capacity, aligned with Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047. 🏭🎓⭐
  3. ONGC discovers natural gas in deep waters of the Mahanadi offshore basin (east coast). 🏭
  4. HAL hands over LCA Tejas FOC trainers, HTT-40 "Ankur" trainers & Dhruv NG helicopters — Dhruv NG is India's first indigenous civil helicopter with AS-4 compliance. 🏭🎓
  5. Adani Power's 3,200 MW ultra-supercritical plant in Dhubri (Assam) — ₹48,000 crore investment; Northeast's largest power asset. 🏦🎓
  6. Asia's first Telecom Manufacturing Zone in Gwalior (MP) — ~₹5,500 crore investment, 18,000+ jobs; DoT–MP MoU signed July 2026. 🏦🎓🏭
  7. India aims to be world's No. 1 silk producer by 2028-29 — output rose 26,000 MT (2014) → 42,000 MT now; Karnataka leads. 🏦🎓

Polity & Governance 🏛️

  1. FCRA (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026 — assets from foreign funds vest in a government-appointed authority if FCRA registration is cancelled/lapses; 22,496 NGO registrations cancelled since 2015. 🎓⭐
  2. Uniform Civil Code debate expands after Uttarakhand's Jan 2025 rollout; Article 44 (DPSP); 21st Law Commission (2018) said UCC "neither necessary nor desirable". 🎓
  3. Registration of Births and Deaths (Amendment) Act, 2026 effective 1 Oct 2026 — stricter delayed-registration norms. 🎓
  4. LPG biometric authentication mandatory from 1 Oct 2026 — 27.43 crore consumers (89.9%) already verified; unverified pay market price. 🎓🏦

Defence 🛡️

  1. IAF to host 2nd edition of Tarang Shakti — multinational air exercise at Jodhpur, 26 Sept–12 Oct 2026. ⭐
  2. Exercise Nomadic Elephant 18th edition begins — India–Mongolia joint military exercise at Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand.
  3. SLINEX-26 — 13th India–Sri Lanka bilateral naval exercise at Visakhapatnam; INS Kavaratti & INS Jyoti participated.
  4. MoD signs ₹586 cr APU contract for indigenously developed Auxiliary Power Units for T-72/T-90 tanks.
  5. India's first indigenous long-range OWA drone 'KAL' tested at Pokhran — 1,000 km range, 7–8 hr endurance, 50 kg payload. 🎓

  2. Delhi tops NITI Aayog's India Electric Mobility Index 2025 with 84 points (Maharashtra 78, Karnataka 73).

  4. WMO State of Global Water Resources 2025 — 34% of groundwater stations below normal; persistent depletion in parts of India (2022–25). 🎓
