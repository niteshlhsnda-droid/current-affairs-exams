🎯 Top stories of the day

Defence 🛡️

  1. Exercise Tarang Shakti 2026 begins today at Jodhpur — the second edition of the IAF's multinational air exercise runs 26 Sept–12 Oct 2026 at Air Force Station Jodhpur; seven foreign air forces take part (US, France, Germany, Australia, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, UAE); the US F-35 fifth-generation fighter will exercise with the IAF for the first time (it had earlier only appeared at Aero India 2025); France brings 7 Rafales plus tankers and A400 transports, the UAE 6 F-16s, Bangladesh a C-130J; the IAF fields Rafale, Su-30MKI, Tejas, Mirage-2000, Jaguar, MiG-29 and 60+ assets; 32 nations attend as observers and ~10 air chiefs will visit; Vice Chief Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit briefed the media. The maiden edition was held in 2024 at Sulur and Jodhpur. 🎓⭐
  2. DRDO inks TDF pact with Zero mK India for 20 mK dilution refrigerator — the first high-value deep-tech agreement under the Technology Development Fund (TDF) scheme: indigenous development of a 20 millikelvin dilution refrigerator for quantum computing, mentored by SSPL Director Dr Meena Mishra; supports the National Quantum Mission by cutting dependence on imported cryogenic infrastructure. TDF was approved in September 2016 under Make in India; the corpus was raised to ₹500 crore. 🎓⭐

Banking & Finance 🏦

  1. PFRDA issues operational guidelines for NPS Swasthya — under Section 14 of the PFRDA Act, 2013, PFRDA notified a new health-linked pension scheme combining retirement savings with healthcare benefits. Family-floater cover for subscriber, spouse and up to two dependent children (parents excluded); entry age 18–70, renewal to 85. Two components: an NPS Swasthya Investment Account and a Super Top-up. Minimum initial contribution is linked to the first-year insurance premium plus ₹200 annual maintenance for the Health Benefit Administrator plus taxes, and at least ₹1,000 to the investment account; minimum subsequent contribution ₹10. Four deductible/cover combos: ₹1 lakh (₹10,000 annual aggregate deductible), ₹5 lakh (₹50,000), ₹10 lakh (₹1 lakh), ₹30 lakh (₹3 lakh). 🏦⭐
  2. RBI alters valuation norms for REITs and InvITs — directions issued 22 Sept 2026, effective immediately, to remove ambiguity and ensure uniform valuation of InvIT/REIT units held by All-India Financial Institutions; apply to commercial banks, AIFIs, small finance banks, payment banks and local area banks. India's REIT market hit USD 17.7 billion, overtaking Hong Kong to become Asia's 4th largest. 🏦🎓
  3. Samvriddhi Inclusive gets RBI in-principle approval to operate as PA-Online — Samvriddhi Inclusive Growth Network Pvt. Ltd. (SIGN, a subsidiary of MOS Utility and a Kolkata-based corporate business correspondent working with PNB, BoB and SBI) will offer online payment aggregation to merchants, focused on small businesses in rural, semi-urban and underserved markets; the platform will support UPI, QR, credit/debit cards and net banking. 🏦
  4. PayGlocal gets IFSCA in-principle approval to set up a PSP in GIFT City — the cross-border payments company (founded 2021, HQ Bengaluru) will set up a Payment Service Provider entity in GIFT City, Gandhinagar, for e-money account issuance, cross-border money transfers and merchant acquisition; it already holds RBI's final authorisation as a Payment Aggregator–Cross Border (inward + outward) and is registered as a Money Services Business with US FinCEN, supporting 130+ countries. 🏦
  5. SBI Card launches FD-backed secured credit cards on YONO — the 'Advantage SBI Card' is fully digital: eligible customers can apply for an FD-backed secured credit card on the YONO app in ~10 minutes. Four variants: SBI Card ELITE, PRIME, SimplyCLICK and SimplySAVE; the FD acts as collateral and helps build a credit profile. Inaugurated by SBI Chairman Challa Sreenivasulu Setty with MD Ashwini Kumar Tewari and SBI Card MD & CEO Salila Pande. 🏦

Economy 🏦🎓

  1. ADB raises India's FY27 GDP growth forecast to 7% — in the Asian Development Outlook (September 2026), ADB raised its FY27 forecast by 40 bps from 6.6% to 7%, citing stronger-than-expected Q1 FY27 growth, robust investment, resilient consumption and solid manufacturing and services growth despite the West Asia crisis; FY28 projected at 7.1% (from 7.3%); inflation seen at 5.0% in FY27 (from 5.2%). This puts ADB alongside Fitch (6.9%), S&P, the OECD and others who raised India's FY27 forecasts to 6.9%–7.1% in September 2026. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. India's FY 2025-26 FDI inflow hits record USD 94.53 billion — Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal announced on 25 Sept that India recorded its highest-ever FDI inflow in FY 2025-26; cumulative FDI for FY 2014-15 to FY 2025-26 stood at USD 843 billion. 🏦

Schemes & Governance 🏛️

  1. NSC approves ₹320 crore SIP for Kurukshetra ITI Cluster under PM-SETU — the National Steering Committee (MSDE) cleared the Strategic Investment Plan for the hub-and-spoke Kurukshetra ITI Cluster (7 government ITIs, Haryana) with Jindal Naveen Avsar Ltd as anchor industry partner for training, apprenticeships and employment; cumulative PM-SETU investment reaches ₹2,491 crore across 10 ITI clusters (a ₹240 crore Barbil, Odisha, cluster was also approved with the same anchor partner). 🎓🏭
  2. UP CM launches 'Bharat Taxi' and 'Mukhyamantri Krishak Samriddhi Yojana' — Bharat Taxi is a cooperative-based, zero-commission taxi service for two-wheeler, taxi and three-wheeler drivers with ₹5 lakh social security cover, starting in Lucknow, Gorakhpur, Varanasi and Prayagraj; MoUs signed with AAI, Lucknow Metro Rail and Lucknow Police. The Krishak Samriddhi Yojana cuts farm loan interest from 11% to 6% via the UP Cooperative Gramya Vikas Bank, including long-term loans for agriculture and farm machinery, targeting 5 lakh small and marginal farmers. 🎓
  3. ICAI issues SSA 5000 on sustainability assurance — the Standard on Sustainability Assurance (SSA) 5000 — General Requirements & Framework for Sustainability Assurance Engagements — is a principle-based framework covering climate, labour practices and biodiversity, aligned with IAASB's ISSA 5000, with Indian carve-outs for joint audits and forward-looking statements; effective 1 April 2027, when SSAE 3000 and SAE 3410 will be withdrawn. ICAI was established on 1 July 1949, is HQ'd in New Delhi and headed by President CA Prasanna Kumar D. 🏦🎓

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. ISRO–ESA extend cooperation agreement to 8 January 2032 — through letters exchanged in Paris during the International Space Summit, the pact (in force since January 2002) is extended by 5 years and broadened to human and robotic exploration, space weather and sustainable outer-space activities, building on the 2025 Joint Statement of Intent on human exploration and the March 2026 Earth-observation agreement; signed by ISRO Chairman Dr V. Narayanan and ESA Director General Dr Josef Aschbacher. 🎓⭐
  2. IIEST Shibpur and TCS launch Centre of Excellence in AI — an MoU sets up the AI CoE at the HPC Lab of IIEST Shibpur's Electrical Engineering Department (West Bengal), with TCS as knowledge partner; focus on NLP, computer vision, image processing and data analytics, plus internships, innovation and AI startups, aligned with Digital India and Viksit Bharat 2047. TCS was established in 1968, is HQ'd in Mumbai and is headed by CEO & MD K. Krithivasan. 🎓
  3. IEA: India's electrification rate could rise from 19% to 32% by 2035 — the IEA's Electrification Special Report 2026 (prepared at the request of Türkiye and Australia for the COP31 strategic partnership) projects transport electrification and EV adoption could save India nearly 30% of its 2025 energy import bill; accelerated electrification could cut global energy import bills by over USD 400 billion by 2035; Southeast Asia could rise from 25% to 37%. 🎓🏦
  4. OAG Megahubs 2026: Delhi Airport rises to 28th globally — Delhi's IGIA jumped 11 places (39th → 28th) among the world's most-connected international airports; Istanbul (337 global destinations) is the most connected, followed by London Heathrow and Amsterdam Schiphol; Asia-Pacific holds 8 of the top 20; Kuala Lumpur topped the low-cost megahubs index, followed by Incheon and Istanbul Sabiha Gokcen. 🎓

Polity 🏛️

  1. JPC begins scrutiny of the FCRA Amendment Bill, 2026 — the Joint Parliamentary Committee held its first meeting on 18 Sept 2026; the Bill's key provision makes all unutilised foreign contributions and assets created from them vest provisionally in a government-appointed Designated Authority when an FCRA certificate is cancelled, surrendered or lapses — permanent vesting if registration is not restored within the prescribed window, with proceeds from sale/transfer credited to the Consolidated Fund of India; appeals lie to the District Judge within 90 days, and the Bill adds deemed cessation and rationalises penalties (max jail term cut from 5 years to 1 year for specific violations). MHA told the JPC that 22,496 FCRA registrations have been cancelled since 2015, leaving 14,466 active, while foreign receipts rose to ₹22,974 crore in 2024-25; Opposition members invoked Article 300A against the asset-takeover clause. 🎓⭐

  2. India, France and UAE hold high-level talks — cooperation on AI, space, energy and cultural exchanges. 🎓

Environment 🌱

  1. New plant species 'Begonia neelamorum' discovered in Arunachal Pradesh — found along the Keyi River in Keyi Panyor district at ~1,000m elevation by Dipankar Borah, Madhusudhan Khanal, Momang Taram and Neelam Gap; published in the journal Taiwania. The 10–20 cm tuberous plant (single sessile light-green leaf, white male and female flowers) is named after the Neelam clan of the Nyishi community (locally Kobiirhopu Baabrai), provisionally classified as Data Deficient by the IUCN; it raises the Begonia count in Northeast India to 54 of ~2,200 globally. 🎓
