📰 Current Affairs — 28 September 2026

🎯 Top stories of the day

Economy & Banking 🏦🎓

  1. India's first blue bond launches today: ₹600 crore by Sagarmala Finance Corporation — the state-owned maritime lender is scheduled to launch India's first-ever blue bond issue on 28 Sept 2026, with a proposed issue size of ₹600 crore. Blue bonds are debt instruments earmarked for sustainable ocean and marine-economy projects, paralleling the sovereign green bond. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. Power Ministry invokes Section 11, orders captive coal plants to run at full capacity — on 27 Sept 2026 the Ministry of Power invoked Section 11 of the Electricity Act (emergency direction power) and directed all captive coal-based generating stations of 50 MW and above to maximise generation from 1 October to 31 December 2026, citing the demand-supply situation and anticipated demand rise. Plants must offer surplus power on power exchanges after meeting captive needs and maintain adequate coal stocks. 🎓🏭⭐
  3. UFBU 3-day bank strike begins today; banks opened on Sunday to cushion it — the United Forum of Bank Unions' nationwide three-day strike (28–30 Sept 2026) begins today (reported in the 24 Sept edition — demands include a 5-day week, pension updation and an NPS-to-OPS switch). To prevent a five-day branch disruption, the government ordered all public-sector banks and regional rural banks to function normally on Sunday, 27 September, with RBI approval for opening branches, offices and currency chests that day; digital channels (UPI, mobile, internet banking) remain functional. 🏦🎓
  4. Economists raise odds of RBI rate hike at the October MPC meeting — on 28 Sept, HSBC's Pranjul Bhandari maintained a call of two 25 bps hikes (October and December), taking the repo rate to 5.75%, as the RBI drains excess liquidity. DBS's Radhika Rao noted system liquidity tightened to below ₹5 lakh crore from a ₹10–11 lakh crore peak, using VRRR auctions, OMO sales and short-tenor sell-buy swaps; USD/INR ended the week around the mid-95 handle (95.7325 on 23 Sept). 🏦🎓⭐

International 🌍

  1. FM Sitharaman in Doha for 11th AIIB Board of Governors meeting — Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman began a three-day official visit to Qatar (27–29 Sept) and, as India's Governor to the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank, attended the 11th Annual Meeting of the AIIB Board of Governors in Doha on 28 Sept. She held bilaterals with the AIIB President and counterparts from Jordan, Switzerland, Algeria, Saudi Arabia and Nepal. India is the second-largest shareholder of the AIIB. 🎓🏦⭐

Governance & Polity 🏛️

  1. President returns West Bengal 'Anti-Goonda Bill' for reconsideration — the President returned the West Bengal Public Safety and Control of Anti-Social Activities Bill, 2026 for reconsideration over potential overlap with Central preventive-detention legislation. The Bill had been passed by the West Bengal Legislative Assembly on 29 June 2026 — an exam-ready case of Presidential action on a state Bill under Article 201, and of the preventive-detention vs civil-liberties debate. 🎓⭐
  2. ₹11,186 crore released under PM-AJAY since FY 2014-15 — the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment reported that over ₹11,186 crore has been released under the three components of the Pradhan Mantri Anusuchit Jaati Abhyuday Yojana (PM-AJAY) since FY 2014-15. PM-AJAY is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme for the development of Scheduled Castes (classic Central Sector vs Centrally Sponsored classification question). 🎓

Defence & Foreign Policy 🛡️

  1. Rajnath Singh: India is a 'Civilisational Power', neither status-quoist nor revisionist — on 27 Sept 2026 Union Defence Minister Rajnath Singh posted on X: "Bharat na Status Quoist Power hai, na Revisionist Power, balki Bharat ek Civilisational Power hai" — India is neither a status-quoist nor a revisionist power, but a civilisational power — adding that India believes in peace and global cooperation but will make no compromise on national interest and strategic autonomy. A quotable framing for GS-2 Mains answers. 🎓

Environment & Health 🔬🌱

  1. Planetary Health Check 2026: seven of nine planetary boundaries breached — the Planetary Health Check 2026 found that 7 of the 9 planetary boundaries — the science-based limits defining a safe operating space for humanity (climate, biosphere integrity, land, freshwater, nutrient flows, ocean acidification, atmospheric aerosols, stratospheric ozone, novel entities) — have now crossed safe limits, and all seven show worsening trends. A fresh number to quote in GS-3 environment answers. 🎓⭐
  2. World Rabies Day 2026 — 28 September, theme "Stronger Together" — the 20th annual World Rabies Day (coordinated by the Global Alliance for Rabies Control, GARC, with WHO support) carries the 2026 theme "Stronger Together", stressing that dog-mediated rabies elimination needs collaboration across human and animal health systems. Rabies causes an estimated 59,000 human deaths globally per year, is ~99.9% fatal once symptoms appear, yet is fully preventable through dog vaccination and timely post-exposure prophylaxis. 🎓⭐

Asian Games 2026: Day 8 recap; Day 10 finals tonight — Day 8 results (27 Sept) were covered in the evening update of the 27 Sept edition (7 medals, tally 37 = 4 gold, 16 silver, 17 bronze, India 12th). On 28 Sept, PM Narendra Modi congratulated the Day 8 medal winners, including Anahat Singh — the first Indian woman to reach an Asian Games squash singles final. Day 10 finals scheduled for this evening IST include the men's cricket final vs Afghanistan, Avinash Sable's 3000m steeplechase and the men's javelin — results will appear in the 29 Sept edition. 🎓

🌙 Evening updates (28 Sept)

Infrastructure & Economy 🏦🎓

  1. Government notifies India's first four "mega ports" — the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways notified Jawaharlal Nehru Port (Nhava Sheva), Paradip, Deendayal (Kandla) and Adani's Mundra as India's first "mega ports" under Section 73 of the Indian Ports Act, 2025 (reported 28 Sept). Status is valid for 5 years, subject to periodic review; the qualifying threshold is 150 million metric tonnes annual throughput for a bulk port or 7.5 million TEUs for a container port. Mundra is the only privately owned port on the list. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. OECD raises India's FY27 growth forecast to 7.1% — the OECD lifted its India GDP growth projection for FY2026-27 to 7.1% (reported 28 Sept). 🏦🎓⭐
  3. India's first LNG-diesel dual-fuel train flagged off at Sabarmati — Union Home Minister Amit Shah flagged off the country's first LNG-diesel dual-fuel DEMU on 27 Sept 2026 from Sabarmati, Gujarat (eight-coach non-AC chair car, inaugural run Sabarmati–Mahesana). Two 1,400 HP Driving Power Cars were converted at the Sabarmati Integrated Coaching Depot; LNG substitutes ~40% of diesel, with estimated annual savings of ₹23.9 lakh per rake. Railways' second alternative-fuel milestone after the first hydrogen train (Jind–Sonipat, 17 July 2026). 🎓🏭⭐

Defence & Science 🛡️🔬

  1. Tarang Shakti 2026: IAF hosts 40-nation air exercise at Jodhpur — the Indian Air Force opened the second edition of the multinational air combat exercise Tarang Shakti 2026 in Jodhpur, Rajasthan, with about 40 participating nations (reported 28 Sept). 🎓🏭⭐
  2. IIT Delhi builds India's first indigenous working micro-GPU — IIT Delhi developed the country's first indigenous working micro graphics processing unit (reported 28 Sept). 🎓⭐

Appointments & Schemes 🏛️

  1. Mandeep K. Bhandari appointed CBSE Chairperson — the Appointments Committee of the Cabinet cleared the appointment of 2001-batch AGMUT-cadre IAS officer Mandeep K. Bhandari (currently Principal Secretary to the Lt. Governor of J&K; MBBS graduate) as Chairperson of the Central Board of Secondary Education on 25 Sept 2026, replacing Lokhande Prashant Sitaram, who held additional charge since 2 June. 🏛️🎓⭐
  2. Livestock Insurance Portal launched — the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying launched the Livestock Insurance Portal in New Delhi on 28 Sept 2026, unveiled by Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh (Lalan Singh) — a single digital platform for livestock insurance. 🎓
  3. Make in India completes 12 years — DPIIT marked the 12th anniversary of Make in India on 25 Sept 2026 (launched 25 September 2014; guiding principle "Minimum Government, Maximum Governance"; covers 27 sectors — 15 manufacturing, 12 services). 🎓
  4. Tamil Nadu's 'Maternal Uncle Gold Ring' scheme — CM C. Joseph Vijay announced the Thaaimaman Thanga Mothiram Thittam in Madurai on 28 Sept 2026: newborns in government hospitals get a 22-carat, 1-gram gold ring. 🎓

Sports 🏆

  1. Asian Games Day 9 (28 Sept): India's tally rises to 39 — Day 9 medals: Esha Singh (silver, women's 25m pistol — world No. 1, missed gold by one shot after five shoot-offs; Manu Bhaker finished 11th in qualification and missed the final), Pincky Balhara (bronze, kurash women's −78kg — her second Asian Games medal after 2018 silver), Rohit Yadav (silver) and Yash Vir Singh (bronze) in men's javelin, Murali Sreeshankar (silver, men's long jump), Gulveer Singh (silver, men's 1500m), Vithya Ramaraj (bronze, women's 400m hurdles) and Parul Chaudhary (bronze, women's 5000m). In squash singles, Anahat Singh and Abhay Singh settled for silver after losing their finals to Malaysians Sivasangari Subramaniam (4-11, 4-11, 7-11) and Ng Eain Yow (9-11, 5-11, 5-11); Anahat became the first Indian woman to reach an Asian Games squash singles final, but both missed the LA 2028 Olympic quota reserved for the singles champions. 🎓
  3. Men's cricket: rain hands India the semi-final; women retain gold — India's men's quarter-final against Afghanistan on 28 Sept was abandoned without a ball bowled due to rain at Korogi Sports Park, Nisshin, so India advanced to the semi-final as the higher-ranked side and will face the Sri Lanka–Nepal QF winner (team led by T20 captain Shreyas Iyer, with VVS Laxman as stand-in head coach). The women's team has already defended its Asian Games title, beating Sri Lanka by 147 runs in the final under Harmanpreet Kaur. 🎓⭐
