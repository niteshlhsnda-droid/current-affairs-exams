🎯 Top stories of the day

Nobel Prizes 🌍🔬

  1. Nobel Peace Prize 2026 announced TODAY (9 Oct, ~2:30 PM IST) — the Norwegian Nobel Committee names this year's Peace laureate at the Norwegian Nobel Institute, Oslo, with the prize ceremony to follow on 10 December at Oslo City Hall. The prize carries a gold medal, diploma and 12 million Swedish kronor (~US$1.2 million). The Norwegian Nobel Institute clarified the prize cannot be transferred or shared — a note aimed at US President Donald Trump, who has publicly lobbied for it, claiming he "settled eight wars" and criticising Barack Obama's 2009 award. Already announced this week: Medicine (5 Oct) — Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel (optogenetics / light-gated ion channels); Physics (6 Oct) — Francis Halzen (IceCube Neutrino Observatory, high-energy astrophysical neutrinos); Chemistry (7 Oct) — Henri B. Kagan (France) and Kenso Soai (Japan) (non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis, announced by the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences); Literature (8 Oct) — Anne Carson (Canada) (Swedish Academy). Still to come: Economic Sciences (12 Oct). 🎓⭐

Economy & Trade 📊

  1. World Bank raises India's FY27 GDP forecast to 7.1% — in its India Development Update / October South Asia Economic Update, the World Bank upgraded India's FY27 growth projection from 6.6% (April) to 7.1%, citing a better-than-expected Q1 print of 7.8% and stronger private consumption and investment; FY26 closed at 7.8%. Flagged risks: oil prices, El Niño and capital-flow volatility. South Asia projected at 6.9% in 2026. The report's special focus is India's AI opportunity — private AI investment rose from US$1.2 billion (2024) to US$4.1 billion (2025). 🎓🏦⭐

Governance & Polity 🎓

  1. PM Modi completes 25 years as head of government — on 7 October 2026, Narendra Modi completed 25 years at the head of government, dating from his swearing-in as Gujarat Chief Minister on 7 October 2001. He addressed a gathering at Delhi's Indira Gandhi Indoor Stadium, thanked the people and reiterated the 2047 developed-India goal. The BJP is observing a month-long Seva Sankalp Abhiyan, and the Union Cabinet adopted a formal resolution with a standing ovation. 🎓⭐
  1. INDIA bloc march against SIR, demand CEC's resignation — opposition MPs led by Mallikarjun Kharge and Rahul Gandhi marched toward the Election Commission on 6 October, demanding cancellation of the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls and the resignation of CEC Gyanesh Kumar (along with PM Modi and Amit Shah). Congress MP Dharamveer Gandhi alleged roughly 13 crore voter deletions — the EC says deletions target dead, duplicate and ineligible voters; the matter is before the Supreme Court. 🎓
  1. SC to examine recall plea on CEC-law split verdict — CJI Surya Kant (with Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V Mohana) agreed on 7 October to examine an interlocutory application seeking recall of the 23 September split verdict (Justices Dipankar Datta vs Satish Chandra Sharma) on the 2023 CEC appointments law, over apprehension of bias — Justice Sharma's son is standing counsel for the ECI before the MP High Court; a curative petition (by Jaya Thakur) is also filed. Section 7 of the 2023 Act puts CEC/EC appointment on the recommendation of a committee comprising the PM, the LoP (Lok Sabha) and a Cabinet minister. 🎓

Defence & Security 🛡️

  1. 94th Indian Air Force Day observed (8 Oct) — IAF Day marked 94 years since the force's establishment on 8 October 1932; the main parade and flypast were held at Air Force Station Hindan (Ghaziabad), reviewed by ACM A.P. Singh. Theme: "Indian Air Force – Infallible, Impervious and Precise". Rafale, Su-30MKI, MiG-29, Tejas, C-17 and Apache featured in the flypast; Mi-17s carried the National Flag, the IAF flag and the Operation Sindoor flag. 🎓🏭⭐

🌆 Evening updates

Economy & Trade 📊

  1. RBI hikes repo rate 25 bps to 5.50%, stance shifts to 'calibrated tightening' — the MPC (met 5–7 Oct, Mumbai) voted unanimously on 7 October to raise the repo rate under LAF to 5.50% — the first hike since February 2023 — citing the West Asia conflict, crude above US$100/barrel and August CPI at 4.8%. The stance shifted from "neutral" to "calibrated tightening"; SDF adjusted to 5.25%, MSF and Bank Rate to 5.75%. FY27 GDP forecast raised to 7.1% (from 6.7%); CPI inflation projected at 5.2%. Governor Sanjay Malhotra: rate cuts are off the table in the near term; next MPC meeting 2–4 December. 🎓🏦⭐
  1. Cabinet clears ITLA — apex Integrated Transport & Logistics Authority — the Union Cabinet (chaired by PM Modi, 6 Oct) approved setting up the Integrated Transport & Logistics Authority (ITLA) as a Special Purpose Vehicle — the national apex body for integrated transport and logistics planning, research, project appraisal, monitoring and data analytics; announced by Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw. ITLA will prepare a 10+ year National Transport Master Plan (roads, railways, ports, aviation, inland waterways, coastal shipping, urban mobility, logistics), complementing PM GatiShakti and the National Logistics Policy (2022). 🎓🏦⭐
  1. Cabinet approves Rs 10,000 crore SME Growth Fund — in the same Cabinet meeting (6 Oct), the government approved a Rs 10,000-crore Small and Medium Enterprises Growth Fund for long-term equity investment in SMEs — directed largely at high-potential manufacturing SMEs, plus services, technology and innovation-driven sectors; announced by Ashwini Vaishnaw. 🏦🎓⭐

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. PM Modi inaugurates 10th India Mobile Congress (8 Oct) — Modi inaugurated IMC 2026 at Yashobhoomi (India International Convention & Expo Centre), New Delhi; the 10th edition runs 7–10 October with the theme "Scale Without Boundaries", jointly organised by DoT and COAI, covering 5G, 6G, AI, semiconductors, cybersecurity, quantum communication and satellite networks. India now has 5G in every district with 5.5+ lakh base stations; mobile data cost has fallen from ~Rs 268/GB (2014) to ~Rs 8 (2026). 🎓🏭⭐
  1. MoSPI releases Environmental Economic Accounts Strategy 2026–2030 — the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation released the five-year strategy to measure the economic value of natural assets and ecosystem services alongside GDP, succeeding the 2022–2026 strategy. About 10 priority areas: forests, water, land, soil, minerals, carbon stocks, biodiversity, pollination services, environment economic activities and environment statistics — aligned with the UN System of Environmental-Economic Accounting (SEEA) and the 2025 System of National Accounts; India's environmental accounts support around 40 international indicators across 9 SDGs. 🎓🏦⭐

Environment 🌱

  1. Ladakh launches India's first high-altitude snow leopard safari — during Wildlife Week 2026, LG Vinai Kumar Saxena flagged off six specially designed open-back vehicles for the Ladakh Snow Leopard Safari at Choglamsar, Leh (7 Oct) — run by the SHAN Society with UT Ladakh's Forest, Ecology & Environment Department; initially in Rumbak, Shang and Uley, later expanding across Hemis, Changthang and Karakoram wildlife areas. Ladakh holds 477 of India's estimated 718 snow leopards; an AI-based human-wildlife conflict mitigation device (first of its kind in Ladakh) was also inaugurated. 🎓⭐

Sports 🏅

  1. Shreyas Iyer's 102* powers India past West Indies in 1st T20 — captain Shreyas Iyer hit an unbeaten 102 off 43 balls (10 fours, 6 sixes) as India beat West Indies by 8 wickets in Lucknow on 6 October, chasing 172 with 32 balls to spare in the first of five T20s; Ishan Kishan shared a 137-run partnership; Arshdeep Singh and Axar Patel struck early. 🏦🎓
  1. ACC announces U19 Asia Cup 2026 schedule — the Asian Cricket Council announced the U19 Asia Cup 2026 in Oman (5–14 November, 15 matches, 50-over format) at Muscat's Oman Cricket Academy Grounds; India in Group A with Pakistan, UAE and Nepal; India vs Pakistan on 8 November; opener Sri Lanka vs Malaysia on 5 November; semifinals 12 November, final 14 November. 🎓
