🎯 Top stories of the day

Banking & Markets 🏦🎓

  1. SEBI board approves PMS overhaul and settlement-rules revamp — in September 2026 the SEBI board approved a comprehensive revamp of the Portfolio Managers Regulations: PMS providers may now invest client funds in direct mutual-fund schemes and Specialised Investment Funds (SIFs) through PRIM, with a ₹25 lakh minimum ticket size, and up to 10% of client AUM in investment-grade unlisted debt under discretionary PMS with client consent, plus access to IPOs, primary debt issuances and exchange-traded derivatives. It also overhauled the settlement framework — a formula-based method for settlement amounts, wider access to settlement proceedings, a fast-track mechanism for disclosure-related violations up to ₹10 lakh, the application window extended from 60 to 90 days, and a separate 90-day one-time settlement window for specified pending cases. Other approvals: FPIs get access to exchange-traded commodity derivatives, Vault Manager rules expanded to bullion-linked ETFs with minimum net worth raised to ₹75 crore, eased retrospective listing norms for unlisted NCDs, and a 4th settlement scheme for illiquid stock-option cases. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. Net FDI hits five-year high of $7.35 billion in July 2026 — RBI data released in September 2026 (monthly bulletin) shows net Foreign Direct Investment rose to USD 7.35 billion in July 2026, the highest since May 2021's $8.80 billion and up 64% YoY. Gross inflows stood at $14.58 billion (third-highest in six years); communication, financial and computer services drew over 60% of equity inflows. For April–July 2026, net FDI reached $13.4 billion vs $9.7 billion a year earlier (+38%); gross inflows grew 12.6% from $38.9B to $43.9B. 🏦🎓⭐
  3. IndusInd Bank launches 'GCC Banking Vertical' — India's private-sector lender launched a dedicated Global Capability Centre (GCC) banking vertical integrating five core capabilities under one relationship: digital banking, employee banking, commercial card solutions, capital-account & FEMA solutions, and foreign-currency accounts via its International Banking Unit at GIFT City, Gandhinagar. Context: India's GCC ecosystem has grown 32% since FY21 to 2,117 GCCs. IndusInd Bank, established 1994, is headquartered in Mumbai. 🏦🎓

SISC will be chaired by Sandeep Poundrik, Secretary, Ministry of Steel, with CEOs of steel companies and representatives from DGMS, PESO, NDMA and technical institutions; the Steel Industry Safety Directorate (SISD) will provide technical support, audits and incident investigations. 🏭🎓

Registration runs 25 September–24 October 2026; the national finale is in New Delhi in December 2026. Organised by MoE, AICTE and UGC with IISc as nodal institute; three levels (Junior: classes 6–12; Technical: ITI/polytechnic; Open: UG/PG/research scholars); covers five states (Karnataka, TN, Kerala, AP, Telangana) + three UTs (A&N Islands, Lakshadweep, Puducherry). 🎓

Delhi's 'Udyog Mitra' single-window portal launched — Chief Minister Rekha Gupta launched the 'Delhi Udyog Mitra' portal under the Ease of Doing Business initiative — a single-window platform for licences and NOCs, integrating 50+ services from 15 departments (to expand to 100+ services, 21 departments) with SSO, Common Application Form and 'Know Your Approvals', linked to the National Single Window System.   4. UN adopts its first-ever Declaration on Sea-Level Rise — adopted by acclamation on 24 September at a high-level meeting of the 81st UN General Assembly session — the first common text of all member states (including the US) on the issue. It affirms that countries threatened by rising seas retain their statehood, sovereignty, maritime zones (under UNCLOS) and UN membership even if territory is submerged, and calls for stable maritime baselines, adaptation finance and early warning systems. Context: 770 million people live less than 5m above the high-tide line; global sea level rose at 4.7 mm/yr in 2015–24 (vs 2.1 mm/yr in 1993–2002). 🎓⭐
  5. 11th BIMSTEC JWG-CTTC meets in New Delhi (28–29 Sept) — India hosted the 11th meeting of the BIMSTEC Joint Working Group on Counter Terrorism and Transnational Crime with delegations from all seven member states (Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand), chaired by Suhel Ajaz Khan, Additional Secretary (Counter Terrorism), MEA. The session reviewed the five sub-groups — human trafficking; illicit drugs/psychotropic substances/precursors; anti-money laundering & CFT; legal & law enforcement; countering radicalisation — and followed the July meeting of BIMSTEC National Security Chiefs. 🎓
  6. INS Tarkash arrives at Mombasa, Kenya — the Indian Navy's frontline guided-missile frigate arrived at Mombasa Port on 29 September for an Operational Turnaround (29 Sep–2 Oct) with professional exchanges, crew interactions and community outreach; she is open to visitors on 1 October (11 AM–4 PM) and will do a Passage Exercise with the Kenya Navy on departure — advancing India–Kenya maritime cooperation in the Western Indian Ocean under India's MAHASAGAR vision. 🛡️🎓
  7. Centre sanctions ₹1,200+ crore PM-RKVY support to 10 states — on day 2 of the National Agriculture Conference – Rabi Campaign 2026, Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan handed over sanction letters under the Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana: West Bengal ₹290 cr, Haryana ₹198 cr, Chhattisgarh ₹181 cr, Arunachal Pradesh ₹154 cr, Bihar ₹114 cr, Karnataka ₹104 cr, Mizoram ₹75 cr, Himachal Pradesh ₹64 cr, Jharkhand ₹58 cr, Punjab ₹43 cr — calling for a "Team Agriculture" Centre–states partnership. He noted farm growth has risen from 3% (2015) to 4.5%, and rabi foodgrain output rose by 205 lakh tonnes between 2023-24 and 2025-26. 🎓🏦
  8. Kerala's IAV builds AI-based Dengue Early Warning System (DEWS) — scientists at the Institute of Advanced Virology, Thiruvananthapuram (model by Abhinand C.S., Dept of Virus Genomics, Bioinformatics & Statistics) developed a machine-learning model trained on six years of data (March 2020–Feb 2026) combining dengue case counts from the State Surveillance Unit with IMD rainfall/temperature/humidity data to generate weekly district-level forecasts classified into four risk levels (very high / high / moderate / low) — still a research system, live at dews.iav.res.in. 🎓⭐
