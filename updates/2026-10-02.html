📰 Current Affairs — 2 October 2026

🎯 Top stories of the day

Gandhi Jayanti special 🎓

  1. 157th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi — the nation observes Gandhi Jayanti today, 2 October; it is also the birth anniversary of Lal Bahadur Shastri (of the 'Jai Jawan, Jai Kisan' call). The UN observes the day globally as the International Day of Non-Violence (UNGA Resolution 61/271, 2007). In the 138th episode of Mann Ki Baat, PM Modi called cleanliness the "most beautiful tribute" to Gandhi, recalling Bapu's mantra that cleanliness is one of his life's teachings. 🎓⭐
  2. Swachhata Hi Seva 2026 concludes; Swachh Bharat Diwas — the nationwide Swachhata Hi Seva 2026 campaign (17 September–2 October, theme *"Swachhata Mein Sahbhag; Swachh Bharat, Viksit Bharat"*) culminates today, observed as Swachh Bharat Diwas; special Gram Sabhas and urban council meetings are being held to convert campaign actions into continuing local effort. Separately, the government's Special Campaign 6.0 for office cleanliness runs 2–31 October. 🎓⭐
  3. National Wildlife Week begins — observed every year from 2 to 8 October; programmes, nature walks, competitions and biodiversity-awareness activities will run across the country. 🎓

Governance & Policy 🎓

  1. ECI extends SIR dates for Andhra Pradesh and Meghalaya — the Election Commission met for the second time in four days and extended the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls for Andhra Pradesh and Meghalaya. 🎓⭐
  2. Oct 2 protest programmes announced — several separate programmes were announced for Gandhi Jayanti: a CJP 'Jail Bharo Andolan' in Mumbai (after police denied permission for a Shivaji Park gathering), an AISA-linked protest at Jantar Mantar, New Delhi (11 am), and INDIA Bloc programmes — all centred on the Election Commission's functioning, electoral rolls and the SIR, with some groups demanding the CEC's removal. 🎓
  3. SC dismisses plea to operate frozen accounts — the Supreme Court dismissed a plea by a TMC faction seeking permission to operate four debit-frozen bank accounts. 🎓
  4. Mann Ki Baat marks 10 years of the 2016 surgical strikes — in the same 138th episode, PM Modi marked the tenth anniversary of the Indian Army's 2016 surgical strikes across the Line of Control, calling it a demonstration of India's resolve against terrorism. 🎓

Banking & Markets 🏦🎓

  1. RBI bulk-deposit disclosure norm kicks in (1 Oct) — the RBI's amended Directions on Interest Rates on Deposits are in force: banks must publish interest rates for bulk deposits of ₹3 crore and above on their websites every working day between 10:00 and 10:10 am, and must offer uniform rates for similar deposits booked the same day across all branches. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. RBI bars sale-back of seized assets to defaulters — new directions effective 1 October tighten norms for stressed accounts: defaulting borrowers cannot repurchase seized properties; banks must dispose of such assets through public auctions within seven years and disclose them separately, outside NPA ratios. 🏦🎓
  3. International card payments hit a reporting grey area — new RBI trade-payment rules in force from 1 October mandate reporting of international credit-card transactions — the first time services trades are captured in RBI's digital reporting platforms (earlier only goods via customs and software data). No reporting mechanism has been specified yet for individuals' personal card payments abroad or corporate-card use, all categorised as 'import' under the rules. 🏦
  4. India's first Blue Financing deal: JNPA–IFC, USD 70 million — the Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority and the International Finance Corporation signed a USD 70 million deal for Shore Power Supply infrastructure — India's first Blue Financing arrangement. 🏦🏭🎓⭐
  5. Goyal–USTR talks on India–US trade deal — Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal reported a "productive discussion" with US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer on the early conclusion of the India–US trade deal. 🏦🎓

Foreign Policy & World 🌍

  1. Captain Smit Machchhar hailed as hero — on 30 September, a co-pilot allegedly stabbed Indian pilot Captain Smit Machchhar in a bid to crash flydubai flight FZ1073 (Dubai–Tel Aviv, Boeing 737 MAX 8, 174 passengers including 27 children); the injured captain activated the cockpit door release so crew and passengers could overpower the attacker, and the flight made an emergency landing at Tabuk, Saudi Arabia. On 1 October, President Droupadi Murmu and PM Modi hailed his "heroic courage", Israeli PM Benjamin Netanyahu credited him with averting a catastrophe, and Gujarat announced the 'Visisht Gujarat Garima Award' for him. He has been airlifted from Saudi Arabia to Dubai for treatment. 🎓⭐
  2. Trump renames 'Artificial Intelligence' to 'Super Intelligence' in executive order — US President Donald Trump formally replaced the term in a federal executive order. 🎓
  3. UK PM: 'strong indications' Iran involved in airbase incident — the UK Prime Minister said there are strong indications of Iranian involvement in an airbase incident, amid wider West Asia tensions. 🎓

Sports 🏆

  1. Asian Games Day 14 (2 Oct): India at 66 medals, 8th — India's tally stands at 10 gold, 23 silver, 33 bronze (66 total); China lead the table (154 gold). The 20th Asian Games at Aichi-Nagoya, Japan run 19 September–4 October; the Asian Para Games follow there 18–24 October — India's 5th appearance. 🏆🎓⭐
  2. Men's hockey: India beat Pakistan 4-3 to reach the final — a thriller booked India's place in the Asian Games men's hockey final. 🏆🎓⭐
  3. Men's cricket into the final — Ishan Kishan's unbeaten 80 on a tough pitch carried India through the semifinal into the Asian Games men's T20 cricket final. 🏆🎓
  4. Wrestler Nitesh wins Greco-Roman silver — India's wait for an Asian Games Greco-Roman champion continues; Nitesh took silver. 🏆🎓
  5. RRB ALP CBT 2 Result 2026 declared — zone-wise merit list and scorecard for the Railway Assistant Loco Pilot recruitment released. 🎓🏭
