🎯 Top stories of the day

Asian Games — Day 15 (3 Oct) 🏆

  1. Asiad enters its penultimate day — India sits 5th with 76 medals (16 gold, 25 silver, 35 bronze) after a six-gold surge on 2 October, edging ahead of Uzbekistan on total medals. Today (3 October) brings two blockbuster finals: men's T20 cricket, India vs Pakistan (10:00 am IST) and men's hockey, India vs Malaysia (3:30 pm IST) — the hockey final also carries a direct LA 2028 Olympic berth — plus medal action in archery, wrestling and golf. 🏆🎓⭐
  2. Parinaaz Dhaliwal's skeet double bronze — the skeet shooter took two bronzes: the women's skeet team bronze (score 331) with Raiza Dhillon and Maheshwari Chauhan — India's first-ever women's skeet team medal at the Asian Games — and the mixed team skeet bronze with Anantjeet Singh Naruka (44 hits across six series; tied with South Korea but took bronze on qualification-ranking tie-break). 🏆🎓⭐
  3. Shooting campaign closes with 15 medals — India's shooters finished with 3 gold, 8 silver and 4 bronze, below the record 22 of Hangzhou; trap shooter Neeru Dhanda was the standout (2 gold, 1 silver). In compound archery, Chikitha Taniparthi lost the women's individual quarterfinal 143-144; India lost the men's volleyball quarterfinal 0-3 to Pakistan, ending medal hopes in the event for the first time since 1986. 🏆

Governance & Policy 🎓

  1. Gujarat's UCC gets Presidential assent — President Droupadi Murmu gave assent to the Gujarat Uniform Civil Code, 2026, notified as Gujarat Act No. 15 of 2026 (Gazette Extraordinary published 1 October; assent given 17 September). Gujarat becomes the second state after Uttarakhand to enact a UCC. Key points: common law on marriage, divorce, succession and live-in relationships; marriage registration mandatory within 60 days (fine up to ₹10,000); live-in relationships must be registered (up to ₹10,000 fine or 3-month jail for non-registration); equal inheritance for sons and daughters; Scheduled Tribes exempt. Drafted by a five-member panel under retired SC judge Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai; Bill passed 24 March. 🎓⭐
  2. UPSC completes 100 years: 'Manthan' — the Union Public Service Commission held centenary celebrations titled 'Manthan' on 30 September–1 October at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. Lineage: 1855 ICS exam (first Indian ICS officer Satyendranath Tagore) → 1922 first ICS exam in India → 1924 Lee Commission → 1926 Public Service Commission → constitutional body under Articles 315–323. 🎓⭐
  3. New Environment Secretary — Narendra Bhooshan (IAS, 1992 batch, UP cadre) assumed charge as Secretary, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change. 🎓
  4. Delhi HC orders takedown of deepfake content against Drishti IAS founder — Justice Anup Jairam Bhambhani directed removal of fake social-media profiles and deepfake videos impersonating Vikas Divyakirti (founder, Drishti IAS), noting personality-rights protection; Meta agreed to remove 11 of 13 offending URLs. 🎓

  2. High-level banking panel likely by October-end — the government is likely to announce a 10-member committee on banking for Viksit Bharat 2047 (part of the 2026-27 Budget announcements) with public and private-sector representation; names under consideration include former RBI Deputy Governor Michael Patra, former Financial Services Secretary M. Nagaraju and former SBI chairman Dinesh Khara, plus IBA, fintech and EAC-PM voices. 🏦🎓

  2. IICT signs five MoUs for AVGC-XR — the Indian Institute of Creative Technologies signed five MoUs on 30 September at FICCI FRAMES 2026, Mumbai to build the AVGC-XR (Animation, Visual Effects, Gaming, Comics – Extended Reality) ecosystem. 🎓
  3. New fish species in Bay of Bengal — *Bathymyrus perplexus*, identified by the Zoological Survey of India (ZSI) unit at Gopalpur, is a newly recognised fish species from the Bay of Bengal. 🎓⭐

🌆 Evening updates — results come in

Asian Games — Day 15 results 🏆🎓

  1. Men's cricket gold: India beat Pakistan by 19 runs — the defending champions posted 211/6 in the final at Korogi Sports Park, Nisshin, powered by fifties from Abhishek Sharma (61) and Tilak Varma (51*); Pakistan replied 192/6 despite Hasan Nawaz's 96. The 403-run aggregate is the highest ever in an India-Pakistan T20I. India completed a cricket double gold — the women's team had beaten Sri Lanka by 147 runs on 22 September. Sri Lanka took bronze with a 63-run win over Bangladesh. 🏆🎓⭐
  1. Men's hockey gold: India beat Malaysia 5-1 — India's fifth Asian Games men's hockey gold (1966, 1998, 2014, 2022, 2026) and its first successful back-to-back title defence. Captain Harmanpreet Singh struck a brace (11', 57'), with Jugraj Singh (23'), Hardik Singh (42', penalty stroke) and Shilanand Lakra (54'); Malaysia replied through Shello Silverius (14'). The win earned direct qualification for the LA 2028 Olympics — and with the women's team having beaten China 1-0 on 2 October, India won men's and women's hockey gold at the same Asian Games for the first time ever (hockey becomes the third Indian team sport with a double gold, after kabaddi and cricket). 🏆🎓⭐
  1. Aman Sehrawat strikes wrestling gold — Aman Sehrawat beat DPR Korea's world champion Han Chong Song 11-7 in the men's 57kg freestyle final, fighting back from 6-7 down with a decisive four-point move; he is the first Indian to win the 57kg title (he had won bronze at Hangzhou 2023). His gold follows Sujeet Kalkal's 65kg gold on 2 October (10-9 over Tajikistan's Abdulmazhid Kudiyev) — India's first two men's freestyle golds since 1978. Antim Panghal took silver in the women's 53kg, losing 9-1 to Japan's Moe Kiyooka. 🎓⭐
  1. More Day 15 golds — golf & archery — Pranavi Urs clinched gold in women's individual golf; Kumkum Mohod won women's individual recurve archery gold. In men's golf, South Korea's Tom Kim took gold, earning exemption from compulsory military service — the reward South Korea grants Asian Games champions. 🎓
  1. India end Day 15 with 85 medals, 4th in the table — with three more golds on Saturday, India's tally reached 85 medals (21 gold, 27 silver, 37 bronze), leapfrogging Uzbekistan into 4th place on the gold/silver count. The final order will only be settled on Sunday (4 October) — Uzbekistan still have an equestrian event left. 🎓⭐

Banking & Economy 🏦🎓

  1. CCEA approves record Rabi MSPs — the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approved higher Minimum Support Prices for all mandated Rabi crops for the 2027-28 marketing season, at 50–106% above the all-India weighted average cost of production: wheat up ₹25 to ₹2,610/quintal, safflower up ₹675 to ₹7,215, rapeseed & mustard up ₹413 to ₹6,613, lentil/masur up ₹390 to ₹7,390. Separately, MSP procurement of pulses and oilseeds worth ₹5,547.99 crore (~7.06 lakh tonnes) was cleared for Kharif 2026-27 in Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka and Telangana. The CCEA also approved a ₹1,789-crore intelligent traffic system for Delhi. 🏦🎓⭐
  1. Windfall tax cut on fuel exports — effective 1 October, the Finance Ministry cut the SAED on diesel exports to ₹16/litre (from ₹20) and on ATF exports to ₹10.5/litre (from ₹15); petrol exports remain at ₹0.5/litre for the fortnight. The levies — imposed in March 2026 amid West Asia tensions to protect domestic fuel availability — are revised fortnightly; domestic levies are unchanged. 🏦🎓

Policy, Energy & Misc 🎓

  1. Green Energy Corridor Phase-III approved — the Union Cabinet cleared GEC-III with an outlay of ₹1.86 lakh crore to build intra-state transmission systems capable of evacuating 135 GW of renewable energy by FY 2032-33. 🎓⭐
  1. 'Mann Ki Baat' turns 12 — the PM's monthly radio programme completed 12 years on 3 October 2026 (launched 3 October 2014, Vijayadashami day); the 138th episode aired on 27 September 2026. 🎓
  1. Odisha rewards the hockey queens — Odisha Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi announced a ₹11 lakh cash award each for the members (and support staff) of the Indian women's hockey team that beat China 1-0 to win gold. 🎓
