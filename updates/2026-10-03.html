🎯 Top stories of the day

Asian Games — Day 15 (3 Oct) 🏆

  1. Asiad enters its penultimate day — India sits 5th with 76 medals (16 gold, 25 silver, 35 bronze) after a six-gold surge on 2 October, edging ahead of Uzbekistan on total medals. Today (3 October) brings two blockbuster finals: men's T20 cricket, India vs Pakistan (10:00 am IST) and men's hockey, India vs Malaysia (3:30 pm IST) — the hockey final also carries a direct LA 2028 Olympic berth — plus medal action in archery, wrestling and golf. 🏆🎓⭐
  2. Parinaaz Dhaliwal's skeet double bronze — the skeet shooter took two bronzes: the women's skeet team bronze (score 331) with Raiza Dhillon and Maheshwari Chauhan — India's first-ever women's skeet team medal at the Asian Games — and the mixed team skeet bronze with Anantjeet Singh Naruka (44 hits across six series; tied with South Korea but took bronze on qualification-ranking tie-break). 🏆🎓⭐
  3. Shooting campaign closes with 15 medals — India's shooters finished with 3 gold, 8 silver and 4 bronze, below the record 22 of Hangzhou; trap shooter Neeru Dhanda was the standout (2 gold, 1 silver). In compound archery, Chikitha Taniparthi lost the women's individual quarterfinal 143-144; India lost the men's volleyball quarterfinal 0-3 to Pakistan, ending medal hopes in the event for the first time since 1986. 🏆

Governance & Policy 🎓

  1. Gujarat's UCC gets Presidential assent — President Droupadi Murmu gave assent to the Gujarat Uniform Civil Code, 2026, notified as Gujarat Act No. 15 of 2026 (Gazette Extraordinary published 1 October; assent given 17 September). Gujarat becomes the second state after Uttarakhand to enact a UCC. Key points: common law on marriage, divorce, succession and live-in relationships; marriage registration mandatory within 60 days (fine up to ₹10,000); live-in relationships must be registered (up to ₹10,000 fine or 3-month jail for non-registration); equal inheritance for sons and daughters; Scheduled Tribes exempt. Drafted by a five-member panel under retired SC judge Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai; Bill passed 24 March. 🎓⭐
  2. UPSC completes 100 years: 'Manthan' — the Union Public Service Commission held centenary celebrations titled 'Manthan' on 30 September–1 October at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. Lineage: 1855 ICS exam (first Indian ICS officer Satyendranath Tagore) → 1922 first ICS exam in India → 1924 Lee Commission → 1926 Public Service Commission → constitutional body under Articles 315–323. 🎓⭐
  3. New Environment Secretary — Narendra Bhooshan (IAS, 1992 batch, UP cadre) assumed charge as Secretary, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change. 🎓
  4. Delhi HC orders takedown of deepfake content against Drishti IAS founder — Justice Anup Jairam Bhambhani directed removal of fake social-media profiles and deepfake videos impersonating Vikas Divyakirti (founder, Drishti IAS), noting personality-rights protection; Meta agreed to remove 11 of 13 offending URLs. 🎓

Banking & Markets 🏦🎓

  1. Kotak Mahindra Bank: Anup Kumar Saha as MD & CEO — the RBI approved the appointment on 1 October 2026 (GKToday, 1 Oct banking news) — a second major private-bank leadership change this week after HDFC Bank's Anup Bagchi. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. High-level banking panel likely by October-end — the government is likely to announce a 10-member committee on banking for Viksit Bharat 2047 (part of the 2026-27 Budget announcements) with public and private-sector representation; names under consideration include former RBI Deputy Governor Michael Patra, former Financial Services Secretary M. Nagaraju and former SBI chairman Dinesh Khara, plus IBA, fintech and EAC-PM voices. 🏦🎓

Science, Tech & Environment 🎓

  1. SatLeo's TAPAS-1 lifts off — Ahmedabad-based spacetech startup SatLeo Labs launched its thermal-imaging satellite TAPAS-1 on 1 October; the 3-kg payload flew aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9. 🎓⭐
  2. IICT signs five MoUs for AVGC-XR — the Indian Institute of Creative Technologies signed five MoUs on 30 September at FICCI FRAMES 2026, Mumbai to build the AVGC-XR (Animation, Visual Effects, Gaming, Comics – Extended Reality) ecosystem. 🎓
  3. New fish species in Bay of Bengal — *Bathymyrus perplexus*, identified by the Zoological Survey of India (ZSI) unit at Gopalpur, is a newly recognised fish species from the Bay of Bengal. 🎓⭐
  4. Two new islands after Anak Krakatau eruption — mariners spotted two new islands off Indonesia's Anak Krakatau volcano following a 25-hour continuous eruption between 4 and 6 September 2026. 🎓
  5. CHIME telescope sees the early universe — Canada's CHIME telescope detected the faint glow of hydrogen from the early universe. Japan's Fukutoku-Okanoba volcano was also in the news. 🎓

International & World 🌍

  1. ASEAN–India Maritime Exercise 2026 — the second edition of AIME began on 28 September at Subic Bay, the Philippines; Indian Naval Ships INS Sahyadri and INS Kulish are participating. 🎓⭐
  2. California bans child marriage — the first US state to do so, through Assembly Bill 1267, which sets 18 years as the minimum age for marriage. 🎓
  3. World Congress of Gastroenterology 2026 — WCOG 2026 is being held in New Delhi from 1–3 October. 🎓
