📰 Current Affairs — 10 October 2026

*Daily exam-ready digest for Bank • UPSC • PSU aspirants, compiled from

GKToday, Insights IAS, Drishti IAS, Testbook & AffairsCloud.*

Tags: 🏦 Bank • 🎓 UPSC • 🏭 PSU • ⭐ high-yield

🎯 Top stories of the day

Elections 🗳️

  1. Bihar MLC biennial polls: withdrawal deadline ends 9 Oct — with the 9 October withdrawal deadline over, 164 candidates are in the fray for the 8 Bihar Legislative Council seats (elected by graduates' and teachers' constituencies): 98 in graduates' and 66 in teachers' constituencies. Scrutiny was completed on 7 October. Polling is on 23 October (8 AM–4 PM) and counting on 27 October; the incumbents' terms expire on 16 November. The four graduates' seats are Patna, Darbhanga, Tirhut and Kosi, and the four teachers' seats Patna, Darbhanga, Tirhut and Saran. Party tickets: BJP 4 (including sitting MLCs Nawal Kishore Yadav, N.K. Yadav and Sarvesh Kumar), JD(U) 4, Congress 5 (facing ally RJD directly in 3 seats), and Prashant Kishor's Jan Suraaj 8 candidates. 🎓⭐
  1. Rajya Sabha biennial elections: withdrawal last date 9 Oct — 9 October was the last date for withdrawal of candidatures for the Rajya Sabha seats falling vacant in November 2026; polling (if needed) and counting are scheduled for 16 October. 🎓

Sports 🏅

  1. West Indies stun India in 2nd T20 (9 Oct, Ranchi) — West Indies chased down India's 249/5 with 252/4 in 18.3 overs, winning by 6 wickets — their highest T20I chase ever and the second-highest in men's T20I history. Shai Hope's unbeaten 102 off 45 balls and Sherfane Rutherford's 84 off 37 led the chase; Sanju Samson had made 82 off 41 for India. The five-match series is level 1–1 after India's win in the 1st T20 (6 Oct, Lucknow); the 3rd T20 is on 11 October in Indore. 🎓🏦⭐
  1. PV Sindhu exits Arctic Open (9 Oct) — Sindhu lost her quarterfinal at the Arctic Open (BWF Super 500, Vantaa, Finland) to Thailand's Ratchanok Intanon 21-11, 19-21, 21-18; India's campaign ended with the men's doubles pair Garaga/Pruthvi also out. 🎓

Governance & Polity 🎓

  1. Press Council of India reconstituted for 15th term (8 Oct) — the Centre has reconstituted the Press Council of India for its 15th term under the Press Council Act, 1978: 29 members including the Chairperson, of whom 13 are working journalists (6 editors and 7 others). 🎓
  1. Centre opposes Rajoana mercy plea (9 Oct) — the Union Government told the Supreme Court it opposes convict Balwant Singh Rajoana's plea to commute his death sentence, citing the 14-year delay in his mercy petition, which has been pending since 2012. 🎓

Economy & Trade 📊

  1. AITIGA 15th Joint Committee meets in Jakarta (6–9 Oct) — the India–ASEAN Trade in Goods Agreement (AITIGA) Joint Committee met at the ASEAN Secretariat in Jakarta; India's delegation was co-chaired by Nitin Kumar Yadav, Additional Secretary, Department of Commerce. The meeting reviewed negotiation progress — no revised agreement was signed (a process milestone, not a signing). 🎓🏦
  1. NSO releases first National Household Travel Survey (9 Oct) — the National Statistics Office (under MoSPI) released the key findings of the first-ever all-India household travel survey, mapping how India travels. 🎓🏦

Environment 🌱

  1. UNEP Adaptation Gap Report 2026: finance keeps shrinking — international adaptation finance to developing nations fell from US$28 billion (2022) to US$26 billion (2023); the annual adaptation gap now stands at US$284–339 billion, with developing countries' needs 12 times current flows — putting the COP26 goal of doubling adaptation finance by 2025 out of reach. 🎓⭐

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. World Space Week ends today (4–10 Oct) — the global observance wraps up today with the 2026 theme "Rocket Revolution", marking the 1957 Sputnik launch (4 Oct) and the 1967 entry into force of the Outer Space Treaty (10 Oct). 🎓

Diplomacy 🌍

  1. Arctic Circle Assembly (8–10 Oct, Reykjavik) — MoS for External Affairs & Environment, Forest & Climate Change Kirti Vardhan Singh is leading India's delegation at the Arctic Circle Assembly in Reykjavik, Iceland, which covers climate, scientific cooperation, Indigenous leadership and Arctic geopolitics. 🎓

Important Days 📅

  • 10 October — National Postal Day (India): observed during National Postal Week (9–15 October); yesterday (9 Oct) was World Post Day, marking the founding of the Universal Postal Union (9 October 1874, Bern) — the 2026 theme is "One Postal Network, Infinite Possibilities".
  • Also today: 10 October — World Mental Health Day; tomorrow 11 October — International Day of the Girl Child.

🌙 Evening updates — stories the morning page missed

Awards & Appointments 🏆

  1. Nobel Peace Prize 2026: Navi Pillay (9 Oct) — the Norwegian Nobel Committee has awarded the Peace Prize to South African jurist Navanethem "Navi" Pillay, of Indian Tamil descent, for her efforts to promote peace and international law — former head of the UN Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory and former ICC judge. For the exam register: Medicine — Deisseroth, Hegemann, Nagel (optogenetics); Physics — Francis Halzen (IceCube neutrinos); Chemistry — Henri B. Kagan & Kenso Soai (asymmetric synthesis); Literature — Anne Carson; Economics due 12 Oct. 🎓⭐
  1. Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit to be 29th Chief of the Air Staff (6 Oct) — currently Vice Chief, Dixit will take charge in the rank of Air Chief Marshal on the afternoon of 31 October 2026, succeeding Air Chief Marshal Amar Preet Singh, who superannuates the same day. Experimental Test Pilot and Qualified Flying Instructor with ~3,500 flying hours; earlier served as Chief of Integrated Defence Staff and AOC-in-C, Central Air Command. 🎓
  1. HAL board: DRDO's Anindya Biswas (7 Oct) — Dr. K Rajalakshmi Menon ceased as Government Nominee Director (nomination withdrawn); Anindya Biswas, DS & Director of DRDO's Research Centre Imarat, takes over the government-nominee slot on the HAL board. 🏭🎓

Economy 💰

  1. RBI MPC (7 Oct): repo rate raised 25 bps to 5.50% — the first rate hike since February 2023; the repo goes from 5.25% to 5.50%, stance shifted from 'neutral' to 'calibrated tightening' (rate cuts "off the table in the near term" — Governor Sanjay Malhotra). SDF 5.25%, MSF & Bank Rate 5.75%; FY27 GDP forecast raised to 7.1%, CPI inflation projection to 5.2%; trigger: West Asia re-escalation and hardening crude. 🏦🎓⭐
  1. Cabinet (6 Oct) approves ₹10,000-crore SME Growth Fund — a long-term equity-capital fund for SMEs (majority to manufacturing and Tier-2/3 clusters) under the "Creating Champion MSMEs" strategy, first announced in Budget 2026-27. Also cleared: the Integrated Transport & Logistics Authority (ITLA), a DPIIT-controlled SPV for transport-and-logistics research, planning and impact assessment. 🏦🎓

Defence 🛡️

  1. MoD–BrahMos Aerospace ₹661.50-crore contract (6 Oct) — MoD signed a ₹661.50 crore deal with BrahMos Aerospace for fire control systems and launchers for Indian Navy ships, under the Buy (Indian-IDDM) category (≥68% indigenous content); signed in the presence of Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh. BrahMos is the Navy's primary surface-to-surface missile for maritime strike. 🎓🏭
  1. Exercise Mountain Guardian 2026 (5–18 Oct) — the India–UAE joint military exercise (3rd edition) is under way at the Foreign Training Node, Chaubatia, Uttarakhand: 92 personnel (46 each — Indian Army Bhairav Battalion and UAE Mountain Infantry Battalion), focused on mountain warfare; women officers and combatants are serving in specialist roles. 🎓

State & Society 🏛️

  1. Andhra Pradesh declares drought in 21 of 26 districts (GO, 1 Oct) — 388 of 688 mandals affected (283 severe, 105 moderate) after a 53.4% rainfall deficit in June–August (197.6 mm vs a normal of 424.3 mm), linked to prevailing El Niño conditions; an early declaration ahead of the usual 31-October assessment. 🎓

Sports 🏅

  1. Bhagat & Patel — India's flagbearers for the 2026 Asian Para Games — para-badminton's Pramod Bhagat and para table tennis's Bhavina Patel will carry the flag at Aichi-Nagoya, Japan (18–24 October). 🎓
