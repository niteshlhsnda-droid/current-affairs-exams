🎯 Top stories of the month

Polity & Governance 🏛️

  1. Parliament passed the VB–G Ram G Bill on 18 December 2025 — the Viksit Bharat Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission replaces MGNREGA and guarantees 125 days of rural wage employment; fund-sharing is 60:40 between Centre and states (90:10 for northeastern and Himalayan states and UTs). 🎓⭐
  2. Parliament passed the SHANTI Bill on 18 December 2025 — the Sustainable Harnessing and Advancement of Nuclear Energy for Transforming India Bill replaces the Atomic Energy Act, 1962 and the Civil Liability for Nuclear Damage Act, 2010; it permits regulated Indian private participation in civil nuclear energy and gives the AERB statutory status. 🎓⭐

Economy, Banking & Finance 🏦

  1. RBI's December MPC cut the repo rate by 25 bps to 5.25% (unanimous, neutral stance retained) on 5 December; the SDF stands at 5% and the MSF/Bank Rate at 5.50%. The FY26 GDP forecast was raised to 7.3% (from 6.8%) and the inflation forecast cut to 2% (from 2.6%) after October CPI fell to 0.3%. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. UPI processed 20.47 billion transactions worth ₹26.32 lakh crore in November — volume up 32% year-on-year, the third time the platform crossed 20 billion monthly transactions. 🏦🎓⭐
  3. GST collection in December stood at ₹1,74,550 crore, up 6% year-on-year, per Finance Ministry data. 🏦🎓
  4. IndiGo's FDTL crisis: 550+ flights were cancelled on 4 December amid crew shortages after revised DGCA flight-and-duty-time norms (48-hour weekly rest, maximum two night landings per week) took effect on 1 November; over 1,000 flights were cancelled across four days before DGCA temporarily relaxed the weekly-rest rule. 🏦🎓⭐

Environment & Ecology 🌿

  1. Cyclone Ditwah, named on 27 November, ravaged Sri Lanka with floods and landslides before weakening and making landfall on the north Tamil Nadu coast — between Kalpakkam and Puducherry — on 3 December; India launched Operation Sagar Bandhu on 28 November, sending 53 tonnes of relief material and 80 NDRF personnel to Sri Lanka. The name "Ditwah" was proposed by Yemen after the Detwah Lagoon on Socotra. 🎓⭐

Sports 🏅

  1. Ashes 2025–26: Australia took an unassailable 3–0 lead with wins at Brisbane (8 wickets) and Adelaide (82 runs); England won the Boxing Day Test at Melbourne by 4 wickets. 🎓

Appointments & Resignations 👔

  1. B. Sairam took charge as Chairman-cum-Managing Director of Coal India on 15 December 2025, succeeding Sanoj Kumar Jha who held interim charge; the Maharatna PSU produces over 80% of India's domestic coal. 🏭🎓
  2. Saleem Ahmad took charge as Chairman & Managing Director of Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd (RVNL) on 23 December 2025; he was earlier Director (Projects) at NBCC. 🏭🎓
  3. Nitin Nabin was appointed BJP National Working President on 14 December by the party's Parliamentary Board; the 45-year-old five-time Bankipur (Patna) MLA and Bihar minister succeeds J.P. Nadda. 🎓⭐

International Relations 🌐

  1. Russian President Vladimir Putin visited India on 4–5 December for the 23rd Annual Summit, marking 25 years of the India–Russia Strategic Partnership; both sides set a $100 billion trade target for 2030 and signed pacts on trade, migration and mobility, maritime cooperation, health and food safety. 🎓⭐
  2. José Antonio Kast won Chile's presidential runoff on 14 December with about 58% of the vote, defeating Communist candidate Jeannette Jara (~42%); the first round was held on 16 November. 🎓⭐

State News 🗺️

  1. UDF swept Kerala's local body elections (results 13 December): it won 38.81% of the vote against the LDF's 33.45% and the NDA's 14.71%, leading 505 of 941 grama panchayats, 54 municipalities and 4 of 6 corporations; the BJP became the largest front in Thiruvananthapuram Corporation with 50 of 101 wards. 🎓⭐

Obituaries 🕯️

  1. Malayalam actor-filmmaker Sreenivasan died in Kochi on 20 December, aged 69 — the screenwriter behind Nadodikkattu and Gandhinagar 2nd Street, and director of Vadakkunokkiyanthram and Chinthavishtayaya Shyamala. 🎓
