## 🎯 Top stories of the month

### Polity & Governance 🏛️
1. **NDA swept the Bihar Assembly elections**, winning 202 of 243 seats; the BJP emerged as the single-largest party with 89 seats, followed by the JD(U) with 85, LJP (Ram Vilas) 19, HAM (Secular) 5 and RLM 4. Results were declared on 14 November 2025. 🎓⭐
2. **Four labour codes came into force on 21 November 2025** — the Code on Wages (2019), the Industrial Relations Code (2020), the Code on Social Security (2020) and the OSH & Working Conditions Code (2020) — consolidating 29 central labour laws. 🎓⭐
3. **Blast near Delhi's Red Fort on 10 November** — a Hyundai i20 exploded near Gate 1 of the Red Fort Metro Station, killing 13 people; the case was registered under the UAPA and the Explosives Act with a multi-agency investigation. 🎓

### Economy, Banking & Finance 🏦
4. **India's GDP grew 8.2% in Q2 FY26**, data released on 28 November showed; manufacturing expanded 9.1%, services 9.2% and agriculture 3.5%, taking H1 FY26 growth to 8%. 🏦🎓⭐
5. **UPI hit a record ₹27.28 lakh crore in October** (reported in November) with 20.7 billion transactions — the highest monthly volume and value ever; it crossed 750 million transactions in a single day on 18 October. 🏦🎓⭐
6. **GST collection in November stood at ₹1,70,276 crore**, up 0.7% year-on-year; domestic GST revenue fell 2.3% while import GST rose 10.2%, and net collection was ₹1,52,079 crore. 🏦🎓⭐

### Science & Technology 🔬
7. **ISRO launched CMS-03 (GSAT-7R)** on 2 November — the 4,410-kg communication satellite rode the LVM3-M5 from Sriharikota, the heaviest communication satellite ever placed in geostationary transfer orbit from Indian soil, meant for naval and strategic communications. 🎓⭐

### Environment & Ecology 🌿
8. **COP30 was held in Belém, Brazil (10–22 November)**; the "Belém Package" called for tripling adaptation finance by 2035 and launched a Just Transition Mechanism, but the final consensus omitted a roadmap to phase out fossil fuels. 29 decisions were adopted. 🎓⭐

### Sports 🏅
9. **India won its maiden Women's ODI World Cup** on 2 November, beating South Africa by 52 runs at Navi Mumbai; Shafali Verma was Player of the Match and Deepti Sharma — 215 runs and 22 wickets — was Player of the Tournament. 🎓⭐
10. **South Africa whitewashed India 2–0 in the Test series** (Kolkata by 30 runs; Guwahati by 408 runs — India's heaviest home Test defeat by runs), their first series win in India since 2000. 🎓
11. **FIDE Chess World Cup, Goa (31 Oct–27 Nov)** — Uzbekistan's 19-year-old Javokhir Sindarov beat China's Wei Yi in tiebreaks to become the youngest-ever champion; the trophy was renamed the Viswanathan Anand Cup, and the top three qualified for the 2026 Candidates. 🎓⭐

### Awards & Honours 🏆
12. **2025 Booker Prize went to David Szalay's novel *Flesh***, announced on 10 November; the prize carries £50,000. 🎓⭐
13. **56th IFFI (Goa, 20–28 November)** — the Golden Peacock for Best Film went to the Vietnamese film *Skin of Youth*, directed by Ash Mayfair. 🎓
14. **IFFI felicitated Rajinikanth** for completing 50 years in cinema. 🎓
15. **IFFI also gave the ICFT-UNESCO Gandhi Medal to *Safe House*** and named *Bandish Bandits Season 2* the Best Web Series. 🎓

### Appointments & Resignations 👔
16. **Justice Surya Kant was sworn in as the 53rd Chief Justice of India** on 24 November, succeeding Justice B.R. Gavai; his term runs until 9 February 2027. 🎓⭐
17. **Nitish Kumar took oath as Bihar Chief Minister for a record tenth time** on 20 November at Gandhi Maidan, Patna; Samrat Choudhary and Vijay Kumar Sinha returned as Deputy CMs, with Governor Arif Mohammed Khan administering the oath. 🎓⭐

### International Relations 🌐
18. **G20 Leaders' Summit in Johannesburg** — the first on African soil, themed "Solidarity, Equality, Sustainability"; PM Modi proposed six global ideas including countering the drug-terror nexus, a global healthcare response team, the Africa Skills Multiplier, a traditional-knowledge repository, an open satellite-data partnership and critical-minerals circularity. 🎓⭐
19. **India–Bhutan:** PM Modi and King Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck inaugurated the 1,020 MW Punatsangchhu-II hydropower project on 11 November; India extended a ₹4,000 crore line of credit to Bhutan and agreed to resume work on the 1,200 MW Punatsangchhu-I. 🎓

### Obituaries 🕯️
20. **Dharmendra died in Mumbai on 24 November, aged 89** — Padma Bhushan recipient, star of *Sholay* with 300+ films across six decades, and a former Lok Sabha MP. 🎓⭐
21. **Kamini Kaushal died in Mumbai on 13 November, aged 98** — Filmfare Best Actress (1956) for *Biraj Bahu*; her debut *Neecha Nagar* (1946) remains the only Indian film to win the Cannes Palme d'Or. 🎓
22. **Sulakshana Pandit died in Mumbai on 6 November, aged 71** — playback singer and actress who won the Filmfare Best Female Playback Singer award in 1976 for "Tu Hi Sagar Tu Hi Kinara" (*Sankalp*); sister of Jatin–Lalit and niece of Pandit Jasraj. 🎓

### Important Days 📅
23. **National Education Day — 11 November** (birth anniversary of Maulana Abul Kalam Azad). 🎓
24. **Children's Day — 14 November** (birth anniversary of Jawaharlal Nehru). 🎓
25. **National Press Day — 16 November** (Press Council of India). 🎓
26. **World Children's Day — 20 November** (UNICEF). 🎓
27. **Constitution Day (Samvidhan Diwas) — 26 November** (adoption of the Constitution in 1949). 🎓⭐

### State News 🗺️
28. **44th India International Trade Fair (IITF)** was held at Bharat Mandapam, Pragati Maidan, New Delhi, 14–27 November; theme "Ek Bharat, Shreshtha Bharat"; organised by the India Trade Promotion Organisation. 🏭🎓
29. **Jharkhand observed its 25th Foundation Day on 15 November 2025** — the state was carved out of Bihar in 2000. 🎓
30. **Uttarakhand observed its Foundation Day on 9 November 2025** — the state was formed in 2000. 🎓
