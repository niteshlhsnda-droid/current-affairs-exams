## 🎯 Top stories of the month

### Polity & Governance 🏛️
1. **79th Independence Day (15 August)** — themes 'Naya Bharat', '150 Years of Vande Mataram' and 'Yuva Shakti for Viksit Bharat@2047'; PM Modi's record 103-minute, 13th consecutive Red Fort address covering the Emergency, Operation Sindoor, 'Made in India' defence, semiconductors, critical minerals, women empowerment and RSS's centenary year; Flying Officer Rashika Sharma assists the flag-hoisting; two Mi-17 helicopters shower petals — one carrying the national flag, the other an Operation Sindoor flag; 2,500 NCC cadets and My Bharat volunteers form the 'Naya Bharat' logo. 🎓⭐
2. **Cabinet Secretary T.V. Somanathan and Home Secretary Govind Mohan get a one-year extension** (6 August), providing continuity at the top of the bureaucracy. 🎓⭐
3. **Supreme Court Collegium recommends new Chief Justices** (6 August) — Justice Ravindra V. Ghuge for the Calcutta High Court; Justice Mahesh Chandra Tripathi for the Bombay High Court, as its 50th Chief Justice. 🎓⭐

### Economy, Banking & Finance 🏦
4. **RBI holds the repo rate at 5.25% (MPC, 3–5 August)** — neutral stance; FY27 GDP forecast raised to 6.7% and CPI forecast trimmed to 5.0%; Governor Sanjay Malhotra cites resilient demand and easing supply disruptions, with deficient monsoon and volatile crude as risks. 🏦🎓⭐
5. **India's GDP grows 7.8% in Q1 FY27** (NSO, 31 August) — real GDP ₹81.36 lakh crore (vs ₹75.46 lakh crore in Q1 FY26); nominal GDP +10.3% to ₹88.27 lakh crore; GVA +8.2%; RBI had projected 7%. 🏦🎓🏭⭐
6. **July retail inflation at 4.45%** (released 12 August) — up from 4.38% in June; food inflation 5.52%; rural 4.84% vs urban 3.96%. 🏦
7. **Forex reserves rise $10.51 billion to $692.87 billion** (week ended 31 July) — highest in over two months; record $728.494 billion (February 2026). 🏦
8. **MoSPI signs two MoUs with ISI Kolkata (IDEAS foundation)** — a desktop-based GDP nowcasting tool and a harmonised indicator framework linking health/nutrition scheme data with household-level NSS data. 🏦🎓
9. **RBI appoints Monisha Chakraborty as Executive Director** (3 August) — career central banker (30+ years); to oversee the Foreign Exchange Department and Financial Markets Regulation Department. 🏦
10. **July GST collections ₹2,11,205 crore** (+15.4% YoY; released 1 August) — imports-led growth; the 57th GST Council meeting announced for 12 September in New Delhi. 🏦

### Sports 🏅
11. **India win the Test series 1-0 in Sri Lanka** — the 2nd Test at Colombo ends in a draw on 27 August after Sonal Dinusha's unbeaten 133* (following-on) saves Sri Lanka; India had declared at 503-9; debutant spinner Saransh Jain among the wicket-takers. 🎓⭐

### Awards & Honours 🏆
12. **Deepak Dhar receives the 2026 Dirac Medal** (ICTP, Trieste, 8 August) — shared with Bernard Derrida, Marc Mézard and Haim Sompolinsky; for contributions to statistical mechanics, optimisation, theoretical neuroscience and AI; famed for the sandpile model and self-organised criticality. 🎓⭐
13. **James Gray to receive the Pardo alla Carriera** at the 79th Locarno Film Festival (9 August) — the career-achievement award to be presented before the screening of his film 'Paper Tiger'. 🎓

### Appointments & Resignations 📋
14. **Dr Srinivasa Kumar Tummala assumes charge as MoES Secretary** (11 August) — Ministry of Earth Sciences. 🎓
15. **Senior bureaucratic reshuffle (10 August)** — Deepti Gaur Mukerjee becomes Higher Education Secretary; Pallavi Jain Govil becomes Corporate Affairs Secretary; Sunil Paliwal becomes Youth Affairs Secretary. 🎓
16. **Anand Prakash appointed High Commissioner to Eswatini** — 2009-batch IFS officer, currently Director in the MEA; Eswatini (formerly Swaziland) is a landlocked southern-African nation. 🎓
17. **Satish Golcha gets a new posting in the Delhi Home Guards Directorate** (26 August). 🎓

### Defence 🛡️
18. **India test-fires the nuclear-capable Agni-4** (6 August) — from the Integrated Test Range, Chandipur, Odisha, under the Strategic Forces Command; the 4,000-km medium-range ballistic missile validated all operational parameters; Rajnath Singh calls it a "textbook mission launch". 🎓⭐
19. **Exercise Pitch Black 2026 concludes (7 August)** — the IAF contingent returns from the biennial multinational air exercise in northern Australia (4 Rafales, 2 C-17s, 120+ Air Warriors; ~100 aircraft, 20 nations). 🎓
20. **Sqn Ldr Bhawana Kanth becomes the first woman fighter pilot in the IAF to complete the Fighter Combat Leader (FCL) course** (7 August) — the 20-week advanced air-combat course at TACDE, Gwalior; she joined the IAF in the first batch of women fighter pilots (2016). 🎓⭐

### Government Schemes 📜
21. **Assam opens applications for the Nijut Moina and Nijut Babu schemes** (6 August) — higher-education support: Nijut Moina (girls; ~4 lakh beneficiaries in 2025-26) and Nijut Babu (boys; 50,000 last year, 70,000 more targeted); eligibility expanded to polytechnic girls and integrated-PG students. 🎓

### Books & Authors 📚
22. **PM Modi releases Ram Nath Kovind's autobiography** — "Triumph of the Indian Republic: My Life, My Struggles" at Vigyan Bhavan, New Delhi (12 August). 🎓⭐

### Obituaries 🕯️

24. **Raja Sen (16 August, age 71)** — National Award-winning Bengali filmmaker, died in Kolkata. 🎓
25. **Sowcar Janaki (21 August, age 94)** — veteran Indian actress, passed away in Chennai. 🎓
26. **Dolly Parton (25 August, age 80)** — American country-music legend, singer-songwriter and philanthropist. 🎓
