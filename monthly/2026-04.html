🎯 Top stories of the month

Polity & Governance 🏛️

  1. PM Modi visits Sikkim (Apr 27–28) — inaugurates projects worth Rs 4,000 crore. 🎓
  2. Ladakh gets 5 new districts — LG Vinai Kumar Saxena approves the notification of five new districts for the UT. 🎓⭐
  3. NITI Aayog launches 'DPI@2047 Roadmap' — for the adoption of Digital Public Infrastructure 2.0. 🎓

Economy, Banking & Finance 🏦

  1. RBI revokes banking licence of PPBL — Paytm Payments Bank Ltd loses its licence over non-compliance with banking regulations. 🏦⭐
  2. RBI launches 'Mission Saksham' for urban co-operative banks — a capacity-building programme for the UCB sector. 🏦
  3. RBI introduces ECL framework — expected credit loss framework effective April 1, 2027; phased FX derivatives rollout from July 2027. 🏦
  4. RBI unveils 'Utkarsh 2029' — targets central bank digital currency and a global UPI expansion drive. 🏦
  5. RBI approves Emirates NBD's 60% stake acquisition in RBL Bank. 🏦
  6. ADB raises India's FY27 GDP forecast from 6.5% to 6.9%. 🏦🎓⭐
  7. SEBI launches three new IT platforms to transform the regulatory landscape; also extends IPO approval validity and MPS norms to September 30, 2026. 🏦
  8. Amul becomes 1st Indian FMCG company to hit Rs 1 trillion turnover in FY26. 🏦🎓⭐

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. NASA launches Artemis II — world's first crewed lunar mission with four astronauts; the crew members later returned to Earth. 🎓⭐
  2. FedEx & IIT-Madras complete India's first intra-city drone delivery. 🎓

Environment & Ecology 🌿

  1. MoEFCC designates 'Bhavasagara' — India's first National Repository for Deep-Sea Fauna. 🎓⭐
  2. MoEFCC declares Eco-Sensitive Zone around UP's Barasingha Wildlife Sanctuary. 🎓

Sports 🏅
