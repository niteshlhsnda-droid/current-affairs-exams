🎯 Top stories of the month

Polity & Governance 🏛️

  1. C.P. Radhakrishnan elected India's 15th Vice President (9 September) — the NDA nominee defeated INDIA bloc's Justice B. Sudershan Reddy 452–300; turnout was 98.2% (767 of 781 eligible MPs) with 752 valid ballots; the election followed Jagdeep Dhankhar's resignation on 21 July. 🎓⭐
  2. GSTAT formally launched in New Delhi — the Goods and Services Tax Appellate Tribunal will accept appeals from September 2025 and begin hearings from December, as decided by the 56th GST Council. 🏦🎓
  3. ECI delists 474 Registered Unrecognised Political Parties (22 September) — removed for not contesting any election in the last six years. 🎓
  4. ECI launches Aadhaar-linked e-sign verification on the ECINET portal and app (September 2025) for electronic voter verification. 🎓
  5. Supreme Court directs Sikh marriage registration rules — 17 states and 8 UTs must frame rules under the Anand Marriage Act, 1909 within four months. 🎓
  6. SC examines Governor's powers (Articles 200 & 201) — a Constitution Bench hears the May 2025 Presidential reference carrying 14 questions on Governors' assent to Bills. 🎓

Economy, Banking & Finance 🏦

  1. 56th GST Council (3–4 September, New Delhi): 12% and 28% slabs scrapped — a two-tier structure of 5% (merit) and 18% (standard), plus a 40% demerit rate for sin/luxury goods; individual life and health insurance exempted; effective 22 September 2025 — the "GST 2.0" reform. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. Retail inflation at 2.07% in August 2025 — a benign CPI print amid subdued nominal GDP growth. 🏦
  3. US imposes a 25% tariff on Indian imports — exposing regional export imbalances; inverted-duty corrections follow in textiles, chemicals and fertilisers. 🏦🎓

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. NISAR sends its first Earth images (30 September) — the NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar satellite's inaugural imagery as it readies for full operations. 🎓⭐
  2. Foundation stone laid for the Mahi Banswara Rajasthan Atomic Power Project — a major upcoming nuclear plant in Rajasthan. 🎓
  3. India's first Maritime Simulation Centre inaugurated at AMET Knowledge Park near Chennai. 🎓
  4. BSF developing drone-mounted radars with ISRO's assistance for smarter border surveillance. 🎓
  5. New edible mushroom 'Lactifluus khasianus' discovered in Meghalaya's East Khasi Hills. 🎓
  6. New Impatiens species discovered in the Kudremukh range of the Western Ghats. 🎓

Environment & Ecology 🌿

  1. India adds two Ramsar wetlands in Bihar — total reaches 93, the highest in Asia. 🎓⭐
  2. Palk Bay Dugong Conservation Reserve gets IUCN recognition at the IUCN World Congress. 🎓
  3. 54% dragonfly surge in Delhi's biodiversity parks (25–27 September survey across seven DDA parks) — a sign of improving wetland health. 🎓
  4. CPCB: river pollution shows slight improvement in 2023 — fewer monitoring locations unfit for use. 🎓
  5. Dhordo (Kutch) becomes fully solar-powered — the UNWTO-recognised Best Tourism Village. 🎓

Sports 🏅

  1. India win Asia Cup 2025 (28 September, Dubai) — beat Pakistan by five wickets in the final; Tilak Varma's 69* and Kuldeep Yadav's 4/30; a record-extending ninth title, unbeaten across all seven matches. 🎓⭐
  2. US Open 2025: Carlos Alcaraz beats Jannik Sinner 6-2, 3-6, 6-1, 6-4 (7 September) — sixth Grand Slam, second US Open crown, reclaims world No. 1; Aryna Sabalenka beats Amanda Anisimova for back-to-back women's titles. 🎓⭐
  3. Ballon d'Or 2025 (22 September, Paris): Ousmane Dembélé (men) and Aitana Bonmatí (women — third consecutive win). 🎓⭐
  4. World Athletics Championships, Tokyo (13–21 September): Neeraj Chopra finishes 8th (84.03 m); Sachin Yadav 4th with an 86.27 m personal best; gold to Keshorn Walcott (88.16 m). 🎓

Awards & Honours 🏆

  1. Mohanlal conferred the Dadasaheb Phalke Award 2023 — announced 20 September (PIB); presented by President Droupadi Murmu at the 71st National Film Awards ceremony on 23 September; the 55th recipient, honoured at 65 (the youngest ever); Padma Shri (2001), Padma Bhushan (2019). 🎓⭐

Defence 🛡️

  1. IAF retires its last MiG-21s (26 September) — No. 23 (Panthers) and No. 3 (Cobras) squadrons decommissioned, ending the type's 62-year Indian service. 🎓⭐
  2. Agni-Prime test-fired from a rail-based mobile launcher — a first-of-its-kind trial for India (September 2025). 🎓
  3. 'Jal Prahar 2025': Navy–Army joint amphibious exercise on the eastern coast — the biannual drill. 🎓
  4. AFSPA extended for six more months in parts of Manipur, Arunachal Pradesh and Nagaland from October 2025. 🎓

International Relations 🌐

  1. India elected to the Interpol Asian Committee at the 25th Asian Regional Conference in Singapore. 🎓
  2. India–SICA Foreign Ministers' Meeting in New York — EAM S. Jaishankar deepens ties with the Central American Integration System. 🎓
  3. France, UK, Canada and Australia recognise the State of Palestine (September 2025 wave); India had recognised Palestine in 1988. 🎓
  4. Saudi Arabia–Pakistan Strategic Mutual Defence Agreement (17 September) — aggression against one treated as aggression against both. 🎓

Government Schemes 📜

  1. PM E-DRIVE: operational guidelines for 72,300+ public EV charging stations across India. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. PM-KISAN 21st instalment released — prioritised for flood-hit farmers in Himachal Pradesh, Punjab and Uttarakhand. 🏦🎓
  3. Bihar Mukhya Mantri Mahila Rozgar Yojana — PM Modi transfers the first instalment of ₹7,500 crore (25 September). 🎓
  4. Haryana Deendayal Lado Lakshmi Yojana — ₹2,100 per month to eligible women. 🎓

Reports & Indices 📊

  1. Child marriage declined 69% among girls and 72% among boys — Just Rights for Children report (September 2025). 🎓

Obituaries 🕯️

  1. Zubeen Garg (19 September, age 52) — Assamese music icon; died in Singapore; famed for "Ya Ali" (Gangster, 2006); state funeral on 23 September. 🎓⭐

Important Days 📅

  • Teachers' Day — 5 September (Dr S. Radhakrishnan's birth anniversary)
  • Hindi Diwas — 14 September
  • World Tourism Day — 27 September (theme: 'Tourism and Sustainable Transformation')

State News 🗺️

  1. Ladakh protests (September) — demands for Sixth Schedule safeguards and statehood; 24 September violence in Leh, including the torching of a BJP office; tear gas used. 🎓
  2. Himalayan disasters 2025 — cloudbursts, flash floods and landslides batter Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand and Punjab; relief routed through the PM-KISAN 21st instalment. 🎓
  3. Bidar (Karnataka) wins the Jal Sanchay Jan Bhagidari Award under Jal Shakti Abhiyan–Catch the Rain. 🎓
  4. Kokrajhar–Gelephu rail link (69 km) designated a Special Railway Project — the first-ever rail link between India and Bhutan. 🎓⭐
  5. ICGS Adamya commissioned at Paradip (19 September) — the first of eight Adamya-class Fast Patrol Vessels for the Coast Guard. 🎓
