🎯 Top stories of May 2026

🏛️ Polity & Governance

  1. Suvendu Adhikari sworn in as West Bengal Chief Minister on 9 May 2026 — ended 15 years of Trinamool Congress rule and formed the state's first-ever BJP-led government after a landslide win. 🎓⭐
  2. V. D. Satheesan sworn in as Kerala Chief Minister on 18 May 2026 — the UDF returned to power after a decade, winning 102 of 140 seats, with the Congress alone on 63. 🎓⭐
  3. NEET-UG 2026 (held 3 May) was cancelled by the NTA on 12 May 2026 over alleged paper-leak and irregularity findings; the matter was referred to the CBI and a fresh exam was ordered (held 21 June). 🎓⭐
  4. The Supreme Court permitted the euthanasia of rabid, incurably ill and demonstrably dangerous stray dogs in its May 2026 ruling on stray-dog management. 🎓

💰 Economy, Banking & Finance

  1. BIS notified IS 19850:2026 (Gazette, 18 May; effective 15 May 2026) — India's first fuel-quality standard for petrol blends beyond E20, covering E22, E25, E27 and E30. 🎓🏭⭐
  2. Mahindra & Mahindra and DBS Bank rolled out a sustainability-linked dealer financing programme in May 2026 — dealer credit terms tied to sustainability performance. 🏦🏭

🔬 Science & Technology

  1. Sweden joined ISRO's Shukrayaan Venus orbiter programme — the mission will carry 19 payloads to study Venus. 🎓
  2. The IAF signed contracts with IIT Bombay for predictive maintenance of the Su-30MKI fleet — using AI-driven health monitoring of fighter aircraft. 🎓
  3. The Indian Navy selected Tonbo Imaging to develop a high-power microwave (HPM) system — a directed-energy capability against drones and electronic threats. 🎓
  4. Squadron Leader Saanya became the IAF's first woman Category-A flying instructor in May 2026. 🎓

🌿 Environment & Ecology

  1. The IEA released the Global Methane Tracker 2026 in May 2026 — the annual assessment of methane emissions from the energy sector. 🎓

🏅 Sports

  1. Royal Challengers Bengaluru won IPL 2026 on 31 May — defeating Gujarat Titans by five wickets at Ahmedabad; Rajat Patidar captained the side and Virat Kohli starred with an unbeaten 75. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. Marta Kostyuk won the Madrid Open women's singles title on 2 May 2026 — defeating Mirra Andreeva 6–3, 7–5 in the final. 🎓
  3. Kuldeep Kumar set a national pole-vault record of 5.41 m in May 2026. 🎓

🏆 Awards & Honours

  1. The 2026 Pulitzer for Illustrated Reporting and Commentary went to Anand RK, Suparna Sharma and Natalie Obiko Pearson for trAPPed — an investigation of digital-arrest scams, announced in early May. 🎓⭐
  2. Parveen Shaikh won the 2026 Whitley Award for her conservation work on the Indian skimmer. 🎓

👤 Appointments & Resignations

  1. Prasoon Joshi was appointed Chairman of Prasar Bharati on 2 May 2026 (PIB). 🎓⭐
  2. Lt Gen N. S. Raja Subramani was appointed India's third Chief of Defence Staff — he assumed charge on 31 May 2026, also serving as Secretary, Department of Military Affairs. 🎓⭐

🛡️ Defence

  1. DRDO and the Indian Navy conducted the maiden salvo launch of NASM-SR (Naval Anti-Ship Missile–Short Range) in early May 2026 — two missiles fired in quick succession from a naval helicopter off the Odisha coast, demonstrating waterline-hit capability. 🎓⭐
  2. An advanced Agni missile with MIRV technology was successfully flight-tested on 8 May 2026 from Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island, with multiple payloads directed at spatially separated targets. 🎓⭐
  3. Agni-1 was successfully test-launched on 22 May 2026 from ITR Chandipur under the Strategic Forces Command (PIB). 🎓
  4. India received its fourth S-400 (Triumf) air-defence squadron from Russia in May 2026. 🎓
  5. The US approved Apache helicopter and M777 howitzer support deals for India worth a combined $428.2 million in May 2026. 🎓
  6. Bharat Forge tied up with Andhra Pradesh for a marine gas-turbine manufacturing complex in May 2026. 🎓🏭

🌐 International Relations

  1. India and the US signed a bilateral framework on critical minerals and rare earths on 26 May 2026 in New Delhi — signed by S. Jaishankar and Marco Rubio during the Quad Foreign Ministers' meeting, covering mining, processing, recycling and investment. 🎓⭐
  2. India and Vietnam signed MoUs on AI and quantum technology cooperation in May 2026. 🎓
  3. Russia and Afghanistan signed a military-technical cooperation pact in May 2026. 🎓
  4. Russia agreed to build Kazakhstan's first nuclear power plant — estimated at $16.5 billion — in May 2026. 🎓
  5. The UN added Israeli and Russian forces to its blacklist of parties credibly suspected of conflict-related sexual violence in May 2026. 🎓

🗺️ State News

  1. Maharashtra signed MoUs with four companies — NTPC, Adani Power, Reliance Industries and Lalitpur Power — on 19 May 2026 for 25,400 MW of nuclear capacity: ₹6.5 lakh crore investment and ~1.23 lakh projected jobs. 🏦🎓🏭⭐
  2. Delhi's IGI airport moved ahead with plans for a driverless air train connecting its terminals (May 2026). 🎓

📅 Important Days

  1. World Migratory Bird Day was observed on 9 May 2026 (second Saturday of May) — the UN's annual day highlighting migratory bird conservation. 🎓
