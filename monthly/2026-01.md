## 🎯 Top stories of the month

### Polity & Governance 🏛️
1. **77th Republic Day 2026 celebrated on Jan 26** — chief guests were EU leaders Ursula von der Leyen (European Commission President) and António Costa (European Council President); theme "150 Years of Vande Mataram"; first parade after Operation Sindoor; ~10,000 special guests and free Delhi Metro travel. 🎓⭐
2. **India–EU Free Trade Agreement concluded** — described as the "Mother of All Deals" at the 16th India–EU Summit (Jan 27, 2026); India's first-ever Defence & Security Partnership with the EU also signed, opening the EU's SAFE defence financing to Indian companies. 🎓⭐
3. **Census 2027 announced: two-phase, with caste enumeration** — Phase-I list of 33 questions released; reference date March 1, 2027 (Oct 1, 2026 for Ladakh and snowbound areas of J&K, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand); first census after 16 years. 🎓⭐
4. **India launches BRICS 2026 chairship** — fourth tenure (Jan–Dec 2026); logo, theme and website launched with theme "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability". 🎓

### Economy, Banking & Finance 🏦
5. **NSO First Advance Estimates: FY26 GDP growth at 7.4%** — real GDP at ₹201.90 lakh crore; nominal GDP growth of 8% to around ₹357 lakh crore; SBI's Ecowrap projected 7.5% with an upward bias. 🏦🎓⭐
6. **RBI's revised Integrated Ombudsman Scheme from July 1, 2026** — Central Complaints Processing Centre (CRPC); unresolved bank complaints auto-transfer to the Internal Ombudsman within 30 days; compensation up to ₹30 lakh. 🏦⭐
7. **SEBI approves PhonePe's ₹12,000 crore IPO** — via Offer for Sale with 45% UPI market share and zero fresh capital infusion; Juspay became India's first unicorn of 2026 at a $1.2 billion valuation. 🏦

### Science & Technology 🔬
8. **ISRO's PSLV-C62/EOS-N1 mission faces third-stage anomaly on Jan 12** — 64th PSLV flight carrying DRDO's strategic surveillance satellite Anvesha plus 15 co-passenger satellites (7 from Dhruva Space); mission data under analysis; NSIL's 9th dedicated commercial launch. 🎓⭐
9. **DRDO's 12-minute scramjet engine test** — sustained supersonic combustion at Mach 5+ speeds, advancing indigenous hypersonic propulsion. 🎓
10. **'PARAM Shakti' supercomputing facility inaugurated at IIT-Madras** — fully indigenous C-DAC PARAM RUDRA system delivering 3.1 petaflops. 🎓
11. **CSIR-NPL's National Environmental Standard Laboratory inaugurated (Jan 5)** — Asia's first and the world's second facility for testing and certifying air-pollution monitoring devices (supports the National Clean Air Programme). 🎓

### Environment & Ecology 🌿
12. **India becomes the world's first country to commercially produce bio-bitumen** — announced Jan 7 by Minister Nitin Gadkari; CSIR-developed technology converts paddy straw via pyrolysis; 15% blending can save ~₹4,500 crore in forex. 🎓⭐
13. **Rare red aurora over Hanle, Ladakh (Jan 19–20)** — triggered by an X-class solar flare and CME at 1,700 km/s; G4 geomagnetic and S4 solar radiation storm, the strongest since 2003; first well-documented red aurora from India. 🎓⭐

### Sports 🏅
14. **Vidarbha wins maiden Vijay Hazare Trophy 2025-26** — beat Saurashtra by 38 runs at Bengaluru; Atharva Taide scored 128 in the final; Aman Mokhade was Player of the Tournament with 814 runs. 🏦🎓
15. **Saina Nehwal retires after a 21-year career** — first Indian woman to reach World No. 1; 2012 London Olympics bronze medallist. 🎓
16. **Aaryan Varshney becomes India's 92nd chess Grandmaster** — earned his final norm at the Andranik Margaryan Memorial in Armenia; Virat Kohli crossed 28,068 international runs, becoming the second-highest run-scorer ever. 🎓

### Awards & Honours 🏆
17. **Padma Awards 2026: 131 honours** — 5 Padma Vibhushan, 13 Padma Bhushan, 113 Padma Shri announced on Republic Day eve (Jan 25); Maharashtra leads with 15 awardees. 🎓⭐
18. **Gallantry awards: 1 Ashoka Chakra and 3 Kirti Chakra** approved on Republic Day eve for exceptional bravery; Sikkim Police received the President's Police Colour — 15th state and 3rd from the North-East. 🎓

### Appointments & Resignations 📋
19. **Rakesh Aggarwal (IPS, 1994 batch) appointed Director General of NIA** — tenure till August 31, 2028. 🎓⭐
20. **Praveen Vashista (1991-batch IPS) appointed Central Vigilance Commissioner** for 4 years; Shatrujeet Singh Kapoor takes over ITBP and Praveen Kumar takes over BSF as DGs. 🎓
21. **Justice Revati Mohite Dere sworn in as Meghalaya HC's first woman Chief Justice**; Arijit Basu appointed Chairman of IndusInd Bank (effective Jan 31, succeeding Sunil Mehta). 🎓

### Defence 🛡️
22. **MoD clears ₹4,666 crore defence procurement** — ₹2,770 crore for close-quarter battle carbines and ₹1,896 crore for heavy-weight torpedoes. 🎓⭐
23. **DRDO's LR-AShM hypersonic long-range anti-ship missile debuts at the Republic Day parade** — 1,500 km range at Mach 5+ speeds. 🎓⭐
24. **ICGS 'Samudra Pratap' dedicated to the nation (Jan 5)** — India's first indigenous pollution-control vessel, built by Goa Shipyard Limited. 🎓

### International Relations 🌐
25. **US withdraws from 66 international bodies including India-led ISA (Jan 7)** — executive order covering IRENA, IPCC, IUCN, UNFCCC, UNFPA, UN Water under the "America First" review; the US also formally exited WHO after 78 years. 🎓⭐
26. **UN High Seas Treaty enters into force on Jan 17, 2026** — first legally binding treaty for oceans beyond national jurisdiction, ratified by 60+ countries. 🎓⭐
27. **German Chancellor Friedrich Merz visits India (Jan 12–13)** — Ahmedabad and Bengaluru; 19 strategic agreements covering defence tech, critical minerals, supply chains and clean energy; India–UAE agreed to double bilateral trade to $200 billion by 2032 with a new Strategic Defence Partnership. 🎓

### Government Schemes 📜
28. **Atal Pension Yojana extended till FY 2030-31** — 8.66 crore subscribers get guaranteed ₹1,000–₹5,000/month pensions after age 60; PFRDA introduced the NPS Swasthya Pension scheme under a regulatory sandbox. 🏦🎓⭐
29. **Market Access Support (MAS) scheme for MSME exports** — ₹4,531 crore outlay (FY26–31) for trade fairs, exhibitions and buyer–seller meets; Punjab's 'Mukhya Mantri Sehat Yojana' gives ₹10 lakh annual cashless cover to 65 lakh families. 🎓

### Reports & Indices 📊
30. **Responsible Nations Index launched (Jan 19) by ex-President Ram Nath Kovind** — India ranked 16th (score 0.551513); Singapore topped, ahead of France, the US and China. 🎓⭐
31. **NITI Aayog's 4th Export Preparedness Index 2024** — Maharashtra (68.01) leads large states, followed by Tamil Nadu (64.41) and Gujarat (64.02); Henley Passport Index 2026 put India at 80th (55 visa-free destinations). 🎓

### Books & Authors 📚
32. **'History That India Ignored' (2nd edition) by Prem Prakash** — launched in Panaji by Goa CM Pramod Sawant; revisits unsung freedom fighters, the INA, the Vellore Mutiny (1806) and the Battle of Imphal. 🎓
33. **55 scholarly works in classical languages released (Jan 6)** — by Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan, covering Kannada, Telugu, Malayalam, Odia and Tamil classics, with a 45-episode sign-language Tirukkural series. 🎓

### Obituaries 🕯️
34. **Maharashtra Deputy CM Ajit Pawar dies in an aircraft crash at Baramati on Jan 28, 2026** — aged 66; six-time deputy CM and NCP chief; 3-day state mourning declared. 🎓⭐
35. **Madhav Gadgil (83)** — father of modern Indian ecology and chairman of the Western Ghats Ecology Expert Panel, died Jan 8 in Pune; **Suresh Kalmadi (81)** — former Union Minister and IOA president (1996–2012), died Jan 6 in Pune. 🎓

### Important Days 📅
36. **Jan 9 — Pravasi Bharatiya Divas**; **Jan 12 — National Youth Day**; **Jan 15 — Army Day**; **Jan 16 — National Startup Day** (10 years of Startup India); **Jan 23 — Parakram Diwas**; **Jan 25 — National Voters Day**; **Jan 26 — Republic Day**; **Jan 30 — Martyrs' Day** (78th death anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi). 🎓

### State News 🗺️
37. **India's first sleeper Vande Bharat Express flagged off on Jan 17** — Howrah–Kamakhya (Guwahati), flagged off from Malda Town in West Bengal. 🎓⭐
38. **Kerala: India's first fully paperless district court system** inaugurated in Kalpetta, Wayanad (Jan 6, virtually by CJI Surya Kant); Kerala becomes the first state to formally recognise *Bacillus subtilis* as its state microbe. 🎓
