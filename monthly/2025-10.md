## 🎯 Top stories of the month

### Polity & Governance 🏛️
1. **Bihar Assembly election schedule announced (6 October)** — 243 seats; voting on 6 and 11 November (121 seats in phase I, 122 in phase II); counting on 14 November. 🎓⭐
2. **ECI mandates disclosure of AI-generated political content** — parties must clearly label synthetic audio and video during elections. 🎓
3. **Coal Ministry launches 'Koyla Shakti' smart analytics dashboard** for coal-sector monitoring. 🎓
4. **Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) reforms announced** — upgraded standards for special educators and rehabilitation professionals. 🎓

### Economy, Banking & Finance 🏦
5. **RBI keeps the repo rate at 5.5% (MPC, 1 October)** — a second consecutive pause with a neutral stance; FY26 GDP forecast raised to 6.8% (from 6.5%) and CPI forecast cut to 2.6% (from 3.1%); Governor Sanjay Malhotra. 🏦🎓⭐
6. **₹5,532 crore: seven electronics-component projects approved** under the Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme. 🏦🎓
7. **Andhra Pradesh Data Centre Advisory Council targets 6,000 MW capacity by 2030** — the state eyes a data-centre boom. 🏦
8. **India grants rare-earth magnet import licences to four firms** (including Hitachi and Continental India) sourcing from China — easing a critical supply choke. 🏦🎓

### Science & Technology 🔬
9. **NCBS and ICTS (Bengaluru) launch CALIBRE** — an AI-driven platform for biological research. 🎓
10. **China's CR450 hits 453 km/h in trials** — the fastest high-speed train prototype in the world. 🎓
11. **China unveils a transparent 'ghost' jellyfish drone** for deep-sea surveillance and reconnaissance. 🎓
12. **New coelacanth species identified in China** — an Early Triassic 'living fossil' from Anhui. 🎓

### Environment & Ecology 🌿
13. **FAO Global Forest Resources Assessment 2025: India 9th in forest area and 5th in carbon sinks** — also third in annual forest-area gain. 🎓⭐
14. **Gahirmatha seven-month fishing ban (1 November–31 May)** — Odisha's annual ban to protect Olive Ridley turtles. 🎓
15. **Uttarakhand to levy a green tax on outside-state vehicles** from December 2025. 🎓

### Sports 🏅
16. **Preetismita Bhoi wins gold with a world record** in the 44-kg weightlifting category at the 3rd Asian Youth Games — Odisha's rising star. 🎓⭐
17. **ICC Women's ODI World Cup 2025 begins (30 September)** — co-hosted by India and Sri Lanka. 🎓

### Awards & Honours 🏆
18. **Nobel Prizes 2025** — Medicine: Mary E. Brunkow, Fred Ramsdell and Shimon Sakaguchi (peripheral immune tolerance); Physics: John Clarke, Michel H. Devoret and John M. Martinis (macroscopic quantum tunnelling); Chemistry: Susumu Kitagawa, Richard Robson and Omar M. Yaghi (metal–organic frameworks); Literature: László Krasznahorkai; Peace: María Corina Machado (democratic rights in Venezuela); Economics: Joel Mokyr, Philippe Aghion and Peter Howitt (innovation-driven growth). 🎓⭐
19. **Swasth Nari Sashakt Parivar Abhiyaan enters the record books** — three Guinness World Records during the nationwide women's-health campaign. 🎓⭐
20. **South Korea's Grand Order of Mugunghwa conferred on US President Donald Trump** — Korea's highest civilian honour. 🎓
21. **UN Tourism names 52 Best Tourism Villages 2025** — no Indian destination makes the cut. 🎓

### Appointments & Resignations 📋
22. **Justice Surya Kant recommended as India's 53rd Chief Justice** — by incumbent CJI B.R. Gavai. 🎓⭐

### Defence 🛡️
23. **Cochin Shipyard delivers 'Mahe' to the Navy (24 October)** — the first indigenous Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft of her class. 🎓⭐
24. **Survey vessel 'Ikshak' to be commissioned at Kochi (6 November)** — the Navy's third ship of her survey class. 🎓
25. **Russia announces final testing of the Burevestnik nuclear-powered cruise missile** — claimed "unlimited range". 🎓
26. **HAL and Russia's UAC sign an MoU to manufacture the SJ-100 passenger aircraft in India.** 🎓

### International Relations 🌐
27. **US sanctions Colombian President Gustavo Petro (24 October)** — escalating the counternarcotics standoff. 🎓
28. **Bhutan to host the Global Peace Prayer Festival in Thimphu.** 🎓

### Government Schemes 📜
29. **Tourism 'Vision 2029'** — India to develop 50 world-class tourism destinations. 🎓⭐
30. **Tribal Freedom Fighters Museum** — India's first digital museum on tribal freedom fighters comes up in Chhattisgarh, ahead of the state's 25th foundation day (1 November). 🎓

### Reports & Indices 📊
31. **UNEP Adaptation Gap Report 2025 released.** 🎓⭐
32. **Global Hunger Index 2025: India ranks 102nd of 123 (score 25.8, 'serious')** — published by Concern Worldwide, Welthungerhilfe and IFHV. 🎓⭐

### Important Days 📅
- Gandhi Jayanti — 2 October
- Dhanteras — 18 October
- Diwali / Lakshmi Puja — 20 October
- Bhai Dooj — 23 October
- United Nations Day — 24 October
- National Unity Day — 31 October (Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel's birth anniversary; his 150th birth year)

### State News 🗺️
33. **Cyclone Montha batters the Andhra coast (28 October)** — a severe cyclonic storm making landfall near Kakinada with 90–100 kmph winds gusting to 110 kmph; crops over 38,000+ hectares damaged. 🎓⭐
34. **Kerala proposes India's first underwater tunnel** between Vypin and Fort Kochi. 🎓
35. **Assam–Nagaland border violence (2 October)** — around 100 houses torched in communal clashes. 🎓
36. **Nagaland nominates Dzükou Valley** for the 50-destination tourism development programme. 🎓
