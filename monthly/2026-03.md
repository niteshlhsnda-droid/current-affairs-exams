## 🎯 Top stories of the month

### Polity & Governance 🏛️
1. **Gujarat becomes 2nd state in India to pass a Uniform Civil Code** — the Assembly passed the UCC Bill in March 2026, following Uttarakhand's Jan 2025 rollout; traces to Article 44 (DPSP). 🎓⭐
2. **Union Cabinet approves revamped UDAN (RCS–Modified)** — Rs 28,840 crore outlay for FY27–FY36, targeting ~120 new destinations and 40 million passengers, focused on Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities. 🎓⭐
3. **Cabinet extends IVFRT scheme till March 2031** — Rs 1,800 crore outlay for the Immigration, Visa, Foreigners Registration & Tracking scheme; a Mission Mode Project under NeGP, implemented by MHA through the Bureau of Immigration, aligned with the Immigration and Foreigners Act, 2025. 🎓
4. **India's CCTV rules shift from April 1, 2026** — sale of non-certified internet-connected CCTV cameras banned, largely affecting Chinese-linked firms; a major regulatory reset of the surveillance equipment market. 🎓🏦

### Economy, Banking & Finance 🏦
5. **RBI imposes Rs 2+ crore penalties on UBI, Central Bank, BoI & Pine Labs** — Rs 95.40 lakh on Union Bank of India, Rs 63.60 lakh on Central Bank of India, Rs 58.50 lakh on Bank of India and Rs 3.10 lakh on Pine Labs for regulatory non-compliance. 🏦⭐
6. **S&P Global lifts India's FY27 GDP forecast to 7.1%** — raised the growth outlook for the Indian economy. 🏦🎓⭐
7. **SEBI launches 'Verified App Label' initiative** — launched by Chairperson Tuhin Kanta Pandey to protect investors against fraudulent mobile apps and ensure safe digital investing. 🏦
8. **CCI approves MUFG Bank's 20% stake acquisition in Shriram Finance for Rs 39,618 crore** — Japan's MUFG proposal cleared. 🏦
9. **Nagaland records highest GST growth — 37% in FY25-26** (up to Dec 2025), far above the national average of 6.8%. 🏦🎓
10. **PLFS: India's unemployment rate declines to 4.9%** in February 2026. 🏦🎓
11. **Banking-awareness notes** — RBI wins 'Initiative of the Year' award for the '.bank.in' domain rollout; NBSL (NPCI BHIM Services Ltd) appoints **M.S. Dhoni** as brand ambassador for the BHIM payments app. 🏦

### Science & Technology 🔬
12. **India's first hydrogen train completes oscillation trials** — RDSO conducted trials on the Jind–Sonipat route in Haryana; the trainset was built by ICF Chennai and covered 20 km at a maximum speed of 70 kmph. 🎓🏭⭐
13. **Agnikul Cosmos test-fires world's first fully 3D-printed booster engine 'Agnite'** in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. 🎓⭐
14. **GoI approves 23 institutions to establish 'Quantum Labs'** under the National Quantum Mission. 🎓
15. **ISV Kaundinya returns to Mumbai from Muscat** — the Indian Navy's traditionally constructed stitched sailing vessel, showcasing ancient Indian maritime heritage. 🎓

### Environment & Ecology 🌿
16. **Cabinet approves India's NDC targets for 2031–35** — 47% emissions-intensity reduction by 2035 (vs 2005), 60% of installed power capacity from non-fossil sources by 2035, and an additional 3.5–4 billion tonnes of carbon sink through forest and tree cover. 🎓⭐

### Sports 🏅
17. **Hockey India Annual Awards 2025** — Navneet Kaur & Hardik Singh named Players of the Year; Zafar Iqbal receives the Lifetime Achievement award. 🎓
18. **India to host World Indoor Athletics Championships 2028** in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. 🎓

### Awards & Honours 🏆
19. **Saraswati Samman 2025 — Bengali writer Ramkumar Mukhopadhyay** selected for the prestigious literary honour. 🎓⭐
20. **Stockholm Water Prize 2026 — Professor Kaveh Madani** named the laureate. 🎓⭐

### Appointments & Resignations 📋
21. **Sadhvi Niranjan Jyoti assumes charge as Chairperson of NCBC** (National Commission for Backward Classes). 🎓
22. **Lt Gen Dhiraj Seth to assume charge as Vice Chief of Army Staff** on 1 April 2026. 🛡️

### Defence 🛡️
23. **INS Nilgiri joins Exercise Kakadu 2026** — the Indian Navy frigate took part in the sea phase of the Australia-hosted multilateral maritime exercise in the Western Pacific; its crew also won the Kakadu Shield Trophy at Darwin. 🛡️🎓⭐
24. **MoD signs Rs 858 crore contracts** — Rs 445 crore with Russia's Rosoboronexport for Tunguska air defence missile systems (Army) and Rs 413 crore with Boeing India for depot-level inspection of P-8I maritime reconnaissance aircraft (Navy). 🛡️
25. **DAC grants AoN for defence proposals worth ~Rs 2.38 lakh crore** for the Army, IAF and Coast Guard, chaired by Defence Minister Rajnath Singh. 🛡️
26. **Indian Army begins fielding 'Shaurya Squadrons'** — dedicated drone subunits embedded in armoured regiments (20–30 personnel each, mix of surveillance, attack, swarm, FPV and loitering munitions); plans to equip all 67 armoured regiments. 🛡️🎓

### International Relations 🌐
27. **India, EU sign aviation pact** — DGCA–EASA working arrangement ahead of the EU-South Asia Aviation Partnership workshop; includes Airbus H125/AS350 helicopter assembly in Bengaluru. 🎓
28. **UN launches task force to safeguard Strait of Hormuz trade** — announced by UNSG Antonio Guterres amid the West Asia conflict; led by UNOPS Executive Director Jorge Moreira da Silva (UNCTAD, IMO, ICC as members), with Jean Arnault handling political management. 🎓⭐
29. **PM Modi announces 7 Empowered Groups + 'RELIEF' scheme** — the empowered groups address the long-term impact of the West Asia conflict (post Operation Epic Fury, the Feb 28 US–Israel strikes on Iran); RELIEF supports Indian exporters hit by the conflict. 🎓🏦

### Government Schemes 📜
30. **Gujarat launches herSTART 5.0** — CM Bhupendra Patel launched the 5th edition of the GUSEC-run women entrepreneurs programme at Gujarat University, Ahmedabad; a new research park was inaugurated and the convention centre named Vidyagauri Nilkanth Sabhapuram. 🎓⭐
31. **BPCL launches India's first LPG ATM** in Gurugram, Haryana. 🎓⭐
32. **Delhi government schemes** — Lakhpati Bitiya, Saheli Pink Smart Card and free LPG cylinders for women, rolled out alongside Delhi's FY27 budget of Rs 1.03 lakh crore. 🎓
33. **Meghalaya becomes 12th state to sign JJM 2.0 reforms MoU** — with ~83% rural tap coverage; the Jal Jeevan Mission deadline was extended to December 2028 with a Rs 1.51 lakh crore outlay (Rs 67,300 crore in FY26). 🎓

35. **QS World University Rankings by Subject 2026** — IIT-ISM Dhanbad ranks 21st globally in Engineering–Mineral & Mining; IIM Ahmedabad ranks 21st in both Business & Management Studies and Marketing (India's debut in Marketing); India has 99 ranked institutions. 🎓⭐
