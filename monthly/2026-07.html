📰 Current Affairs — July 2026

*Monthly exam-ready digest for Bank • UPSC • PSU aspirants, compiled from

GKToday, Insights IAS, Drishti IAS, Testbook & AffairsCloud.*

Tags: 🏦 Bank • 🎓 UPSC • 🏭 PSU • ⭐ high-yield

🎯 Top stories of the month

Polity & Governance 🏛️

  1. Monsoon Session of Parliament begins on 21 July 2026 — Union Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju seeks cooperation of all political parties for smooth functioning of both Houses. 🎓⭐
  2. NITI Aayog launches bioeconomy roadmap to 2035 — a 10-year roadmap targeting a USD 691 billion bioeconomy by 2035, launched at the National Media Centre, New Delhi. 🎓⭐
  3. UIDAI enables Aadhaar email-ID updates via app — holders can add/update their email ID on the official Aadhaar app, no Seva Kendra visit needed. 🎓
  4. NCW launches 24×7 Women Helpline 14490 — toll-free line for immediate assistance, complaint registration, psychological counselling and grievance redressal. 🎓
  5. India to chair the 9th session of UNCTAD's Intergovernmental Group of Experts (IGE) on Consumer Protection Law and Policy. 🎓
  6. 125th birth anniversary of Dr. Syama Prasad Mookerjee commemorated on 6 July 2026. 🎓

Economy, Banking & Finance 🏦

  1. India–UK CETA comes into force on 15 July 2026 — the "gold standard" free trade deal under which the UK eliminates or reduces tariffs on 98.8% of its tariff lines; the Double Contribution Convention (DCC) also takes effect. 🏦🎓🏭⭐
  2. Retail inflation (CPI) rises to 4.38% in June 2026 — the first print above RBI's 4% target since January 2025; the CPI base year is revised to 2024 = 100 (from 2012 = 100). 🏦⭐
  3. Gross GST collections hit ₹1,94,812 crore in June 2026 — up 13.9% year-on-year, the fourth straight month above ₹1.9 lakh crore. 🏦
  4. Forex reserves at $675.1 billion as of 10 July 2026. 🏦

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. PM Modi inaugurates CG Semi's OSAT facility at Sanand, Gujarat — a semiconductor assembly & test milestone in India's chip-manufacturing journey. 🎓🏭⭐
  2. CSIR-NAL completes design of the indigenous Saras MkII (8 July) — a 19-seater twin-turboprop for regional connectivity under UDAN, with pressurised cabin, glass cockpit and command-by-wire controls; maiden test flight planned for December 2027; IAF projects a need of at least 15 aircraft; HAL supplies metallic components. 🎓⭐
  3. India's first two 1,000-metre geothermal wells commissioned — inaugurated by LG V.K. Saxena in Jammu & Kashmir. 🎓

Environment & Ecology 🌿

  1. Gujarat builds its first air-filled rubber dams with South Korean technology — at Rajvasana on the Heran river, Chhota Udepur (₹82.97 cr, ~75% complete) and Pathakwadi on the Ambika river, Tapi (₹79.13 cr, ~90% complete); combined investment over ₹162 crore; SCADA-controlled with ~30-year rubber-bladder life. 🎓

Sports 🏅

  1. Spain win the FIFA World Cup 2026 — beat Argentina 1-0 after extra time on 19 July at MetLife Stadium, New Jersey (Ferran Torres, 106th minute); 2nd title (first since 2010); first country to hold the men's and women's titles simultaneously; Kylian Mbappé takes the Golden Boot. 🎓⭐
  2. Wimbledon 2026 — Jannik Sinner defends the men's singles title (beat Alexander Zverev 6-7, 7-6, 6-3, 6-4 on 12 July); Linda Nosková wins the women's singles (beat Karolína Muchová 6-2, 5-7, 6-3 on 11 July); men's doubles Heliövaara/Patten; women's doubles Guo Hanyu/Mladenovic; mixed doubles Arévalo/Ostapenko. 🎓⭐
  3. England win the ODI series 2-1 against India — Ben Duckett's 141 at Lord's on 19 July is a record ODI innings at the venue (beating Viv Richards' 138* in the 1979 World Cup final); England's first bilateral ODI series win over India since 2018; Rohit Sharma scores 138 for India. 🎓
  4. England's first-ever T20 series win over India — 4-0 (the opener at Chester-le-Street was washed out); India bowled out for 76 at Trent Bridge; final T20I at Southampton on 12 July. 🎓
  5. Ireland whitewash India 2-0 in a T20 series in early July, before the England tour. 🎓

Awards & Honours 🏆

  1. PM Modi conferred 'Bintang Adipurna' — Indonesia's highest civilian honour, by President Prabowo Subianto in Jakarta on 7 July, for strengthening India–Indonesia relations; Modi dedicates it to the people of India. 🎓⭐
  2. International Booker Prize to be renamed the Bukhman International Booker Prize from 2027 — a 10-year funding deal with Bukhman Philanthropies; prize money doubles to £100,000, shared equally between author and translator. 🎓⭐
  3. Samriddh Gram wins a global WSIS prize — the BharatNet-powered rural digital-hub initiative (village "phygital" Samriddhi Kendras) is the global winner among 18 award categories after 2.2 million votes; recognised by the ITU for Digital Public Infrastructure leadership. 🎓

Appointments & Resignations 📋

  1. New service chiefs take charge on 1 July — Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit becomes Vice Chief of the Air Staff; Air Marshal Jasvir Singh Mann takes over as AOC-in-C, Southern Air Command; Air Marshal Tejinder Singh becomes Chief of Integrated Defence Staff (CISC); Lt Gen Rajesh Pushkar takes charge of the Army's Southern Command. 🎓⭐
  2. Satya Pal Kumar appointed Director of FIU-India (25 July) — IRS officer, in a major bureaucratic reshuffle. 🎓
  3. Lokhande Prashant Sitaram appointed Additional Secretary, Home Ministry (2 July) — 2001-batch AGMUT-cadre IAS officer; retains the CBSE Chairperson post as additional charge. 🎓
  4. Sanjeev Jain appointed India's Ambassador to North Korea (6 July) — IFS officer, currently India's Ambassador to Cabo Verde. 🎓

Defence 🛡️

  1. DAC approves ~₹52,000 crore Acceptance of Necessity (3 July) — chaired by Defence Minister Rajnath Singh; includes the AKASH TARANG anti-UAV electronic warfare system for the Army. 🎓⭐
  2. DRDO conducts the maiden flight-test of 'Kusha' LR-SAM (23 July) — the indigenous long-range surface-to-air missile, tested from Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Island, Odisha. 🎓⭐
  3. IAF joins Exercise Pitch Black 2026 in Australia (20 July – 7 August) — the biennial multinational air exercise with 4 Rafales, 2 C-17 Globemasters and 120+ Air Warriors; ~100 aircraft and 20 nations in northern Australia. 🎓

International Relations 🌐

  1. PM Modi's three-nation tour: Indonesia, Australia, New Zealand (7–11 July) — the first Indian PM visit to New Zealand in 40 years; ties elevated to a Strategic Partnership with a 'Roadmap to 2030'; target to double bilateral trade to ₹35,000 crore by 2030; FTA talks to be fast-tracked. 🎓⭐
  2. India–Mali Forum for the Promotion of Exports launched in Bamako — the first institutionalised bilateral platform for India–Mali trade cooperation. 🎓

Government Schemes 📜

  1. Delhi approves the Parivartan Scheme (21 July) — the MoRTH flagship to replace BS-IV and older commercial vehicles with BS-VI or electric vehicles in the NCR; 10-year motor-vehicle tax exemption, registration-fee waiver, 5% interest subsidy; ~2.7 lakh truck and bus operators expected to benefit. 🏦🎓
  2. West Bengal approves the PM Cha Shramik Protsahan Yojana strategy (7 July) — a ₹313.3-crore assistance package for tea garden workers in North Bengal. 🎓
  3. Delhi government launches Mission Kayakalp — a comprehensive initiative to modernise government-school infrastructure. 🎓

Books & Authors 📚

  1. "The Voice of Justice: Justice Gavai Speaks" (ed. Prof. S. Sivakumar) — released by Vice-President C.P. Radhakrishnan at Uprashtrapati Bhavan on 14 July; published by Thomson Reuters with the Commonwealth Legal Education Association; compiles former CJI B.R. Gavai's speeches on constitutional governance. 🎓
  2. "RSS @100: A Century of Service, Unity & Sacrifice" by Shyam Jaju and Anupam Trivedi — released by Vice-President Radhakrishnan on 17 July, marking the RSS centenary. 🎓

Obituaries 🕯️

  1. Sir Garfield Sobers (18 July, age 89) — West Indies all-rounder (1954–74), regarded among cricket's greatest; knighted for services to the game. 🎓
  2. Former Qatar Emir Sheikh Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani (13 July, age 74). 🎓
