📰 Current Affairs — October 2025

*Monthly exam-ready digest for Bank • UPSC • PSU aspirants, compiled from

GKToday, Insights IAS, Drishti IAS, Testbook & AffairsCloud.*

Tags: 🏦 Bank • 🎓 UPSC • 🏭 PSU • ⭐ high-yield

🎯 Top stories of the month

Polity & Governance 🏛️

  1. Bihar Assembly election schedule announced (6 October) — 243 seats; voting on 6 and 11 November (121 seats in phase I, 122 in phase II); counting on 14 November. 🎓⭐
  2. ECI mandates disclosure of AI-generated political content — parties must clearly label synthetic audio and video during elections. 🎓
  3. Coal Ministry launches 'Koyla Shakti' smart analytics dashboard for coal-sector monitoring. 🎓
  4. Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) reforms announced — upgraded standards for special educators and rehabilitation professionals. 🎓

Economy, Banking & Finance 🏦

  1. RBI keeps the repo rate at 5.5% (MPC, 1 October) — a second consecutive pause with a neutral stance; FY26 GDP forecast raised to 6.8% (from 6.5%) and CPI forecast cut to 2.6% (from 3.1%); Governor Sanjay Malhotra. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. ₹5,532 crore: seven electronics-component projects approved under the Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme. 🏦🎓
  3. Andhra Pradesh Data Centre Advisory Council targets 6,000 MW capacity by 2030 — the state eyes a data-centre boom. 🏦
  4. India grants rare-earth magnet import licences to four firms (including Hitachi and Continental India) sourcing from China — easing a critical supply choke. 🏦🎓

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. NCBS and ICTS (Bengaluru) launch CALIBRE — an AI-driven platform for biological research. 🎓
  2. China's CR450 hits 453 km/h in trials — the fastest high-speed train prototype in the world. 🎓
  3. China unveils a transparent 'ghost' jellyfish drone for deep-sea surveillance and reconnaissance. 🎓
  4. New coelacanth species identified in China — an Early Triassic 'living fossil' from Anhui. 🎓

Environment & Ecology 🌿

  1. FAO Global Forest Resources Assessment 2025: India 9th in forest area and 5th in carbon sinks — also third in annual forest-area gain. 🎓⭐
  2. Gahirmatha seven-month fishing ban (1 November–31 May) — Odisha's annual ban to protect Olive Ridley turtles. 🎓
  3. Uttarakhand to levy a green tax on outside-state vehicles from December 2025. 🎓

Sports 🏅

  1. Preetismita Bhoi wins gold with a world record in the 44-kg weightlifting category at the 3rd Asian Youth Games — Odisha's rising star. 🎓⭐
  2. ICC Women's ODI World Cup 2025 begins (30 September) — co-hosted by India and Sri Lanka. 🎓

Awards & Honours 🏆

  1. Nobel Prizes 2025 — Medicine: Mary E. Brunkow, Fred Ramsdell and Shimon Sakaguchi (peripheral immune tolerance); Physics: John Clarke, Michel H. Devoret and John M. Martinis (macroscopic quantum tunnelling); Chemistry: Susumu Kitagawa, Richard Robson and Omar M. Yaghi (metal–organic frameworks); Literature: László Krasznahorkai; Peace: María Corina Machado (democratic rights in Venezuela); Economics: Joel Mokyr, Philippe Aghion and Peter Howitt (innovation-driven growth). 🎓⭐
  2. Swasth Nari Sashakt Parivar Abhiyaan enters the record books — three Guinness World Records during the nationwide women's-health campaign. 🎓⭐
  3. South Korea's Grand Order of Mugunghwa conferred on US President Donald Trump — Korea's highest civilian honour. 🎓
  4. UN Tourism names 52 Best Tourism Villages 2025 — no Indian destination makes the cut. 🎓

Appointments & Resignations 📋

  1. Justice Surya Kant recommended as India's 53rd Chief Justice — by incumbent CJI B.R. Gavai. 🎓⭐

Defence 🛡️

  1. Cochin Shipyard delivers 'Mahe' to the Navy (24 October) — the first indigenous Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft of her class. 🎓⭐
  2. Survey vessel 'Ikshak' to be commissioned at Kochi (6 November) — the Navy's third ship of her survey class. 🎓
  3. Russia announces final testing of the Burevestnik nuclear-powered cruise missile — claimed "unlimited range". 🎓
  4. HAL and Russia's UAC sign an MoU to manufacture the SJ-100 passenger aircraft in India. 🎓

International Relations 🌐

  1. US sanctions Colombian President Gustavo Petro (24 October) — escalating the counternarcotics standoff. 🎓
  2. Bhutan to host the Global Peace Prayer Festival in Thimphu. 🎓

Government Schemes 📜

  1. Tourism 'Vision 2029' — India to develop 50 world-class tourism destinations. 🎓⭐
  2. Tribal Freedom Fighters Museum — India's first digital museum on tribal freedom fighters comes up in Chhattisgarh, ahead of the state's 25th foundation day (1 November). 🎓

Reports & Indices 📊

  1. UNEP Adaptation Gap Report 2025 released. 🎓⭐
  2. Global Hunger Index 2025: India ranks 102nd of 123 (score 25.8, 'serious') — published by Concern Worldwide, Welthungerhilfe and IFHV. 🎓⭐

Important Days 📅

  • Gandhi Jayanti — 2 October
  • Dhanteras — 18 October
  • Diwali / Lakshmi Puja — 20 October
  • Bhai Dooj — 23 October
  • United Nations Day — 24 October
  • National Unity Day — 31 October (Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel's birth anniversary; his 150th birth year)

State News 🗺️

  1. Cyclone Montha batters the Andhra coast (28 October) — a severe cyclonic storm making landfall near Kakinada with 90–100 kmph winds gusting to 110 kmph; crops over 38,000+ hectares damaged. 🎓⭐
  2. Kerala proposes India's first underwater tunnel between Vypin and Fort Kochi. 🎓
  3. Assam–Nagaland border violence (2 October) — around 100 houses torched in communal clashes. 🎓
  4. Nagaland nominates Dzükou Valley for the 50-destination tourism development programme. 🎓
