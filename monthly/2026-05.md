## 🎯 Top stories of May 2026

### 🏛️ Polity & Governance
1. **Suvendu Adhikari sworn in as West Bengal Chief Minister on 9 May 2026** — ended 15 years of Trinamool Congress rule and formed the state's first-ever BJP-led government after a landslide win. 🎓⭐
2. **V. D. Satheesan sworn in as Kerala Chief Minister on 18 May 2026** — the UDF returned to power after a decade, winning 102 of 140 seats, with the Congress alone on 63. 🎓⭐
3. **NEET-UG 2026 (held 3 May) was cancelled by the NTA on 12 May 2026** over alleged paper-leak and irregularity findings; the matter was referred to the CBI and a fresh exam was ordered (held 21 June). 🎓⭐
4. **The Supreme Court permitted the euthanasia of rabid, incurably ill and demonstrably dangerous stray dogs** in its May 2026 ruling on stray-dog management. 🎓

### 💰 Economy, Banking & Finance
5. **BIS notified IS 19850:2026 (Gazette, 18 May; effective 15 May 2026)** — India's first fuel-quality standard for petrol blends beyond E20, covering E22, E25, E27 and E30. 🎓🏭⭐
6. **Mahindra & Mahindra and DBS Bank rolled out a sustainability-linked dealer financing programme** in May 2026 — dealer credit terms tied to sustainability performance. 🏦🏭

### 🔬 Science & Technology
7. **Sweden joined ISRO's Shukrayaan Venus orbiter programme** — the mission will carry 19 payloads to study Venus. 🎓
8. **The IAF signed contracts with IIT Bombay for predictive maintenance of the Su-30MKI fleet** — using AI-driven health monitoring of fighter aircraft. 🎓
9. **The Indian Navy selected Tonbo Imaging to develop a high-power microwave (HPM) system** — a directed-energy capability against drones and electronic threats. 🎓
10. **Squadron Leader Saanya became the IAF's first woman Category-A flying instructor** in May 2026. 🎓

### 🌿 Environment & Ecology
11. **The IEA released the Global Methane Tracker 2026** in May 2026 — the annual assessment of methane emissions from the energy sector. 🎓

### 🏅 Sports
12. **Royal Challengers Bengaluru won IPL 2026 on 31 May** — defeating Gujarat Titans by five wickets at Ahmedabad; Rajat Patidar captained the side and Virat Kohli starred with an unbeaten 75. 🏦🎓⭐
13. **Marta Kostyuk won the Madrid Open women's singles title on 2 May 2026** — defeating Mirra Andreeva 6–3, 7–5 in the final. 🎓
14. **Kuldeep Kumar set a national pole-vault record of 5.41 m** in May 2026. 🎓

### 🏆 Awards & Honours

16. **Parveen Shaikh won the 2026 Whitley Award** for her conservation work on the Indian skimmer. 🎓

### 👤 Appointments & Resignations
17. **Prasoon Joshi was appointed Chairman of Prasar Bharati on 2 May 2026** (PIB). 🎓⭐
18. **Lt Gen N. S. Raja Subramani was appointed India's third Chief of Defence Staff** — he assumed charge on 31 May 2026, also serving as Secretary, Department of Military Affairs. 🎓⭐

### 🛡️ Defence
19. **DRDO and the Indian Navy conducted the maiden salvo launch of NASM-SR** (Naval Anti-Ship Missile–Short Range) in early May 2026 — two missiles fired in quick succession from a naval helicopter off the Odisha coast, demonstrating waterline-hit capability. 🎓⭐
20. **An advanced Agni missile with MIRV technology was successfully flight-tested on 8 May 2026** from Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island, with multiple payloads directed at spatially separated targets. 🎓⭐
21. **Agni-1 was successfully test-launched on 22 May 2026** from ITR Chandipur under the Strategic Forces Command (PIB). 🎓
22. **India received its fourth S-400 (Triumf) air-defence squadron** from Russia in May 2026. 🎓
23. **The US approved Apache helicopter and M777 howitzer support deals for India** worth a combined $428.2 million in May 2026. 🎓
24. **Bharat Forge tied up with Andhra Pradesh for a marine gas-turbine manufacturing complex** in May 2026. 🎓🏭

### 🌐 International Relations
25. **India and the US signed a bilateral framework on critical minerals and rare earths on 26 May 2026** in New Delhi — signed by S. Jaishankar and Marco Rubio during the Quad Foreign Ministers' meeting, covering mining, processing, recycling and investment. 🎓⭐
26. **India and Vietnam signed MoUs on AI and quantum technology cooperation** in May 2026. 🎓
27. **Russia and Afghanistan signed a military-technical cooperation pact** in May 2026. 🎓
28. **Russia agreed to build Kazakhstan's first nuclear power plant** — estimated at $16.5 billion — in May 2026. 🎓
29. **The UN added Israeli and Russian forces to its blacklist of parties credibly suspected of conflict-related sexual violence** in May 2026. 🎓

### 🗺️ State News
30. **Maharashtra signed MoUs with four companies — NTPC, Adani Power, Reliance Industries and Lalitpur Power — on 19 May 2026** for 25,400 MW of nuclear capacity: ₹6.5 lakh crore investment and ~1.23 lakh projected jobs. 🏦🎓🏭⭐
31. **Delhi's IGI airport moved ahead with plans for a driverless air train** connecting its terminals (May 2026). 🎓
