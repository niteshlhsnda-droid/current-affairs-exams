## 🎯 Top stories of the month

### Polity & Governance 🏛️
1. **Union Budget 2026-27 presented on Feb 1** — Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman's 9th consecutive budget, themed on 3 Kartavyas (accelerate growth, aspirations, access to all); fiscal deficit targeted at 4.3% of GDP; capex ₹12.2 lakh crore; first budget presented in Kartavya Bhawan. 🏦🎓⭐
2. **New Income Tax Act 2025 takes effect from April 2026** — TCS on overseas tour packages cut to 2%, LRS education/medical TCS to 2%; STT on futures raised to 0.05% and options to 0.15%; MAT cut to 14% as final tax; tax holiday till 2047 for foreign cloud-service companies. 🏦🎓⭐
3. **16th Finance Commission retains states' vertical devolution at 41% (2026-31)** — announced Feb 3; urban local body grants rise 230% to ₹3.56 trillion; ₹1.4 lakh crore announced for states in the budget. 🎓⭐
4. **India AI Impact Summit (Feb 16–20, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi)** — first global AI summit in the Global South; 3 Sutras (People, Planet, Progress) and 7 Chakras; New Delhi Declaration endorsed by 89 countries; Guinness World Record with 2,50,946 responsible-AI pledges in 24 hours. 🎓⭐

### Economy, Banking & Finance 🏦
5. **National Monetisation Pipeline 2.0 targets ₹16.72 lakh crore (FY25-26 to FY29-30)** — plus ₹1 lakh crore Urban Challenge Fund for city growth and ₹10,000 crore Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0. 🏦🎓⭐
6. **India–US interim trade agreement signed** — reciprocal tariffs cut from 25% to 18%; India to buy $500 billion of goods over 5 years; US lifts additional tariffs after India pledges to stop Russian oil imports. 🏦🎓⭐
7. **RBI package: KCC revised guidelines (6-year loan tenure, 20% additional credit); ECB limits raised from $750M to $1B**; digital payment agent norms (IIBF certification, 8AM–7PM, from Jul 1); RBI-DPI index at 516.76; **GDP & IIP base year moves to 2022-23 and CPI to 2024** (food weight 45.86% → 36.75%); New India Co-operative Bank merged into Saraswat Bank. 🏦

### Science & Technology 🔬
8. **Chandrayaan-4 landing site identified: Mons Mouton-4** — on the Moon's south pole for India's crewed lunar mission, announced at the 111th Indian Science Congress. 🎓⭐
9. **Two world-class telescopes sanctioned for Ladakh** — 2-metre National Large Solar Telescope at Pangong Tso and 13.7-metre National Large Optical-Near Infrared Telescope at Hanle. 🎓
10. **UIDAI launches 'Invisible Shield' biometric deduplication engine**; DigiLocker partners Google to map 60,000 Aadhaar centres; Tata–OpenAI announce the Stargate India data centre (100 MW scaling to 1 GW, $7 billion Tata investment). 🎓

### Environment & Ecology 🌿
11. **India's Ramsar tally reaches 98** — Patna Bird Sanctuary (UP) as 97th and Chhari-Dhand Conservation Reserve (Gujarat) as 98th on World Wetlands Day (Feb 2); theme 'Wetlands and Traditional Knowledge: Celebrating Cultural Heritage'. 🎓⭐
12. **E20 (20% ethanol-blended petrol) mandate from April 1, 2026** — India ranks 2nd globally in LEED-certified green building space; NHAI launches 'Bee Corridors' with 40 lakh native trees along highways by FY27. 🎓

### Sports 🏅
13. **India wins 6th ICC U-19 World Cup** — beats England in the final; Vaibhav Suryavanshi scores 175 and bags the Player of the Tournament award. 🎓⭐
14. **Milano Cortina Winter Olympics (Feb 6–22, Italy)** — Norway topped the medal table with a record gold-medal haul; India represented by Arif Mohammad Khan and Stanzin Lundup. 🎓⭐
15. **Australian Open 2026: Carlos Alcaraz beats Novak Djokovic** (men's) and Elena Rybakina beats Aryna Sabalenka (women's); Rybakina ends a 15-month title drought. 🏦🎓
16. **WPL 2026: RCB beat Delhi Capitals chasing 204** — RCB's second title; at the men's T20 World Cup 2026, India beat Pakistan (Ishan Kishan 77) and Rashid Khan became the 3rd bowler with 700 T20 wickets. 🏦🎓
17. **Domestic milestones** — J&K reaches its first-ever Ranji Trophy final; Paras Dogra is the fastest to 10,000 Ranji runs (147 innings); India tops the Asian Shooting Championship with 94 medals (51G/23S/20B); Harmanpreet Kaur becomes the most-capped woman cricketer (356 matches); Smriti Mandhana wins BBC Indian Sportswoman of the Year 2025. 🏦🎓

### Awards & Honours 🏆
18. **Grammy Awards 2026** — Song of the Year: Billie Eilish's *Wildflower*; Album of the Year: Bad Bunny's *Debí Tirar Más Fotos*; Best New Artist: Olivia Dean; Lady Gaga's *Mayhem*; Dalai Lama wins his first Grammy for *Meditations*; Huntr/x's *Golden* marks the first K-pop win. 🎓
19. **'Boong' becomes the first Indian film to win a BAFTA** — Best Children's & Family Film at the 79th British Academy Film Awards (Manipur). 🎓⭐
20. **Crafoord Prize 2026 (Geosciences) to Veerabhadran Ramanathan** — for pioneering work on atmospheric aerosols and climate change; Bhavya Bharat Bhushan Awards 2026 announced (Nambi Narayanan, Saina Nehwal, Vikram Sampath, Alarmel Valli, N. Rajam and the Armed Forces for Operation Sindoor). 🎓

### Appointments & Resignations 📋
21. **Tarique Rahman becomes Bangladesh's 11th Prime Minister (Feb 17)** — BNP wins 212/299 seats. 🎓⭐
22. **Nidhi Chhibber (IAS, 1994) gets additional charge as NITI Aayog CEO** (Feb 24, 2026); Deepak Gupta appointed GAIL CMD; Uday Kotak appointed GIFT City Chairman; Vinay Tonse joins YES Bank as MD & CEO; R. Vijay Anandh takes City Union Bank; Rohit Rishi joins IIFCL as MD. 🏦🎓
23. **Historic PM transitions abroad** — Sanae Takaichi becomes Japan's first woman PM (snap-election landslide); Rob Jetten becomes Netherlands' youngest PM (38); Anutin Charnvirakul becomes Thailand PM; Mia Mottley wins a 3rd consecutive term in Barbados. 🎓

### Defence 🛡️
24. **DAC approves 114 Rafale jets for ₹3.25 lakh crore** — largest-ever defence procurement; 18 fly-away + 96 Make-in-India; IAF's 7th and 8th squadrons at Ambala and Hasimara. 🎓⭐
25. **MILAN 2026 (Feb 15–25, Visakhapatnam) hosts 74 nations** — largest edition; International Fleet Review on Feb 18; India assumes IONS chairmanship (2026–28). 🎓
26. **Joint exercises and firepower** — KHANJAR-XIII (India–Kyrgyzstan, Missamari, Feb 4–17); DHARMA GUARDIAN 7th (India–Japan, Chaubattia, Feb 24–Mar 9); VAJRA PRAHAR 16th (India–US, Bakloh, Feb 23–Mar 15); IAF's Vayushakti-26 at Pokhran (Feb 27); INS Anjadip (3rd ASW-SWC, GRSE) commissioned; CTS 'Krishna' launched at L&T Kattupalli. 🎓

### International Relations 🌐
27. **India–France ties elevated to Special Global Strategic Partnership** — during Macron's February 2026 visit; 2026 declared the Year of Innovation; India–Israel upgraded to a Special Strategic Partnership (Modi's visit; India–Israel FTA talks Feb 23–26). 🎓⭐
28. **India formally joins Pax Silica Coalition as 12th member** — US-led critical-minerals initiative; India–GCC sign Terms of Reference to restart FTA negotiations. 🎓
29. **Summit diplomacy** — PM Modi visits Malaysia (first bilateral in a decade); Seychelles President Herminie's first foreign visit to India (SESEL vision, $175M package); Brazil's Lula visits Feb 18–22 ($30B trade target by 2030); China records a 3 million population decline in 2025; South Korea's ex-President Yoon Suk Yeol sentenced to life imprisonment. 🎓

### Government Schemes 📜
30. **National Household Income Survey from April 2026** — MoSPI's first-ever NHIS with digital CAPI, led by the Technical Expert Group chaired by Surjit Bhalla; a new Survey on Migration runs July 2026–June 2027. 🎓⭐
31. **'PM RAHAT': ₹1.5 lakh cashless care for road-accident victims** — 7 days via ERSS-112; Bharat-VISTAAR AI crop advisory on 155261; 3.53 lakh houses sanctioned under PMAY-U 2.0. 🎓
32. **'Insurance for All by 2047' — Alwar becomes India's first fully-insured district**; PM-SETU (₹60,000 crore, 1,000 ITIs modernised); Delhi's 'Lakhpati Bitiya' (one-time FD for girls) and Kerala's 'Sthree Suraksha' (₹1,000/month for women aged 21–60). 🎓
