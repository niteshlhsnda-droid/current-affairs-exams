📰 Current Affairs — February 2026

Monthly exam-ready digest for Bank • UPSC • PSU aspirants, compiled from GKToday, Insights IAS, Drishti IAS, Testbook & AffairsCloud.

Tags: 🏦 Bank • 🎓 UPSC • 🏭 PSU • ⭐ high-yield

🎯 Top stories of the month

Polity & Governance 🏛️

  1. Union Budget 2026-27 presented on Feb 1 — Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman's 9th consecutive budget, themed on 3 Kartavyas (accelerate growth, aspirations, access to all); fiscal deficit targeted at 4.3% of GDP; capex ₹12.2 lakh crore; first budget presented in Kartavya Bhawan. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. New Income Tax Act 2025 takes effect from April 2026 — TCS on overseas tour packages cut to 2%, LRS education/medical TCS to 2%; STT on futures raised to 0.05% and options to 0.15%; MAT cut to 14% as final tax; tax holiday till 2047 for foreign cloud-service companies. 🏦🎓⭐
  3. 16th Finance Commission retains states' vertical devolution at 41% (2026-31) — announced Feb 3; urban local body grants rise 230% to ₹3.56 trillion; ₹1.4 lakh crore announced for states in the budget. 🎓⭐
  4. India AI Impact Summit (Feb 16–20, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi) — first global AI summit in the Global South; 3 Sutras (People, Planet, Progress) and 7 Chakras; New Delhi Declaration endorsed by 89 countries; Guinness World Record with 2,50,946 responsible-AI pledges in 24 hours. 🎓⭐

Economy, Banking & Finance 🏦

  1. National Monetisation Pipeline 2.0 targets ₹16.72 lakh crore (FY25-26 to FY29-30) — plus ₹1 lakh crore Urban Challenge Fund for city growth and ₹10,000 crore Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0. 🏦🎓⭐
  2. India–US interim trade agreement signed — reciprocal tariffs cut from 25% to 18%; India to buy $500 billion of goods over 5 years; US lifts additional tariffs after India pledges to stop Russian oil imports. 🏦🎓⭐
  3. RBI package: KCC revised guidelines (6-year loan tenure, 20% additional credit); ECB limits raised from $750M to $1B; digital payment agent norms (IIBF certification, 8AM–7PM, from Jul 1); RBI-DPI index at 516.76; GDP & IIP base year moves to 2022-23 and CPI to 2024 (food weight 45.86% → 36.75%); New India Co-operative Bank merged into Saraswat Bank. 🏦

Science & Technology 🔬

  1. Chandrayaan-4 landing site identified: Mons Mouton-4 — on the Moon's south pole for India's crewed lunar mission, announced at the 111th Indian Science Congress. 🎓⭐
  2. Two world-class telescopes sanctioned for Ladakh — 2-metre National Large Solar Telescope at Pangong Tso and 13.7-metre National Large Optical-Near Infrared Telescope at Hanle. 🎓
  3. UIDAI launches 'Invisible Shield' biometric deduplication engine; DigiLocker partners Google to map 60,000 Aadhaar centres; Tata–OpenAI announce the Stargate India data centre (100 MW scaling to 1 GW, $7 billion Tata investment). 🎓

Environment & Ecology 🌿

  1. India's Ramsar tally reaches 98 — Patna Bird Sanctuary (UP) as 97th and Chhari-Dhand Conservation Reserve (Gujarat) as 98th on World Wetlands Day (Feb 2); theme 'Wetlands and Traditional Knowledge: Celebrating Cultural Heritage'. 🎓⭐
  2. E20 (20% ethanol-blended petrol) mandate from April 1, 2026 — India ranks 2nd globally in LEED-certified green building space; NHAI launches 'Bee Corridors' with 40 lakh native trees along highways by FY27. 🎓

Sports 🏅

  1. India wins 6th ICC U-19 World Cup — beats England in the final; Vaibhav Suryavanshi scores 175 and bags the Player of the Tournament award. 🎓⭐
  2. Milano Cortina Winter Olympics (Feb 6–22, Italy) — Norway topped the medal table with a record gold-medal haul; India represented by Arif Mohammad Khan and Stanzin Lundup. 🎓⭐
  3. Australian Open 2026: Carlos Alcaraz beats Novak Djokovic (men's) and Elena Rybakina beats Aryna Sabalenka (women's); Rybakina ends a 15-month title drought. 🏦🎓
  4. WPL 2026: RCB beat Delhi Capitals chasing 204 — RCB's second title; at the men's T20 World Cup 2026, India beat Pakistan (Ishan Kishan 77) and Rashid Khan became the 3rd bowler with 700 T20 wickets. 🏦🎓
  5. Domestic milestones — J&K reaches its first-ever Ranji Trophy final; Paras Dogra is the fastest to 10,000 Ranji runs (147 innings); India tops the Asian Shooting Championship with 94 medals (51G/23S/20B); Harmanpreet Kaur becomes the most-capped woman cricketer (356 matches); Smriti Mandhana wins BBC Indian Sportswoman of the Year 2025. 🏦🎓

Awards & Honours 🏆

  1. Grammy Awards 2026 — Song of the Year: Billie Eilish's Wildflower; Album of the Year: Bad Bunny's Debí Tirar Más Fotos; Best New Artist: Olivia Dean; Lady Gaga's Mayhem; Dalai Lama wins his first Grammy for Meditations; Huntr/x's Golden marks the first K-pop win. 🎓
  2. 'Boong' becomes the first Indian film to win a BAFTA — Best Children's & Family Film at the 79th British Academy Film Awards (Manipur). 🎓⭐
  3. Crafoord Prize 2026 (Geosciences) to Veerabhadran Ramanathan — for pioneering work on atmospheric aerosols and climate change; Bhavya Bharat Bhushan Awards 2026 announced (Nambi Narayanan, Saina Nehwal, Vikram Sampath, Alarmel Valli, N. Rajam and the Armed Forces for Operation Sindoor). 🎓

Appointments & Resignations 📋

  1. Tarique Rahman becomes Bangladesh's 11th Prime Minister (Feb 17) — BNP wins 212/299 seats. 🎓⭐
  2. Nidhi Chhibber (IAS, 1994) gets additional charge as NITI Aayog CEO (Feb 24, 2026); Deepak Gupta appointed GAIL CMD; Uday Kotak appointed GIFT City Chairman; Vinay Tonse joins YES Bank as MD & CEO; R. Vijay Anandh takes City Union Bank; Rohit Rishi joins IIFCL as MD. 🏦🎓
  3. Historic PM transitions abroad — Sanae Takaichi becomes Japan's first woman PM (snap-election landslide); Rob Jetten becomes Netherlands' youngest PM (38); Anutin Charnvirakul becomes Thailand PM; Mia Mottley wins a 3rd consecutive term in Barbados. 🎓

Defence 🛡️

  1. DAC approves 114 Rafale jets for ₹3.25 lakh crore — largest-ever defence procurement; 18 fly-away + 96 Make-in-India; IAF's 7th and 8th squadrons at Ambala and Hasimara. 🎓⭐
  2. MILAN 2026 (Feb 15–25, Visakhapatnam) hosts 74 nations — largest edition; International Fleet Review on Feb 18; India assumes IONS chairmanship (2026–28). 🎓
  3. Joint exercises and firepower — KHANJAR-XIII (India–Kyrgyzstan, Missamari, Feb 4–17); DHARMA GUARDIAN 7th (India–Japan, Chaubattia, Feb 24–Mar 9); VAJRA PRAHAR 16th (India–US, Bakloh, Feb 23–Mar 15); IAF's Vayushakti-26 at Pokhran (Feb 27); INS Anjadip (3rd ASW-SWC, GRSE) commissioned; CTS 'Krishna' launched at L&T Kattupalli. 🎓

International Relations 🌐

  1. India–France ties elevated to Special Global Strategic Partnership — during Macron's February 2026 visit; 2026 declared the Year of Innovation; India–Israel upgraded to a Special Strategic Partnership (Modi's visit; India–Israel FTA talks Feb 23–26). 🎓⭐
  2. India formally joins Pax Silica Coalition as 12th member — US-led critical-minerals initiative; India–GCC sign Terms of Reference to restart FTA negotiations. 🎓
  3. Summit diplomacy — PM Modi visits Malaysia (first bilateral in a decade); Seychelles President Herminie's first foreign visit to India (SESEL vision, $175M package); Brazil's Lula visits Feb 18–22 ($30B trade target by 2030); China records a 3 million population decline in 2025; South Korea's ex-President Yoon Suk Yeol sentenced to life imprisonment. 🎓

Government Schemes 📜

  1. National Household Income Survey from April 2026 — MoSPI's first-ever NHIS with digital CAPI, led by the Technical Expert Group chaired by Surjit Bhalla; a new Survey on Migration runs July 2026–June 2027. 🎓⭐
  2. 'PM RAHAT': ₹1.5 lakh cashless care for road-accident victims — 7 days via ERSS-112; Bharat-VISTAAR AI crop advisory on 155261; 3.53 lakh houses sanctioned under PMAY-U 2.0. 🎓
  3. 'Insurance for All by 2047' — Alwar becomes India's first fully-insured district; PM-SETU (₹60,000 crore, 1,000 ITIs modernised); Delhi's 'Lakhpati Bitiya' (one-time FD for girls) and Kerala's 'Sthree Suraksha' (₹1,000/month for women aged 21–60). 🎓

Reports & Indices 📊

  1. Corruption Perceptions Index 2025: India 91st (score 39) — Denmark leads with 89. 🎓⭐
  2. Henley Passport Index (Feb 2026): India 75th (up 5 spots) with 56 visa-free destinations; Singapore leads with 192. 🎓
  3. Global wealth and knowledge — India is 3rd in global billionaires (188); India ranks 7th in R&D spending ($75.7B); Network Readiness Index 2025: India 45th; WPI inflation at 1.81% in January 2026; Delhi named the world's most affordable student city. 🏦🎓

Books & Authors 📚

  1. Gen. Manoj Mukund Naravane's autobiography 'Four Stars of Destiny' — first-ever autobiography by a former Indian Army Chief (2026). 🎓⭐
  2. 'Atal Bihari Vajpayee: The Eternal Statesman' coffee-table book released; Tamil & Gujarati editions of the Constitution launched on International Mother Language Day by VP Radhakrishnan. 🎓

Obituaries 🕯️

  1. Mukul Roy (71) — former Union Railway Minister, died Feb 23 in Kolkata. 🎓
  2. R Nallakannu (101) — veteran CPI leader and freedom fighter, died Feb 25 in Chennai; Ricky Andrew J Syngkon (54) — Shillong MP, died Feb 23; also Andre Beteille (91, sociologist), Hugh Gantzer (95, travel journalism), Joe Simon (80, comics legend) and Robert Duvall (95, actor). 🎓

Important Days 📅

  1. Feb 2 — World Wetlands Day; Feb 4 — World Cancer Day; Feb 13 — World Radio Day & National Women's Day (Sarojini Naidu's 147th); Feb 18–20 — Losar; Feb 20 — Arunachal Foundation Day & World Day of Social Justice; Feb 21 — International Mother Language Day; Feb 22 — World Thinking Day centenary (100th); Feb 24 — Central Excise Day; Feb 27 — National Protein Day & World NGO Day. 🎓

State News 🗺️

  1. Gujarat: Shops & Establishments Amendment Bill 2026 passed — flexible working hours and women's safety norms; UP Budget: ₹9.12 trillion — the biggest-ever state budget; ADB approves a $182M flood-loan for Assam. 🏦🎓
  2. VOC Port (Tamil Nadu) becomes the first major port with IGBC Platinum + Shunya Plus; Bihar celebrates the centenary of the Ol Chiki script (invented 1925 by Pt. Raghunath Murmu) with the President. 🎓
