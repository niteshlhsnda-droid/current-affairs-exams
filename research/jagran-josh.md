## How Jagran Josh organizes current affairs
- **Generalist CA machine**: daily articles, daily one-liners, daily MCQ quizzes, **free monthly CA PDFs**, video CA, and **exam-specific buckets** (SSC/Banking/UPSC/State PCS) plus static GK pages.
- **Archives go back to 2009+** — one of the oldest CA archives in India.
- **Exam focus: SSC-strong generalist** — also covers Banking/UPSC/State PCS, but banking coverage is **weak-moderate** (an Economy section exists; no bank-specific curation like Guidely/AffairsCloud).
- **Among the strongest Hindi CA ecosystems**: full parallel Hindi current affairs + Hindi quizzes/PDFs.
- **Overwhelmingly free** (ad-driven); no paywall on core CA.

## Strengths
1. **Best-in-class Hindi CA ecosystem** — full parallel Hindi articles, one-liners, quizzes and monthly PDFs; the top pick for Hindi-medium SSC/State PCS aspirants.
2. **Exam-specific categorisation + free monthly PDFs** — easy to filter to your exam and consolidate the month for free.
3. **Oldest archives (2009+)** — useful for tracing how topics repeat across exam cycles.

## Weaknesses
1. **Ad-heavy, cluttered UX** — pages are dense with ads; reading experience is the worst in this repo's set.
2. **Banking depth far below the bank specialists** — no RBI-circular lens (Oliveboard), no finance granularity (Guidely), no dedicated banking section (AffairsCloud).
3. **Generalist curation** — items are rarely written with exam-recall precision; breadth over sharpness.

## Notes for exam-prep use
- Best for: **Hindi-medium aspirants** and **SSC/State PCS** candidates wanting a free daily companion — bilingual one-liners + free monthly PDFs.
- Pair with: a bank specialist (Guidely/Oliveboard) if you're targeting IBPS/SBI Mains.
- Not for: banking-finance depth; RBI Grade B.
