## How ClearIAS organizes current affairs
- **Medium-depth explainer notes** (What is / Why in news / background / significance / tables / way forward / conclusion) with **heavy internal cross-linking** — every note links to related static-syllabus notes.
- **MCQ-first pedagogy**: historically published **"ClearIAS Current Affairs Capsules (CCC)"** — monthly **MCQ-format PDF eBooks, free** (historically "for a limited period" via the ClearIAS Store).
- **Topic-driven, not a dated daily bulletin** — notes appear a few days after events (slower than ForumIAS/IASbaba); no publish dates on the listing page.
- Current Affairs Notes and CCC PDFs are **free**; paid = ClearIAS Premium test series/mocks. English only. No Bank/PSU.

## Strengths
1. **MCQ-first pedagogy = fastest Prelims revision format** — "learn by question" beats re-reading notes for MCQ exams.
2. **Entirely free core content** with excellent internal cross-linking — one note branches into the full related syllabus.
3. **Cleaner reading experience** than ad-heavy GKToday/AffairsCloud pages; printable/PDF-friendly.

## Weaknesses
1. **Not a same-day news service** — days of lag, so it can't replace a daily feed; it's a *revision* instrument.
2. **English-only** — no Hindi support.
3. **Weaker Mains depth than Insights IAS**; no daily quiz habit like Testbook — the MCQ loop is monthly, not daily.

## Notes for exam-prep use
- Best for: **MCQ-driven UPSC Prelims current-affairs revision** — use the free CCC monthly MCQ PDFs as the monthly test of everything you read elsewhere.
- Pair with: a daily feed (IASbaba / ForumIAS) for freshness + Vision IAS PT 365 for final revision.
