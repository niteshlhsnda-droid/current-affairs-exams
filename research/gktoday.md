## How GKToday organizes current affairs
- **"Current Affairs Today" section** (gktoday.in/current-affairs/): updated **daily**, with same-day dated entries (e.g., Sept 22 items live on Sept 22). Each item is a short news note tagged with a category and date.
- **Category taxonomy** (useful for exam-wise filtering): Government Schemes, Defence, Awards/Honours & Persons in News, Science & Technology, Economy & Banking, International/World, Environment & Biodiversity, Legal & Constitution, Reports & Indexes, Art & Culture, Sports.
- **Dedicated "Economy & Banking" category** and a **Business/Economy/Banking MCQ quizbase** — directly relevant to Bank/IBPS/RBI exam prep (questions cover RBI, SEBI, NPCI/UPI, IBA awards, inflation indices, co-operative banks, PSU banks).
- **Daily MCQs**: a "Daily 20 MCQs" series plus date-wise daily current affairs quizzes (e.g., "Daily Current Affairs Quiz: September 20-21, 2026").
- **Monthly MCQ PDF e-books (paid)**: each e-book has ~200 select MCQs drawn from the month's 500+ quiz questions. Sold as instant PDF downloads (no hard copy). GKToday itself notes these are "direct, factual questions suitable for SSC, Banking, States, CLAT, CAT, Railways" and **"may not be suitable for UPSC Examinations."**
- **GKToday Android app** with exclusive courses (Daily 20 MCQs series sourced from the app's exclusive course).
- **Free vs paid**: daily current affairs articles and online quizzes appear free; PDF compilations and app courses are paid.

## Strengths (vs typical exam-prep current affairs sites)
1. **Same-day daily updates with clean subject categorization** — the Economy & Banking and Legal & Constitution buckets map directly onto Bank/UPSC syllabus areas, making targeted revision easy.
2. **Large free MCQ bank with subject-wise quiz sections** — the banking quizbase covers RBI/SEBI/NPCI/IBA-type factual questions that Bank exams actually ask; quizzes are free to attempt online.
3. **Exam-targeted framing** — the section explicitly targets UPSC, IAS/PCS, Banking, IBPS, SSC, Railway and state PCS, and items are written as exam-ready factual notes rather than long news analysis.

## Weaknesses (vs typical exam-prep current affairs sites)
1. **Depth gap for UPSC** — by GKToday's own admission, its MCQ PDFs are direct/factual and "may not be suitable for UPSC"; sites like InsightsIAS/Drishti IAS offer deeper editorial/analysis content better suited to UPSC Mains.
2. **Key compilations are paywalled** — the monthly MCQ PDF e-books cost money, whereas several competitors publish free monthly current-affairs PDFs/compilations.
3. **Fragmented pagination** — daily content is spread across many paginated pages (e.g., ?pageno=9), which makes browsing a full month's archive slower than a single monthly compilation page.

## Notes for exam-prep use
- Best for: **Bank/IBPS/RBI/SSC/PSU factual current affairs** (daily notes + banking MCQs).
- Supplement with InsightsIAS/Drishti IAS/AffairsCloud for UPSC-oriented analysis and free monthly compilations.
