## How ForumIAS organizes current affairs
- **Flagship: "Must Read News Daily Current Affairs Articles"** — dated daily posts that **curate links to The Hindu + Indian Express editorials/articles**, each tagged by GS paper with commentary. Effectively a newspaper-replacement service.
- **Companion feeds**: Daily Factly (one-page fact notes), **PSIR & GS2 Daily Brief** and **Pub Ad Optional Daily Brief** (daily optional-subject tables — rare anywhere else), and the **7 PM Daily Initiative** (answer-writing practice).
- **PYQ-mapping**: briefs routinely map items to actual past UPSC questions; embedded YouTube videos in posts.
- **Daily on weekdays**; latest dated post is yesterday — fast.
- Daily blog content is **free**; coaching is advertised (paid). No free monthly compilation was found. English only.

## Strengths
1. **Only site that substitutes daily newspaper reading** — curated Hindu/IE links with GS-paper tagging and commentary, saving the 2–3 hours of raw newspaper time.
2. **Unique daily optional-subject briefs** (Pub Ad, PSIR) — the best current-affairs support for those optional students anywhere in this repo's coverage.
3. **PYQ-mapped analysis** in the briefs trains exam-pattern thinking, not just fact accumulation.

## Weaknesses
1. **Mostly curation, not original deep analysis** — thinner than Insights IAS for Mains editorial depth; if the linked article goes behind a paywall, value drops.
2. **English-only** — no Hindi support at all (Drishti IAS / Vision IAS cover Hindi-medium instead).
3. **No consolidated free monthly PDF** and Factly notes are scattered across dated posts — weak for month-end consolidation.

## Notes for exam-prep use
- Best for: **UPSC aspirants wanting to replace the newspaper** — Hindu/IE curation with GS tags; Pub Ad/PSIR optional students should treat the daily optional briefs as non-negotiable.
- Pair with: Insights IAS or StudyIQ for Mains answer-writing depth; Vision IAS PT 365 for final revision.
- Not for: Bank/SSC — no banking coverage.
