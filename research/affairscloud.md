## How AffairsCloud organizes current affairs
- **Daily current affairs pages** (affairscloud.com/current-affairs-<date>-september-2026/): long-form daily editions organized into consistent sections — **National Affairs, International Affairs, Banking & Finance, Economy & Business, Awards & Recognitions, Science & Technology, Environment, Sports, Important Days, State News**. The dedicated **Banking & Finance** section is directly tailored to Bank/IBPS/RBI exam prep.
- **Update frequency:** daily (weekday editions; weekends often published as combined multi-day editions, e.g., "Current Affairs 19, 20 & 21 September 2026"). The site admits a **built-in ~1-day lag**: news is deliberately held over so evening-published reports/rankings and official data are fully covered before posting.

- **Exam coverage stated:** Banking, Insurance, SSC, Railways, UPSC, CLAT and State Government Exams.
- **Extras:** exam-recall posts (e.g., "GA Questions asked in SBI PO Mains Exam 2026"), e-books for sale, partnership recommendations (Oliveboard mock tests), and CareersCloud app integration ("Learn Current Affairs" free course). Strict anti-content-sharing legal notices are posted on pages.

## Strengths (vs typical exam-prep current affairs sites)
1. **Deep, exam-oriented write-ups with "About" boxes** — each item carries background, statutory detail, specifications and context (e.g., exact EPS contribution caps, drone range/payload specs, IEMI methodology), serving both Prelims facts and Mains context in one place; clearly deeper per-item than GKToday-style short notes.
2. **Dedicated Banking & Finance section + exam-recall content** — the Banking & Finance bucket plus posts like "GA Questions asked in SBI PO Mains" directly target Bank aspirants with the factual product/news items exams actually test.
3. **Completeness-over-speed editorial policy** — the deliberate 1-day delay ensures no evening-released reports, rankings or important-days items are missed, and the dated archive stretches back years (721+ pages), aiding systematic revision.

## Weaknesses (vs typical exam-prep current affairs sites)
1. **Curated exam value is paywalled** — the "Exam Hint" and in-depth pointers (the most useful bit for identifying what's actually exam-relevant) were moved exclusively to the paid Toppers CA PDF / Crack Current Course; free pages are dense data dumps without that curation.
2. **1-day lag + heavy, ad-laden long-form pages** — aspirants wanting same-day brevity (like GKToday's short categorized notes) face very long, slow-loading pages; Hindi users are pushed to a separate app.
3. **No visible UPSC Mains-oriented editorial layer** — unlike InsightsIAS/Drishti IAS (editorials, mains answer writing), AffairsCloud's format is Prelims/GA-centric (facts, reports, schemes, appointments); weaker for UPSC Mains preparation.
