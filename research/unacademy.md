## How Unacademy organizes current affairs
- **Video-first**: daily live lessons (6:30 AM), class PDFs, free 4–8 minute daily CA clips, podcasts.
- **Subject-wise monthly UCAN batches** (Economy, Environment, IR, Art & Culture…) — a **unique monthly revision structure** no text site replicates.
- **T20 Daily CA Test Series (free)** + **24,000+ MCQ bank** with monthly CA additions.
- **Exam focus: UPSC-centric**; State PSC/SSC/Bank served via Hindi/Hinglish channels. Lessons in English, Hindi and Hinglish.
- **Freemium**: short courses, demos and podcasts free; UCAN monthly batches and the test series = Plus (paid). Free tier is fragmented and partly login-gated.

## Strengths
1. **Subject-wise monthly batches** — Economy one month, Environment the next — a revision structure no text-first site offers.
2. **Huge free test series / MCQ bank** (24,000+ questions) with monthly CA additions — serious quiz practice.
3. **Live educator interaction** and free short daily clips for quick video revision.

## Weaknesses
1. **Video-only best content** — there is no skimmable daily text feed; slow for quick revision (text sites win on speed).
2. **Free tier is fragmented and login-gated** — the good monthly batches are Plus (paid).
3. **Educator quality varies** across the platform's many creators.

## Notes for exam-prep use
- Best for: **video learners** and **subject-wise monthly revision** — treat UCAN batches as the video complement to a text daily feed.
- Pair with: a daily text source (IASbaba / ForumIAS) for freshness; Testbook for quiz habit.
- Not for: replacing a daily text source; free-only users (the best parts are paid).
