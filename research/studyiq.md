## How StudyIQ organizes current affairs
- **Daily cadence, multiple articles per day**; daily Mains answer-writing articles updated ~7:30 PM.
- **Long-form explainers** (background + why-in-news), a **free daily CA PDF**, **daily mains answer-writing** pieces, quizzes.
- **Heavy YouTube ecosystem**: The Hindu analysis, PIB analysis, in English *and* Hindi — one of the biggest CA video libraries in India.
- **Exam focus: UPSC-heavy + State PCS**; SSC/Banking/Defence are served through the PDF.
- **Strong Hindi support** (Hindi PDFs, Hindi YT channel). Articles + daily PDF free; pages funnel toward paid courses.

## Strengths
1. **Same-day explainer depth** — long-form articles land by late morning, matching Insights IAS's analytical level with fresher timing.
2. **Daily mains answer-writing** — a daily writing habit built into the CA feed, rivaling Insights/Drishti for Mains practice.
3. **Multilingual video ecosystem** — English + Hindi YouTube analysis (The Hindu, PIB) is unmatched by any other UPSC site in this repo except Drishti.

## Weaknesses
1. **Banking gets generic PDF treatment** — far weaker than AffairsCloud/Guidely/Oliveboard for bank exams.
2. **No quiz-first habit loop** like Testbook — quizzes exist but aren't the product's centre of gravity.
3. **Upsell-heavy pages** — free articles constantly push paid courses.

## Notes for exam-prep use
- Best for: **UPSC aspirants wanting one daily habit that covers facts + Mains writing** — pair with Insights IAS.
- Pair with: PIB (StudyIQ's PIB analysis is video-first) for primary-source verification.
- Not for: Bank/IBPS depth.
