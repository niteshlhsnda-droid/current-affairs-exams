## How IASbaba organizes current affairs
- **Structured daily analysis** with an explicit **(PRELIMS Focus) / (MAINS Focus) split in every post**: "Why in News?", key facts, "UPSC Prelims Analysis" pointers, plus listed sources.
- **Same-day/next-morning publishing** — the freshest of the UPSC-focused sites in this repo (21 Sept post live by the 22nd morning).
- **Separate Daily Current Affairs Quiz (Mon–Sat, before 2 PM)** — 5 questions with solutions — plus a Daily Static Quiz.
- Monthly magazines were published free historically (archive to ~2022), but **no 2026 monthly edition was found** — compilation continuity is unverified/stale.
- Daily analysis and quizzes are **free**; coaching is paid. English only. No Bank/PSU coverage.

## Strengths
1. **Clean Prelims/Mains split in every post** — the clearest "what to memorize vs what to think about" framing among the UPSC sites here.
2. **Fastest same-day UPSC cadence** — better than Insights IAS (day-after) and Vision IAS (4–7 day article lag) for staying current.
3. **PYQ-linked Prelims Analysis pointers** plus a built-in 5-question daily quiz habit.

## Weaknesses
1. **English-only** — no Hindi support (Vision IAS / Drishti IAS fill that gap).
2. **Monthly magazine continuity looks stale** vs Vision IAS's active bilingual monthly magazine — weak for month-end consolidation.
3. **No Bank/PSU coverage** — strictly UPSC.

## Notes for exam-prep use
- Best for: **UPSC aspirants wanting the most consistent same-day current-affairs feed** — make the daily analysis + quiz your morning habit.
- Pair with: Vision IAS monthly magazine / PT 365 for consolidation; Insights IAS for deeper Mains editorials.
- Not for: Bank/IBPS/SSC.
