## How Adda247 organizes current affairs
- **Speed-first hub** at currentaffairs.adda247.com: **~15–25 short articles per day**, posts timestamped "4 minutes ago" / "30 minutes ago" — the fastest same-day publisher in this repo.
- **"Weekly Current Affairs One Liners"** series + **monthly date-wise calendar archive** for quick look-back.
- Each article carries a **"Summary of the news" fact table** (exam-ready recall bullets); **daily YouTube explainers** for video revision.
- **Banking vertical** at bankersadda.com covers Bank (IBPS/SBI/RBI), plus SSC, Railway, Police SI, State Govt — multi-exam. Banking coverage is **moderate**: RBI/NBFC news appears, but there's **no dedicated deep banking-finance section** like AffairsCloud/Guidely.
- **Full Hindi verticals** (daily articles, weekly one-liners, YouTube). Daily articles + one-liners + YT free; monthly compilations, Adhyayan CA book, Bank Mahapack paid.

## Strengths
1. **Fastest same-day publishing** of any site in this repo — if it happened this morning, Adda247 likely has it by afternoon.
2. **Integrated bank-exam ecosystem** — BankersAdda quizzes and Mahapack mocks sit right next to the CA feed, so revision flows straight into test practice.
3. **Daily video explainers + one-liners** make it the best speed-revision combo for working aspirants.

## Weaknesses
1. **Breadth over depth** — items are shallow; banking-finance granularity is far below Guidely/AffairsCloud/Oliveboard's regulatory lens.
2. **Aggressive paid upsells** — free content is constantly funnelling toward Mahapack and paid books.
3. **No UPSC Mains analysis** — useless for CSE Mains depth (use Insights IAS / StudyIQ there).

## Notes for exam-prep use
- Best for: **the SPEED FEED for IBPS/SBI/SSC aspirants** — skim the one-liners + YouTube explainer daily; never let a day's news slip.
- Pair with: Guidely or AffairsCloud for banking-finance depth; Oliveboard's free PDFs for monthly consolidation.
- Not for: UPSC Mains; RBI Grade B depth (use Oliveboard instead).
