## How Testbook organizes current affairs

- **Daily byte-sized capsules:** News shrunk into under-100-word / ~370-character bullets, in **English and Hindi** (matching actual exam bilingual format).
- **Daily quizzes:** After each day's capsules, the app routes you to that date's quiz; each question carries Testbook's "signature solutions" in a fixed format — **In News → Key Points → Additional Information → Important Points**.
- **Weekly & monthly quizzes** plus exam-specific **monthly compilations** (e.g., UPSC CSE Current Affairs Test Series 2026–27 lists 87 daily CA tests, 24 monthly 2026 tests).
- **Category-wise MCQ bank** on the website: Current Affairs, Agreements & MoU, India & World, etc., with detailed explanations — no login required.
- **Free daily PDFs:** Daily current-affairs PDFs and free monthly PDFs are downloadable from the app/website (stated in their FAQ).
- **Offline/bookmarking:** App works offline, lets you bookmark items; date-wise navigation to revisit old days.
- **Update frequency:** Daily (capsules + quiz), weekly/monthly compilations.
- **Free vs paid:** Daily CA, daily quizzes, PDFs and the question bank are **free**. Paid layers: SuperCoaching live courses, Pass Pro / Super Pass (mocks, PYQs, notes, AI doubt-solving). Free reading is interrupted by upsell banners ("Start ₹1 Trial").

## Strengths (vs typical exam-prep CA sites)

1. **Quiz-first, bilingual pedagogy** — Capsules are deliberately short (<100 words, Hindi + English like real exams), and every day's reading is followed by that date's quiz with detailed "In News / Key Points / Additional Info / Important Points" solutions — stronger for retention than GKToday's or AffairsCloud's plain article dumps.
2. **Fully free daily layer** — Daily capsules, daily quizzes and monthly PDFs cost nothing (competitors like Drishti/Insights gate much of this behind paywalls), and content is downloadable/offline with date-wise back-reading.
3. **Integrated exam ecosystem** — CA feeds directly into exam-specific test series (e.g., UPSC CSE CA Test Series 2026–27 with 87 daily CA tests) inside the same app as mocks, PYQs and coaching — one-stop for Bank/SSC/Railway aspirants.

## Weaknesses (vs typical exam-prep CA sites)

1. **Too shallow for UPSC Mains** — 100-word capsules with no editorial analysis, answer-writing practice or issue-based deep dives; Insights IAS, Drishti IAS and even GKToday's daily articles give more analytical depth for CSE.
2. **Weak web discoverability** — No open, date-wise daily CA article pages on the website (the blog has no daily CA series); the freshest content lives behind the app/test-series wall, whereas GKToday and AffairsCloud publish openly indexed daily pages.
3. **Upsell-heavy UX** — Free reading is constantly interrupted by "Start ₹1 Trial" / SuperCoaching banners; and some hub pages go stale (e.g., the UPSC daily quiz page was still titled "for Prelims 2025" in Sep 2026).
