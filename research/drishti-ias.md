## 2. How Drishti IAS Organizes Current Affairs

- **Hindi + English:** Full parallel sites — drishtiias.com (English) and drishtiias.com/hindi (Hindi). Hindi daily news is an analysed translation of English newspaper content (The Hindu, Indian Express, LiveMint, Business Line, TOI, Business Standard, PIB, BBC, Al Jazeera, CNN).
- **Sections:** (a) Daily News Analysis (long-form articles), (b) Important Editorials (condensed editorial analysis with "For Prelims / For Mains" tags), (c) Rapid Fire Current Affairs / "To The Point" one-liner facts, (d) Monthly consolidations (PDF compilations for revision).
- **Article format:** Every item follows "Why in News?" → "Key Points" (bold-fact bullets); editorials add "For Prelims:" (keywords) and "For Mains:" framing. Print/PDF download links provided per article.
- **Update frequency:** Daily, **except Sundays and national holidays** (missed significant topics are covered on the following weekdays). Tag pages show dated items (e.g., 21 Sep 2026 as latest national items).
- **YouTube layer:** Daily "The Hindu Newspaper Analysis & PIB" videos, weekly "Economy This Week", "PIB Weekly Analysis", "Duniya Is Hafte" — free, in both Hindi and English.
- **Free vs paid:** Core current affairs content (daily news analysis, rapid-fire facts, editorials, monthly PDFs) is freely accessible on the website. Monetization is via paid courses (GS Foundation, Prelims/Mains/Optional, NCERT) and the Drishti Learning App; course promos are embedded in videos and site banners.

## 3. Strengths & Weaknesses (vs typical exam-prep CA sites)

**Strengths**
1. **Exam-oriented structuring** — "Why in News?", bold key-point bullets, and explicit "For Prelims / For Mains" tags make each item directly usable for UPSC/Bank revision, better than plain news aggregation.
2. **Bilingual depth + trusted sourcing** — Same content in Hindi and English, sourced from The Hindu/PIB/Indian Express etc., with monthly consolidated PDFs for revision.
3. **Multi-format ecosystem** — Text articles + Rapid Fire one-liners + YouTube daily analysis + monthly PDFs cover reading, quick revision, and audio-visual learners in one place.

**Weaknesses**
1. **UPSC-centric, thin on Bank/SSC-specific needs** — Heavy focus on UPSC Mains-style analysis; less emphasis on the high-volume one-liner/Q&A format and banking-awareness depth (RBI circulars, banking terms) that Bank/IBPS aspirants need.
2. **No daily quiz/test integration on the free CA pages** — Unlike some competitors, the free current-affairs pages don't bundle daily MCQs or mock tests alongside articles, so self-assessment requires the paid app/courses.
3. **Publishing gaps** — No updates on Sundays and national holidays (by stated policy), so aspirants relying solely on Drishti miss a day; missed items are only backfilled later in the week.
