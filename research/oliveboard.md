## How Oliveboard organizes current affairs
- **PDF-first ecosystem — the most generous free compilation set in this repo**: FREE **daily / weekly / monthly / 6-month CA PDFs**, free daily CA quiz, **Banking & Financial Awareness PDF**, and a **RBI Daily Current Affairs PDF** (PIB + RBI circulars + GA).
- **Quiz-first delivery**: daily free quiz on discuss.oliveboard.in; PDFs are structured as question banks (paid "BOLT" CA course = 700+ questions).
- **Banking-first exam focus**: SBI/IBPS/RRB/RBI Assistant + **Regulatory (RBI Grade B, NABARD, SEBI)** + SSC/Railways/Insurance. Banking/finance is **very strong** — a dedicated Banking & Financial Awareness stream plus the **unique RBI-circulars daily feed** make it the **best regulatory-exam CA source** in this repo.
- **Hindi support**: "Last 6 Months' Current Affairs in Hindi & English." PDFs + quizzes free; mocks + BOLT paid. UPSC coverage is token only.

## Strengths
1. **Most generous free PDF ecosystem** — daily → 6-month compilations all free, in English *and* Hindi; no other bank site gives away this much.
2. **Unique RBI-circulars/regulatory lens** — nobody else delivers RBI circulars + PIB + GA as a daily regulatory feed; unmatched for RBI Grade B / NABARD / SEBI aspirants.
3. **Quiz-first PDFs** — the compilation doubles as a question bank, so revision is active recall, not re-reading.

## Weaknesses
1. **No clean dated article listing** — content is scattered across the blog, PDF downloads and quiz pages; hard to browse "today's news" quickly (Adda247 is better for that).
2. **Uneven blog depth** — beyond the PDFs, editorial quality varies; weaker per-item write-ups than Guidely/AffairsCloud.
3. **UPSC coverage is token only** — not a CSE source.

## Notes for exam-prep use
- Best for: **RBI Grade B, NABARD, SEBI** aspirants (regulatory CA) and anyone wanting **free monthly/6-month PDF consolidation** for IBPS/SBI/SSC.
- Pair with: Adda247 for the daily speed feed; Guidely/AffairsCloud for per-item banking depth.
- Not for: UPSC; daily skimmable news reading.
