## How Vision IAS organizes current affairs
- **Tiered cadence** (the signature feature): **Daily "News Today" bulletin** → **Weekly Focus** deep-dives → **Monthly Magazine** → **PT 365 / Mains 365** annual compilations → **Quarterly Revision**, plus Budget & Economic Survey specials and a Monthly Workbook.
- **Daily bulletins and monthly magazines are free PDFs** from the downloads hub at dce.visionias.in/downloads — no paywall signal on core CA products.
- **Bilingual**: monthly magazines published in **Hindi as well as English**; Hindi-medium courses offered — the only UPSC CA site here with full Hindi-medium CA parity.
- **Web article archive** under "Current Affairs" on visionias.in (short-to-medium explainers: why-in-news + background + significance + way forward), plus YouTube explainers.
- Exam framing is explicitly **UPSC Prelims + Mains**; no banking or PSU coverage.

## Strengths
1. **PT 365 / Mains 365 are gold-standard last-mile revision** — topic-wise, syllabus-mapped annual compilations; none of the other covered sites matches this for pre-exam consolidation.
2. **Only UPSC CA site here with full Hindi-medium parity** — monthly magazines in Hindi, a real option for Hindi-medium aspirants (alongside Drishti IAS).
3. **Multi-tier revision ladder** (daily → weekly → monthly → 365) means one source can carry you from January to the exam hall; Budget/Economic Survey specials fill syllabus gaps.

## Weaknesses
1. **Article freshness lagged 4–7 days** behind IASbaba/ForumIAS at check — weak as a *daily news* source; use the monthly/365 products, not the web archive, for freshness.
2. **Split across two domains** (visionias.in and dce.visionias.in) — browsing the archive and finding downloads is clunky compared with single-hub sites.
3. **Heavy coaching upsell** around the free CA; and **zero Bank/PSU coverage** — it is strictly a UPSC instrument.

## Notes for exam-prep use
- Best for: **UPSC final revision** — download the free Monthly Magazine and PT 365 / Mains 365; also the best Hindi-medium UPSC CA option alongside Drishti IAS.
- Pair with: IASbaba or ForumIAS for the *daily* feed; Insights IAS for Mains answer-writing depth.
- Not for: Bank/IBPS/SSC — keep AffairsCloud + Guidely + Adda247 there.
