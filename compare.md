### Strengths & weaknesses at a glance (original five)

**GKToday**
- ✅ Same-day updates, clean exam-mapped categories, huge free MCQ bank with banking sections.
- ❌ Self-admittedly weak for UPSC; monthly PDFs paywalled; fragmented pagination.

**Insights IAS**
- ✅ Dual Prelims+Mains orientation, hard data liftable into answers, completely free incl. monthly compilations.
- ❌ Too verbose for Bank-style revision; prelims facts buried in long articles; day-after lag + Sunday gap.

**Drishti IAS**
- ✅ Exam-oriented structure (Why in News? / For Prelims / For Mains), full Hindi+English, trusted sourcing, multi-format (text + video + PDFs).
- ❌ UPSC-centric, thin on Bank needs; no free daily quiz integration; no Sunday publishing.

**Testbook**
- ✅ Quiz-first bilingual pedagogy built for retention; fully free daily layer; CA integrated into exam test series.
- ❌ Too shallow for UPSC Mains; freshest content app-walled; upsell-heavy UX.

**AffairsCloud**
- ✅ Deep per-item write-ups with "About" boxes; dedicated Banking & Finance section; exam-recall content; completeness-over-speed policy.
- ❌ Best curation paywalled; 1-day lag; heavy ad-laden pages; no UPSC Mains editorial layer.

## Verdict by exam (updated, 17 sites)

- **🏦 Bank / IBPS / RBI / SBI** → **Guidely + AffairsCloud** (banking-finance depth) + **Adda247** (speed feed, one-liners) + **Oliveboard's free PDFs** (monthly consolidation).
- **🏦 RBI Grade B / NABARD / SEBI** → **Oliveboard** (RBI-circulars daily feed + free compilations) — the regulatory-exam pick nobody else matches.
- **🎓 UPSC CSE (daily)** → **IASbaba** (fastest same-day Prelims/Mains feed) + **ForumIAS** (newspaper replacement, GS-tagged Hindu/IE curation) + **StudyIQ** (daily mains answer-writing).
- **🎓 UPSC CSE (Mains depth)** → **Insights IAS** (answer-ready deep dives, free monthly magazine) + **Drishti IAS** (bilingual structure) — with **StudyIQ** as the same-day alternative.
- **🎓 UPSC CSE (final revision)** → **Vision IAS PT 365 / Mains 365** (free, bilingual) + **ClearIAS CCC** monthly MCQ PDFs (learn-by-question).
- **🎓 Hindi-medium UPSC** → **Drishti IAS** (daily bilingual) + **Vision IAS Hindi magazines** (monthly/365 consolidation).
- **🏭 PSU / SSC / Railways** → **GKToday** (same-day factual notes) + **Testbook** (quiz habit) + **Jagran Josh** (free monthly PDFs; Hindi-medium SSC pick).
- **⚡ 60-second daily revision** → Testbook capsules, Drishti's Rapid Fire, or Adda247's Weekly One Liners.
- **📖 Monthly consolidation** → Oliveboard (free PDFs, bank lane) • Vision IAS / Insights IAS (free PDFs, UPSC lane) • Jagran Josh (free PDFs, SSC/Hindi lane).
- **🎥 Video learners** → **Unacademy UCAN** (subject-wise monthly batches) or StudyIQ's YouTube analysis.
- **🔍 Fact-checking** → **PIB** — verify scheme dates, Bill provisions and index numbers against the primary source before trusting any prep site.
- **⚠️ Avoid as a live source** → **BYJU'S** — free CA frozen at ~April 2024; archives only.
